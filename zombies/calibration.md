# Hint calibration for this group

Read this before writing or giving hints. Update it after every session from the players' reactions. Keep entries dated and specific: what kind of hint it was, how they reacted, what to do differently. This file is tracked and the repository is shared with a player, so it holds no map content: the delivered lines these lessons came from, and every other quote from a map, go in that map's gitignored `zombies/maps/<game>/<map>/calibration.md`.

## Known so far

- 2026-11-14 — Past attempts (before this setup) failed in two opposite ways: hints that were **very obvious** (basically the instruction itself) and hints that were **very hard to interpret** (riddle-like, nothing concrete to act on). Target the middle: plain language, one concrete thing to go look at or try, with something still left to work out.

## Preferences

- Players' language: **Spanish** (confirmed 2026-11-14; conversation is in Spanish from now on). Prepare everything in English plus `hints.es.md`; answer in Spanish.
- Game language (for area/item names): **Spanish** (confirmed 2026-11-14). Use localised names where a source confirms them, otherwise the English name.
- Usual group size: 2 players.
- 2026-11-14 — Wants the session as deterministic as possible: all data fetched and the guide plus every hint written before playing; hints delivered as written.

- 2026-11-14 — Messages prefixed `REVISION:` are feedback on the coaching, not in-game messages. Log them and act on them; don't treat them as player reports.
- 2026-11-14 — When they report a step done they expect to be pointed onward in the same reply. A confirmation alone reads as "ok" and is useless to them.

- 2026-11-14 — **Tone.** An L1 that read like an instruction was judged too direct and cold. They want hints to feel thematic, like the lines the characters say in the game, which are often very vague hints. This pulls against the "plain language, no riddles" rule written from their earlier complaint (hints too obvious or too hard to interpret). Settled the same day over three rounds on one step: teammate-voice remarks at levels 1 and 2, vague on purpose at level 1 (they liked that one "especially"); level 2 says what the thing is, never where (a level 2 with locations was called "not a hint, literally the locations"); levels 1–2 must be short (a three-sentence level 2 was "too verbose", though its first part "felt right"); places only from level 3. Voice: one generic teammate, confirmed by them ("una sola voz está bien").

- 2026-11-15 — **Multi-part steps.** After finding one part of a multi-part step they were given the whole-step L2, half of which described the part they already had. REVISION: pointless to repeat the part they already found; finding one part with a hint means that hint wasn't enough for the other. Options they named: a new L1 only for the missing part, or L2 minus the found part; **they prefer a new L1 for the missing part.** Rule in `hint-craft.md` and the skill.

(still to learn: preferred starting level, how they feel about step counts, lockout warnings, boss-fight tips, etc.)

## Lessons

(general lessons from the hint log; the lines themselves are in each map's `calibration.md`)

- 2026-11-14 — **No advice that can't be acted on.** An `On success` line told them to keep an item they could not drop, and pointed nowhere. A completion report must be answered with where to go next.
- 2026-11-15 — **Once a part is found, hint only the missing part**, from a fresh L1.
- 2026-11-15 — **An Alt that names what they already have** can work well: it gives the missing part's nature and rules one thing out (REVISION: "la pista tiene buena pinta"; judged on reading, not yet in play).
- 2026-11-15 — **Follow the players' thread, not the guide's order.** A report about a side thread was confirmed in one line and then steered to the dossier's next step. REVISION: "los jugadores se deben de sentir libres a explorar ideas y posibles pasos… pero hay que tener cuidado con no permitir a los jugadores perderse." A blocked thread must never get "hasta aquí hemos llegado, necesitas hacer x para seguir", and threads must not be clearly defined for the players ("es interesante dejar que el jugador explore los distintos hilos y considere qué es un hilo y cómo se relaciona con el resto"). Agreed and applied the same day ("venga, vamos a probar"): open threads kept privately in `session.md`, follow the thread they raise, no thread menus, no linking threads outside a rung, blocked threads silent until asked and then a ladder at the blocker without naming it. Rules in the skill ("Players choose the order") and `hint-craft.md` ("Blocked threads").
- 2026-11-15 — **Optional steps.** An L1 for a required step was read as pointing at an optional one. Agreed the same day: the coach never suggests optional steps; if the players start one, follow it; once they are putting real time into it, say once, in voice, that it isn't essential. They approved a short in-voice heads-up of that kind for each optional step. Rule in the skill.
- 2026-11-15 — **Heads-ups must not out-tell the hint (and must be true).** A heads-up given with an L1 warned of a lockout that was false, and named far more than the vague L1 beside it. Lessons: an unprompted heads-up is only for a lockout that is verified, not one single source suspects; give it at the moment they are about to walk into it, not when the step starts; and it must never reveal more than the rung it goes with.
- 2026-11-15 — **A code step said by its kind** ("a hidden code") and a hint at the tool that reveals it, without naming it, was called "genial".
- 2026-11-15 — **Level 3 carries every condition of the action**, in order, even if an earlier rung already said part of it; a level 3 that dropped one read as contradicting what they had just tried. Their objections were their own misconception and the dossier was right: check the sources before conceding a correction.
- 2026-11-15 — **Retries.** When the step is right but didn't come off, only insinuate that it needs repeating, no details; let them try at least a couple of times and judge from their feedback. The detailed line only after 2+ more failures.
- 2026-11-15 — **Conditional steps and map mechanics.** Round thresholds, unreliable actions and enemies that spawn by themselves all change whether a step is blocked. Their words: "es importante que analices las mecánicas del mapa para no crear falsos blocks donde no los hay." Applied: a Map mechanics section and per-step Conditions in the dossier (and in `research.md` for future maps), near-required optional steps count as requirements, single-use resources get a heads-up only right before use.
- 2026-11-15 — **The round.** Giving it is their job, but ask "¿en qué ronda vais?" now and then, even on steps that don't need it, for context. Timing matters: asked right at a round-dependent step, the question is a hint.
- 2026-11-15 — **Arbitrary steps may carry the action at L2** ("sí, déjalo así"); other types keep it at L3.
- 2026-11-15 — **No action at level 2** outside arbitrary steps; one that gave it was rewritten and the action moved to L3. They suspect some L2s are too direct but aren't sure yet ("probablemente necesitamos seguir para definir si es demasiado o no"): keep watching L2s for actions and for giving too much.
- 2026-11-15 — **Imply, don't state (L1).** Use a saying or a world fact the players must apply themselves; don't name the conclusion. "Es un poco el tono que buscamos."
- 2026-11-15 — **No location-list Alts after level 3.** The list of exact spots belongs to level 4. Accepted: some overlap between L2 and L3.
- 2026-11-16 — **A step that folds "what the trick is" and "how to get it" into one ladder needs an Alt (or parts) for players who work out the first half alone.** A player voiced the first half as a theory while the next rung was the one stating it; the right line was a fresh L1-style nudge toward the second half only. Rule in `hint-craft.md` ("Steps with several parts").
