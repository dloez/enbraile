## Series Rules

- **A change to how a series is played lands in its rulebook in the same change.** Points, tie-breaks, brackets, eggs or any other rule settled anywhere (a site design, the league app, a conversation) is written into `docs/pkmn/series/<n>/reference/rules.md`, with its dated decision in that series' `explanation/competition-format.md`. The rulebook is the one source; the site and the league app follow it (owner, 2026-10-22).
