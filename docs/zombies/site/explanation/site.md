# The Zombies Site

The site in `zombies/site/`, for `zombies.enbraile.vip`, where the group starts attempts on a map and talks to the coach. It lists the maps Claude has published, and members start or join an attempt on one and talk in its shared chat, where the coach answers with the prepared hints, with a zone on the map under those that give a place ([Zones](#zones)), and follows their progress, holds when no prepared line fits, and takes the group's revisions and ratings for Claude, who can watch an attempt from Claude Code while it is played, close its gaps, read code's audit of every reply and step in as the coach; a member in a Discord voice channel can bring the coach into the call and ask it by voice ([Voice](#voice)); what is still to come is in [Zombies Coach](../../../work-in-progress/zombies-coach/discovery.md). Its look is in the [Site Design System](../reference/design-system.md).

Players see an **attempt**: one go at a map's easter egg, in English and Spanish "attempt" and "intento", at `/attempts/<id>`. The code, the tables, the API routes and the terminal's commands call it a **run**, and so does this doc wherever it describes them ([Decisions](#decisions)).

## How It Is Built

It mirrors the [DJ panel](../../../dj/panel/explanation/panel.md): a Vite + React page, and one Hono function holding every route in `api/_lib/app.ts`, bundled into one file at build ([Hosting](#hosting)). A signed-out reader is sent to the [root site](../../../home/site/explanation/site.md) and back: the function reads the session from the root's `/api/me` with the reader's cookie, so the site keeps no sign-in tables of its own. It asks once per request, and the reply that opens a page, `/api/maps`, `/api/maps/<game>/<map>` or `/api/runs/<id>`, carries the reader as `viewer`, so a page load asks the root once and the site has no `/api/me` of its own. Locally, `DEV_USER_ID` stands in for a session: it holds a comma-separated list of members, each `<id>` or `<id>:<name>`, and `GET /api/dev/sign-in/<id>` sets a cookie that makes that browser one of them (the first one otherwise), so one machine can be two members of one attempt. The route answers 404 wherever `DEV_USER_ID` is unset, as it is on every Vercel deployment. The chrome is in English or Spanish, following the reader's `lang` cookie as the other sites do.

Maps live in their own Postgres database, never pkmn's, through Drizzle with `pg` as in [the pkmn site](../../../pkmn/site/explanation/site.md); the migrations in `drizzle/` run in the Vercel build. Its tables:

| Table | Holds |
|-------|-------|
| `maps` | One row per published map, keyed by game and map slug, with its latest version |
| `bundle_versions` | Every published bundle, numbered per map, with the SHA-256 of its canonical JSON and who published it, always `claude` |
| `map_images` | Each map image published, announcement or survey, keyed by the SHA-256 of its bytes, with its type and the bytes |
| `runs` | One row per run: when and by whom it was closed, if it is (`closed_at`, `closed_by`, `closed_by_name`), its map, the language the coach speaks, the voice it speaks with (`voice`, which also picks its character), who started it, when the coach last asked the round, and the lease that lets one of the coach's replies run at a time |
| `coach_queue` | Each turn of the coach's waiting for the run or being answered: who asked, whether by voice, the line (none for More) and since when; a row goes when its turn ends |
| `run_history` | A run's earlier history, imported from the terminal session it continues: each line the players typed there or the coach answered, in order, with when it was said |
| `call_lines` | Each line said in the run's Discord call while the coach was in it, from the bot's transcripts: the speaker's Discord ID and server name, the text, when speech started and the clip's length; never read by the coach model |
| `run_members` | Each time a person joined a run, with when they left it; a unique index over the rows not left keeps a person in one run at a time |
| `messages` | The run's chat, each line from a member or from the coach, `spoken` on a member's line that came through voice, its `kind`: `chat`, a member's `revision`, or the coach's `ack` of one, and `posted_by`, `claude` on a line Claude posted as the coach |
| `run_knowledge` | The knowledge entries known in a run; an entry without a row is unknown |
| `hint_cards` | A hint card: the ladder it belongs to (a step, or one of its parts) and the coach's line it hangs under |
| `deliveries` | Every rung given, in order: its card, ladder, rung, Alt, level, the wording as delivered, its `mentions` and `reveals`, the bundle version and who asked |
| `run_steps` | A run's progress: each step or part the players started or finished, who reported a step done (`done_by`, `done_by_name`, set when a member's line led to it and left empty for a step marked done because a later one requires it, for an import and for a step-in), shown to players only as the roadmap ([Roadmap](#roadmap)), and `blocked_told_at`, from when the coach said a blocked step was not the moment, which only an import's `blockedTold` still sets |
| `heads_ups_given` | Each heads-up the coach gave in a run, so none is given twice |
| `gaps` | Each time the coach held: the line asked, what the model said it was about, the holding line said, the bundle version, and the run's marks, open ladders, done steps and last lines at that moment; then the last version the coach was offered it again on, and when a later version's rung answered it and which delivery that was |
| `feedback` | Each revision (its text, its message and the coach's line it refers to, with that line's card and newest rung) and each member's rating of a card (`obvious`, `right` or `vague`, one per member per card, the newest rung it was given on) |
| `coach_calls` | Every Claude call: model, tokens, latency, stop reason, refusal and its category, the rules code caught the model breaking, and any error |
| `audits` | Each reply of the coach's in which code found a rule broken: the coach's line it became (none when nothing was posted) and the findings |

## Map Bundles

A bundle is everything the live coach will need about one map, as one JSON document checked by the zod schema in `shared/bundle.ts`: the map's name in each language, which drawn plan stands for it, its announcement image if it has one (the file name, the SHA-256 of its bytes, its type and its size in pixels), its survey image if it has one (a community overhead map of the whole level, the same facts), the zones on its rungs, the knowledge entries with their `known` or `unknown` marks and any aliases (other ways players say an entry, per language, matched wherever its name is), the steps (`requires`, conditions, `optional`, the `onSuccess` line, the `milestone` that labels the step on the roadmap, the ladder, part ladders and heads-ups), the map's own calibration notes, and the dossier and sources as the durable copy. Every rung, each of its Alts (with `when` it replaces the rung) and every `onSuccess` line carries its wording in Spanish and English, the knowledge entries it `mentions`, which must already be known, and those it `reveals`; a heads-up carries its `mentions` only. The schema rejects a `mentions`, `reveals` or `requires` that names nothing, repeated IDs, and a bundle with no step that is not `optional`. A zone is an ellipse on the survey image, `{ x, y, rx, ry }`, its centre and radii as fractions of the image's width and height, or a list of up to eight for a rung that names several places; a rung or Alt carries one only when it gives a place, and the schema rejects a zone on a level 1 or 2 rung or on an Alt for one ([Zones](#zones)).

A real map's bundle is not written by hand: `npm run compile -- <dir>` builds it from the files the ee-coach skill prepares in that map's dir ([Research and dossier building → Compile and publish](../../../../.claude/skills/ee-coach/references/research.md#4-compile-and-publish)) and writes `<dir>/bundle.json`. It reads `map.json` (the names, the art plan, and the file names of the announcement image and the survey image), each image it names, rejecting one that is missing, over 4 MB or not a JPEG, PNG or WebP file, `hints.md` and `hints.es.md` paired line by line, the `L4 as spoken` and `Conditions` of each step in `dossier.md` (each indented `- ` item one condition, an indented line without one joined to the item above), `knowledge.md` (its optional `Aliases (English)` and `Aliases (Spanish)` columns holding each entry's aliases, separated by `;`), the map's `calibration.md` every file under `sources/` and `zones.json`, which maps a rung's address as compile's messages name it (`step 5 L3`, `step 5 part 1 L3`, `step 5 Alt 2`) to its zone, or to `null` for a rung that gives no place, or to `"unplaced"` for one that names a place no zone could be found for, and warns with the addresses of the unplaced ones and of every level 3 or 4 rung and Alt it holds nothing for, when the map has a survey image, and checks what the schema cannot: that each zone names a rung that exists, of level 3 or 4, and that a map with zones names a survey image, that both languages hold the same lines with the same IDs, that a line reveals only what it mentions, and that no card text carries a step, hint or level number. Its messages name a file and line or a step and rung, never the text, and its summary is counts only, so its output can be quoted where the players read.

`npm run publish -- <dir>` reads `<dir>/bundle.json` and checks it. When the bundle has an announcement or survey image, it checks that each file in the dir still has the bundle's digest and uploads it first, to `PUT /api/claude/images/<sha-256>`, which stores it unless one with that digest is stored already; the bundle route answers `missingImage` to a bundle whose announcement or survey image is not stored. It then sends the bundle to `PUT /api/claude/maps/<game>/<map>` with `CLAUDE_TOKEN`. The function stores it as a new version, published by `claude`, unless its digest equals the latest one's, so publishing the same bundle twice leaves one version; an older bundle published again becomes the newest version, which is how a map is reverted. Real maps' bundles stay in the gitignored `zombies/maps/<game>/<map>/` ([Repository Layout](../../../general/explanation/repository-layout.md#decisions)); the one committed bundle is the made-up test map in `zombies/site/test-map/`, Harrow Station.

Publishing only adds; `npm run unpublish -- <game>/<map>` is the one way a map goes. It sends `DELETE /api/claude/maps/<game>/<map>` with `CLAUDE_TOKEN`, and the function deletes, in one transaction under the same per-map lock as publishing, every run on the map with everything that hangs off it (its members, chat, earlier history, call lines, marks, cards, deliveries, progress, heads-ups, gaps, audits, feedback and Claude calls), then every bundle version and the map's row, then each announcement or survey image those versions named that no other map's version names. It answers the counts of versions, runs and images deleted, which `unpublish` prints, or `noSuchMap` (404) for a map that is not published. Nothing is kept to undo it but Neon's point-in-time restore; a map unpublished by mistake comes back by publishing its bundle again, as version 1 and without its runs. The test map is kept out of production this way: it is published only to a local site.

`npm run delete-run -- <id>` removes one attempt and leaves its map published. It sends `DELETE /api/claude/runs/<id>` with `CLAUDE_TOKEN`, and the function deletes, in one transaction under the run's coach lock (the one a delivery takes, so a coach turn in progress cannot add a card halfway through), everything that hangs off the run, the same tables as `unpublish`, then the run's row. It answers the run's map and its count of chat lines, which the script prints, or `noSuchRun` (404); any other failure prints its status. Nothing undoes it but Neon's point-in-time restore, so save the run's log with `npm run runs -- <id>` first; the machine API never returns the call lines.

`/api/claude/` is the site's machine API, and it is Claude's: only Claude Code calls it, from the terminal, and every route under it answers 403 to any request without `CLAUDE_TOKEN` as its bearer token, whatever cookie it carries. What it writes is recorded as Claude's, as `published_by` is on a bundle version; the watch stream and the step-in below are under it too, and a line Claude posts as the coach carries `posted_by` `claude`.

`GET /api/claude/runs` lists every run with its map, language, starter, members, when it started and last spoke, and its counts of history lines, lines, call lines, rungs, gaps and feedback. `GET /api/claude/runs/<id>` returns the whole run for a debrief: the run, every membership, its imported history, every line with its `kind`, the cards and every rung delivered, the feedback, the gaps, the marks known (with their names and when) and those still unknown, the progress in `run_steps`, the heads-ups given and every Claude call. `POST /api/claude/runs` imports a run coached from the terminal: the map, the language, the members with their Discord IDs (the first is the starter), the knowledge entries known, every rung given in order (its step or part, rung and Alt, and who asked), each step or part started, done or told blocked, and the heads-ups said. It checks every handle and rung against the latest bundle, that no Discord ID is listed twice and that each ladder's rungs come in order, and answers with the issues, by ID and position only, if any fails; otherwise it creates the run with each member moved into it, one card per ladder under a fixed line of the coach's ("De la sesión anterior." or "From the last session.", an imported run taking the default voice and so Coach) holding that ladder's rungs in the bundle's wording for the run's language, the marks (the bundle's baseline, the entries listed, and what every rung given mentions and reveals), the progress, with every step a done step `requires` done too as `record_progress` does, and the heads-ups, all at the moment of the import. `POST /api/claude/runs/<id>/history` adds to an existing run the conversation the terminal session had, as its earlier history: each line `member` or `coach`, its text and when it was said, in order and every one earlier than the run, which it checks and answers with the issues by position only; it refuses a run that already has a history, so it is sent once. The run page shows that history above the run's own lines, the coach's replies as the character's and the players' under the name "Terminal". `npm run import -- <dir> <name>=<discord id> ...` sends the dir's gitignored `import.json`, with each member's Discord ID, which a subagent writes from the map's `session.md` and `knowledge.md`, and prints the run's ID and counts; `npm run import -- <dir> --history <run id>` sends the dir's gitignored `history.json`, which a subagent writes from Claude Code's transcripts of the terminal session, and prints the counts of lines. `npm run runs` prints the list, with no hint text in it; `npm run runs -- <id>` writes the whole run, its audits included, to the gitignored `zombies/maps/<game>/<map>/runs/<id>.json` and prints only its counts, since the log holds rungs and the gaps name what the players asked.

`GET /api/claude/runs/<id>/events?after=<cursor>` is the watch stream: the run's map and every event since the cursor, oldest first, each with its kind, its row's ID, its time and its details, and the cursor to ask from next. The kinds are a member's line (`line`) and the coach's (`coach`, with the cards under it), each rung given (`hint`: card, ladder, rung, Alt, level, wording, version), a revision, a card rating (sent again when a member changes it), a gap held and a gap answered, a member joining or leaving, a step or part started, done or told blocked (`progress`), a reply's audit findings (`audit`, with the coach's line it became), and a new bundle version of the run's map (`published`), the run closed (`closed`, with who closed it); a line of the coach's that Claude posted says `by` `claude`. The cursor is the last event's time in microseconds, and the route leaves out the last second, so a row whose transaction began a moment before it committed is not skipped. `npm run watch -- <id>` follows it every 2 seconds for `Monitor`: it writes every event, the earlier ones first, to the gitignored `zombies/maps/<game>/<map>/runs/<id>.events.jsonl` and prints one line per new event with its time, kind, member name, card or gap ID, version or rating, and never any text from the chat or the bundle, such as `21:19:13 gap 1 held`, `21:19:42 coach with card 2`, `21:20:05 coach (claude) with card 3` or `21:20:30 audit on coach line 12: number-shown`, so the terminal the players may read shows no hint and no question.

`POST /api/claude/runs/<id>/say` is Claude's step-in: a line posted in the run as the coach, for when the model is lost. It takes `text`, an optional `hint` (a ladder by its bundle `step` and `part`, and an `alt` index), an optional `gap` that the hint answers, and `progress`, steps or parts to record `started` or `done` as `record_progress` does (a step done with what it requires), and needs one of text, a hint or progress. Progress is recorded before the hint is checked, so a step-in can close what the players finished and hand over the next step's rung in one line, and nothing is written when the step-in is refused; one with progress alone posts no line. It waits for the run's lease, as a reply does, and holds to the same rules as the model: the hint is the ladder's next rung, the full solution included when it is next, given only when its step is ready and not done, never on an optional step the players have not started, and only when its `mentions` are known; the text may name nothing unknown except what that rung mentions or reveals, and no step or hint number, nor brackets. A refusal answers with its code alone (`namesUnknown`, `numberShown`, `notReady`, `optional`, `unknownMention`, `noSuchGap`, `gapWithoutHint`, `busy` and the like); otherwise the rung goes on a new card, the marks learn what it mentions and reveals, and the line, stored with `posted_by` `claude`, reaches every member's next poll and the model's later turns like any of the coach's, and is spoken in the call when the coach is in it ([Voice](#voice)). `npm run say -- <id> <file>` sends a step-in written to a JSON file, so its text never appears on a command line, and prints only the line's and card's IDs, how many progress entries it recorded, or the refusal.

The maps page reads `GET /api/maps`, which returns each map's game and map IDs, its names, its `art` plan key, its image's digest and size in pixels (or none), test flag and version and nothing else from the bundle, so no solution reaches a browser. The same reply carries, for each map, how many attempts it has, how many are beaten and whether the reader is in one, and the reader's own attempt. A map's view, `/maps/<game>/<map>`, reads `GET /api/maps/<game>/<map>`: the same map fields, every attempt on it and the reader's own attempt. Each attempt carries its language, its starter, everyone who ever played it (those in it first), whether someone is in it, whether it is the reader's, whether it is beaten and when it was last played, the newest of its start and its last line. Every screen outside an attempt puts the reader's own in the header as a "Your attempt" pill that leads back to it.

A map with an image shows it on its row, in its view's banner and at the head of its join view in place of the drawn plan; a map without one, such as the test map, keeps its plan. The page loads it from `GET /api/images/<sha-256>`, the one route a page uses that needs no sign-in: it answers the stored bytes with `public, max-age=31536000, s-maxage=31536000, immutable`, so Vercel's CDN and the browser keep it and a later page load never reaches the function for it. Until it has loaded, its frame is a skeleton the size it will take; if it fails to load, the plan shows instead.

An attempt is **beaten** once every step of the latest bundle that is not `optional` is recorded done in `run_steps` (its row for the whole step, not a part's). Both replies work it out in the query from the bundle's `steps`, so a later bundle with a new required step makes an earlier attempt not beaten until that step is done too. Beaten is only a mark: the attempt stays open and can be joined and played on like any other, and the replies say nothing about which steps are done or how many are left.

## Attempts

Each map's row on the maps page opens the map's view, which lists every attempt on the map and holds Start an attempt with the voice the coach speaks with (Coach unless they pick another, [Characters](#characters)) and the language it speaks (Spanish unless they pick English) inline: `POST /api/runs`. Any member can change the voice from the attempt's rail (on a phone, the strip) with `PATCH /api/runs/<id>` and `{ voice }`; it holds from the next reply, and every member's next poll, which carries the attempt's `voice`, shows the new name. The list puts the reader's own attempt first, then the others someone is in, most recently played first, then the rest, newest first; the beaten ones carry their mark wherever they fall. Anyone signed in can join any attempt with one tap on its row there, or from its link, `/attempts/<id>`, which shows the join view to a reader not in it and the attempt to a member (`POST /api/runs/<id>/join`); an attempt's "Copy link" gives that link. A person is in one attempt at a time, so starting or joining one leaves the one they were in, which a line under the actions says, without a step to confirm it. An attempt never ends by itself, not even when it is beaten: one everyone has left stays on its map's list and can be joined again. It ends only when a member closes it: Close, red-outlined beside Copy link under the members (a red closing icon in the strip on a phone), asks to confirm in a dialog saying everyone leaves it and the coach stops answering, then `POST /api/runs/<id>/close` (active members only), under the run's coach lock, stamps `closed_at`, `closed_by` and `closed_by_name` on the run and marks every membership left, then, in the background, asks the bot to leave the call with those members' IDs, which it does if one of them brought the coach in ([The Zombies Bot Module](../../bot/explanation/bot.md)). Closing is refused while a reply of the coach's is under way, so no turn posts into a closed attempt; the page says it did not close and offers to try again. A closed attempt answers nothing: every member route refuses its former members, as none is in it any more; joining it is refused; the coach turn finds no run, so a held question is not answered when a version lands and Claude's step-in is refused with `closed`. Anyone signed in can still read it: `/attempts/<id>` shows the log, the cards without More or ratings, and the roadmap, with "Closed by <name> at <time>." where the composer was, the poll and the roadmap answering for a closed run whoever asks, and the voice offer off. Any former member reopens it from there with Reopen, `POST /api/runs/<id>/reopen`, which clears the stamps and moves them into it as joining does (anyone else gets 403 `notMember`). A page open on an attempt someone closes or reopens loads it again on its next poll, which carries `closed`. The map view marks a closed attempt "Closed" and opens it with Read rather than joining it; the watch stream has a `closed` event with who closed it, and none for a reopen, which leaves no trace but the run's lines going on.

A new attempt's knowledge marks are the latest bundle's `known` entries plus every entry known in the starter's earlier runs on that map (an imported run takes the terminal's marks instead, as above); each member who joins adds what was known in their own earlier runs there.

The run page polls `GET /api/runs/<id>/messages?after=<last id>&rungsAfter=<last rung id>` every 2 seconds for new lines (each with `spoken`, which the log marks with a mic beside the speaker's name), the run's coach queue, the current members and every hint card that gained a rung or arrived with a new line, each card carrying its rungs' wording, each rung's zone, whether it shows More, its thread (the ID of the first card on its ladder) and whether its step or part is done. `POST /api/runs/<id>/messages` adds a member's line and answers with the coach's reply as it is spoken. The post answers 403 to anyone not in the run, and so does the poll, but for a closed attempt, which anyone signed in may read ([Attempts](#attempts)). When no poll has succeeded for 8 seconds, whether they fail or hang, the page says it cannot reach the site and polls every 5 seconds until one succeeds, so a phone on a patchy network does not flicker the notice on one lost poll.

Under the log the page shows the coach queue from the poll: "<coach> is answering <name>" with the line asked and a caret for the turn under way, then "<name> is waiting" for each turn waiting for it, oldest first, each with a mic when it came by voice; rows older than 2 minutes are left out, so a turn whose function died never stays on it. The reader's own typed line is left out, since its transmitting row shows it. The voice strip says "<coach> is answering <name>" and moves its meter only while that turn came by voice ([Voice](#voice)).

Members appear under their current Discord server names and avatars. Each row stores the name a person had when it was written (when they joined, posted or started the attempt, or the name `npm run import` was given), and the map view, the attempt's sheet and every poll of its chat swap in the name the bot reports for that Discord ID: the starter, the members and each member's lines. `api/_lib/names.ts` asks the bot's `POST /zombies/names` for the IDs it has not looked up in the last minute and is not already asking about, up to 50 per request, and keeps each answer, a name or none and an avatar URL or none, for 60 seconds in the function's memory; an avatar not on `https://cdn.discordapp.com/` is dropped, and avatars are never stored in the database, so a person whose lookup failed shows their initials chip. Only the reply that starts a request waits on it, at most 1.5 seconds; a reply arriving while another's request is still out serves what the instance already holds. The request itself has 10 seconds for Discord's forced member fetches, and one answering after its reply has gone usually still lands in that instance's memory for its next reply, since polls keep the instance warm, though nothing holds the function open for it after the reply. Each request logs `bot names` with its count of IDs, how long it took and whether the bot answered, which is how the 10 seconds gets measured. Each name that differs from what the instance last heard is written back on that person's member rows, member lines and the attempts they started, so the stored name is the last Discord name rather than the one the row was written with. A failed request, an answer later than 1.5 seconds, or `BOT_URL` unset leaves the stored names in place; a failed request also stops that function instance's lookups for 30 seconds, so a page waits on a missing or hanging bot only on the reply that starts each request, never longer than 1.5 seconds, once per 30 seconds per instance for a bot that refuses and, for one that hangs until the 10 seconds run out, once per 40 for each set of people a reply asks about, and a name older than its minute is not shown meanwhile. Every person in a reply carries their Discord ID as `who`, `live`, whether the name came from the bot in this reply, and `avatar`, the bot's avatar URL when live and none otherwise; the page (`src/store.ts`) keeps the last live name and avatar per Discord ID while it is open and shows them over a reply's stored name, so a reply served by an instance whose lookup failed never turns a Discord name back into a stored one. The coach's lines, the imported history's "Terminal" and what the machine API returns (the run's log and the watch stream) carry stored names, which are the last Discord names written back.

## Roadmap

The attempt shows a roadmap of the steps recorded done ([Decisions](#decisions), "A roadmap of done steps only"). Each poll carries `roadmap`, a short mark of the map's latest version, the count and newest time of the whole steps done and the count of knowledge marks, and the page asks `GET /api/runs/<id>/roadmap` (members only) whenever it changes. That reply holds `beaten` and one node per whole step done, oldest first: an opaque key (an HMAC of the run and the step's ID keyed with `CLAUDE_TOKEN`, so no handle or bundle order reaches the page or can be worked back from it), its milestone in the run's language or none while the milestone mentions an entry still unknown in the run, whether it is optional, when it was done and who reported it (a person as elsewhere, or none), or `earlier` instead of both for a step marked done because a later step that requires it was recorded, which shares that step's exact time and is shown as "done earlier" until it is dated, and `after`, the keys of the steps it requires, which are always done too. Nothing about a step not done reaches it, nor any count of steps, until the attempt is beaten: then every step comes, the ones not done with `done` false, in the bundle's order after the done ones.

The page draws it as a history graph, built in code without a board ([Site Design System](../reference/design-system.md)): one row per step in the order done, each step a stamp on a lane joined to what it required by a line in the lane's ink, optional steps as diamonds on a dotted side trail that branches off at once, a withheld milestone as a hatched bar, and a step that arrived since the page last drew it popping in with a ring. On a screen wider than 1180px it is a column of its own at the right of the chat, 360px wide (420px from 1600px), with "What you've done" over the whole graph, which keeps the newest step in view; narrower, the rail shows the last four with "See the whole roadmap", which opens the whole graph in a sheet, and on a phone the sheet is full screen, opened from a route button in the strip; once beaten, the sheet's title says so and the steps not done show in grey, dashed. A step the coach failed to record, or recorded wrongly, is what the roadmap is there to show, and the group corrects it with a revision; the roadmap itself carries no note saying so, which the owner had removed (2026-11-17).

A step marked done only because a later step that requires it was recorded is dated from the chat: once the record is written, a Haiku call in the background (`api/_lib/dating.ts`, after the reply, through `waitUntil`) gets the `Done when` of each such step and the run's last 300 member chat lines before it, numbered, and names the line in which the players first reported each one; that step's `done_at`, `done_by` and `done_by_name` become that line's time and author, so the roadmap shows when and by whom it was really done. Only chat lines are read, the spoken ones included, never the call's transcripts; a line after the step's recorded time, or one the call names wrongly, is ignored, and a step no line reports stays "done earlier". `POST /api/claude/runs/<id>/date-steps` runs the same for a run's steps done earlier now and answers the counts, which `npm run date-steps -- <id>` prints, for runs recorded before it existed.

## Zones

A rung that gives a place, at level 3 and sometimes level 4, can carry a zone, and its card shows it under the rung's text as the `run-zone` boards draw it: the survey image inset in the slip with the zone over it as a hatched red ellipse, its red edge on a pale halo so it reads on a dark image, and one ring pulsing out from it ([Site Design System](../reference/design-system.md)). A zone marks the exact spot, not the room, and the card shows the survey image cropped and zoomed around it (around all its spots when it has several, each drawn alike, wide enough that they fill at most three quarters of it): a 4:3 window centred on the zone, at least 40% of the image's width (or as wide as its height allows, for an image wider than 4:3) and wide enough that the zone spans at most a third of it, shifted to stay inside the image, with each of the zone's radii drawn at least 2.5% of the window's width so a tiny one still reads as a spot. A tap on the sheet shows the whole map with the same mark, the same size on the image, and another goes back to the crop. Levels 1 and 2 never show one. The card's rungs take their zones from the map's latest bundle when the card is read, by the rung and Alt delivered, so a zone fixed and published mid-run shows on the cards already given; the survey image's digest and size come with each zone, never in the map's own fields, so the overhead map reaches a browser only with the first zone. A bundle with zones and no survey image, which only the hand-written test map has, draws the zone on the map's drawn plan instead, on a ruled sheet. The ee-coach skill finds the survey image and places and blind-checks the zones at prepare ([Research and dossier building → Zones](../../../../.claude/skills/ee-coach/references/research.md#3b-zones-one-agent-with-dossier-access-then-a-blind-check)).

## Revisions and Ratings

A line is a revision when its text starts with `REVISION:` (or `Revisión:`, in any case; a spoken line needs only the word «revisión» first, since speech recognition rarely writes the colon) or when it is sent with the composer's Revision toggle pressed, as the `run-revision` boards draw it; the mark is dropped from the stored text. A revision never reaches the model: it is stored with `kind` `revision`, a `feedback` row records it with the last line of the coach's before it that was not an acknowledgement (and that line's card and newest rung, when it has one), and the coach answers at once with a fixed line in the run's language and its character ("Anotado, gracias." or "Noted, thanks." as Coach), stored as an `ack`, in the same streamed reply shape as any other but with no Claude call. The chat shows a revision with its tag and "re: <the coach's name> <time> · for Claude". Every line whose `kind` is not `chat` is left out of the conversation the model reads.

Every card's foot holds the three ratings (too obvious, right, too vague) in the coach's language, on the pinned slip for its newest card while its thread is the one pinned. A tap is `PUT /api/runs/<id>/cards/<card>/feedback`, which keeps one rating per member per card, the latest, with the card's newest rung at that moment; each reader's poll returns their own rating on each card. Ratings never reach the model either. Claude reads both from the run read above and folds them into calibration and the hints in the ee-coach debrief.

## The Coach

The coach is Claude, `COACH_MODEL` (Haiku 5.5, `claude-haiku-5-5`, unless set) with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, called by the function on every member's line that code does not answer itself (a revision, a hint request); without a key it stays silent. Each call runs with reasoning off (`thinking` disabled) and `effort` low, streams its text, and caches its prompt: a cache breakpoint on the system prompt, which is the same for every run of a character, plus the request's top-level automatic caching, which caches the conversation so far ([Decisions](#decisions)). The model never writes a hint: it acts through seven tools, and code decides what each may do. A tool's result goes back to the model for another call only when it asks for more; when every tool the model called in a call ends the reply (a hint handed over, `record_progress` done, a heads-up said, `hold`, `ask_round`, `repeat_hint`), the reply ends there without a second call.

- **`give_hint`** hands over the next rung of one ladder, or one of that rung's Alts. Code gives only a rung whose `mentions` are all known in the run (its `reveals` may still be unknown, since it is the line meant to give them away), one per reply, the full solution (level 4) like any other rung when it is next, and records the delivery and adds the rung's `mentions` and `reveals` to `run_knowledge` in the same transaction.
- **`repeat_hint`** says again, word for word, the last rung given on one ladder, once per reply, when the players ask to hear it again or did not catch it. It never moves on by itself, however often it is asked: a line asking for the next hint is handled before the model (below).
- **`mark_known`** takes the players' words; code marks known every unknown entry whose name or alias, in either language and without its article, appears both in them and in a member's line among the last 24, so the model cannot mark something only a rung named.
- **`record_progress`** records a step or part as started or done, by its handle. A step marked done marks every step it `requires` done too, since they had to come first. On a step done, code marks known what its `onSuccess` line mentions and the players named in the chat and says that line when all it mentions is known; when it cannot, the model gets one more call to confirm it in a few words of its own, the `judging` filter off for that turn. Once every tool call of the model's call has run, code hands over on a card the first rung of a ready step, the one the model names with `next` or else the first in the bundle's order, so a reply that records several steps never hints one it has just recorded, and none when the model gave a hint itself; the model passes `quiet` only when the players said what they are doing next. A part done just closes that part's ladder.
- **`heads_up`** says one of an open step's heads-ups, word for word, once per run.
- **`hold`** is for a question no listed hint covers. Code says one of three prepared holding lines in the run's language, in turn by how many gaps the run has, records a gap with the model's few words on what was asked and the run's state, and ends the reply; it is refused after a hint in the same reply. Claude writes and checks the missing line from the gap, and publishes it ([ee-coach → Watch a run](../../../../.claude/skills/ee-coach/SKILL.md#watch-a-run-from-claude-code)). From then the gap is listed to the model under "Held earlier", with the line that was asked, on the run's next reply; `give_hint` takes a `gap` number, and a rung given with one records that gap answered and by which delivery. A run still waiting when the version lands, whose newest gap is open and has no member line after it, gets the reply at once: the publish route runs a turn for it in which the model may only give a hint, told that new hints were written for that question, and a hint arrives under a new line of the coach's on everyone's next poll, and is spoken in the call when the coach is in it ([Voice](#voice)), while anything else is dropped. Either way a gap is offered once per new version, so a question no new line answers is not raised on every reply, and `publish` prints how many waiting runs were answered at once.
- **`ask_round`** adds the run's fixed question about the round to the reply, at most once every 10 minutes and never in a reply that gives a hint.

A member's line that asks for a hint never reaches the model. Code reads the line itself, without accents and in lower case, for a hint request (`dame`, `danos`, `dime`, `quiero`, `queremos`, `necesito`, `otra`, `siguiente`, `más`, `give`, `another`, `next`, `more`, `need`, `want` and the like before `pista`, `hint`, `ayuda` or `clue`; one of those followed by `más fácil`, `siguiente`, `otra`, `easier`, `next`; or `ayúdanos`, `help us`, `help me`), and not one asking to repeat or explain (`repite`, `otra vez`, `explica`, `again`, `repeat`, `explain`) or reporting progress (`ya`, `hemos`, `tenemos`, `encontrado`, `conseguido`, `hecho`, `le hemos dado`, `found`, `done`, `got` and the like), which goes to the model so it can record the step done. On such a line it hands over, by `give_hint`'s rules, the next rung of the most recent hint's ladder, or, when there is none or that ladder is done, the first rung of the first ready ladder, on a card and spoken in the call when the coach is in it, and the reply ends with no Claude call. When that rung is not allowed (its `mentions` unknown), the most recent ladder has nothing more but is not recorded done (usually a step they finished without the coach recording it), or there is nothing to give, the line goes to the model as any other.

Every member's line that reaches the model also gets a **progress check**: a second Haiku call, started alongside the reply, with a prompt of its own and one forced tool, `report`, that sees only the last 8 lines of the chat and the `Done when` and kind of each step not done whose `Done when` names nothing unknown and which is at most two unfinished steps away (its `requires` followed down leave at most two steps not done; an optional step only once started), and answers which of them the newest line reports finished. While it runs, the reply's words are held rather than sent. When it names none, the held words go out and the reply goes on as it was, so the check costs only the time it takes past the reply's own first words; when it names some, the reply's first call is dropped unused, code records each step done with what it requires, says its `onSuccess` line when it can, hands over the first rung of the first ready step on a card, and calls the model again with a note on what it recorded, asking it to confirm in its own words when no prepared line was said (the `judging` filter off) and otherwise to add at most a few words. The limit of two steps keeps one misread line from closing a whole quest: a replay without it closed 30 steps on one line. A line handled by the hint request above gets no check.

The prompt is a short system prompt, then the run's state. The system prompt gives the character's name, role and manner, and that the players want to solve it themselves with its prepared hints when they ask; one or two short sentences in the run's language, spoken lines read by what they most likely said, and nothing written that is not to be heard (reasoning, the lists, brackets, step or hint numbers). On hints: when they ask for help or a hint or say they are stuck, `give_hint` at once on the most recent hint's thread unless they clearly ask about something else, never asking first and never refusing, the full solution being a hint like any other; an Alt when what they report matches its `when`; a theory or guess about an open thread put to it counts as asking, so it gives that thread's next rung or the fitting Alt at once and never confirms, denies or comments on the theory in its own words; `repeat_hint` to say a hint again, and a hint already given explained plainly in its own words when they ask what it means, adding nothing it does not say; nothing about the solution in its own words, nothing named beyond what they know or a hint gave, and nothing from its own knowledge of the map; `hold` for a question about the map's easter egg that no hint covers, and a held question's `gap` when a hint now answers it. On progress: code checks every line for finished steps itself (the progress check above), so the model never says in its own words that they did it or are on track, and calls `record_progress` done only for a part of a step they report found, and started for something they start; `mark_known` with their whole line when they name something, a theory included, a heads-up only at its moment, `ask_round` now and then but never right after a hint, and never an optional step offered. Anything else (hearing checks, greetings, jokes, general Zombies questions such as how a perk, a power-up or a mechanic works across the series) it answers briefly in its own words, never reusing a sentence of an earlier reply, with silence only when they are talking among themselves. The run's state follows, opening with the run's language, the most recent hint handed over (its ladder's handle and its text) and what the players know, then sorted the way the skill keeps its open threads:

- **Open threads:** each ladder the players started or were given a rung of, with the rungs given and the next rung and each Alt code would allow, the Alt written as the call that gives it (`If <when>: give_hint <handle> with alt <n>, "<text>"`).
- **Ready:** the first rung of each ladder not opened yet whose step's `requires` are all done and whose `mentions` are known, never an optional step's.
- **Optional, not started:** listed only so the model can record one the players start themselves; code refuses any rung of an optional step until then.
- **Blocked:** steps whose `requires` are not all done, each listed once the players have opened it or, for a required step, once its title names nothing unknown.
- **Done**, and the heads-ups and conditions of the open threads.

Steps and parts are named by their handles only (`s2`, `s2p1`), never by their titles, so the dossier and the titles stay out of it; a title still decides whether a done, blocked or optional ladder is listed at all, which it is once the title names no unknown entry. An Alt, heads-up or condition whose text names an unknown entry is left out, and an unknown entry's name reaches the model only inside a rung meant to reveal it, which is one of the rungs it may give. The last 24 lines of the chat are the conversation, each member's line as `Name: text`, or `Name (spoken): text` for a line that came through voice (`messages.spoken`); the coach's lines are its words alone, the rungs on their cards being in the state above; a run's imported history is never part of it.

A blocked step gets no hint of its own. When the model asks for one, code hands over at once the next rung of the step that is missing, followed down its own `requires` to one that is ready, so the ladder aimed at the blocker is made of that step's checked rungs and names nothing they lack before those rungs do.

The coach never ends a turn silent when it is addressed. The prompt allows silence only for players talking among themselves, and code backs it: a turn on a line spoken after a wake word, or on a typed line holding a question mark or a character's name, that ends with no words and no card says the character's fixed "say that again" line ("No te he entendido bien, ¿me lo repites?" as Coach) and records `silent-to-addressed`, which the audit reports. A typed line that is neither, such as chat between players, may still get silence; a typed turn whose model call failed says so instead (below), while a spoken one gets the same line ([Voice](#voice)).

One turn of the coach's runs at a time per run, under the run's lease ([Decisions](#decisions)). A typed line or a press of More waits up to 30 seconds for it, a spoken one 12; the line is posted only once the turn has the lease, so a line that could not get it is posted nowhere: the asker's stream ends with a `busy` event, and the page puts the line back in the composer and says the coach was answering someone else, with Retry, while a spoken one gets one of the character's fixed "one moment" lines ([Voice](#voice)). Each reply stream opens with an empty line the moment it is asked, so a page or bot waiting for the lease is not left without an answer. Each Claude call has 10 seconds to start answering, one retry, and 20 seconds in all, and a turn's calls together at most 45 seconds, inside the lease's 60; a call that runs out ends as any failed call does ([Decisions](#decisions)).

Its own words are streamed to the asker sentence by sentence as newline-delimited JSON (`text` events), and so is each rung's text the moment it is handed over (a `hint` event, which the run page ignores, since the card shows it, and the voice route speaks), while the run page shows its row "transmitting" with the blinking caret. Code drops a sentence of the model's and records the violation instead when it:

- names an unknown entry (by its name or an alias), holds brackets, or shows a step or hint number ("paso 2", "la última pista", "quedan dos pistas", "2/4");
- repeats a line code said (`repeated`); one that is a verbatim piece of a rung already given is `repeated` too, and that rung is said whole in its place;
- is `meta`: holds a ladder handle, a word such as alt, ladder, thread, card, `tarjeta` or `escalera`, "the players", a tool name, or, in a Spanish run, two or more common English words;
- is `judging`: a confirmation, praise or scolding phrase ("eso es lo que buscáis", "bien visto", "good job", "I've already told you"), or a claim that something is what it gave before ("eso es justo lo que os he dado", "that's what I told you"), except in the call that confirms a step just recorded done;
- is `repeated-own`: a sentence the coach already said in a reply among the last 24 lines;
- `echoes-unhanded`: shares a run of five words with any prepared text not yet handed over in the run (a rung, an Alt, the full answer, an `onSuccess` line, a heads-up) of a step not recorded done;
- is `round-asked`: asks the round in its own words.

Once a sentence is dropped as `meta` or `echoes-unhanded`, every later sentence of the model's own in that reply is dropped too, so no fragment refers back to what was cut; lines code says itself (rungs, fixed lines, a rung restored in place of a verbatim piece of it) still go through.

When the reply ends, its line and card are stored together and every member's next poll shows them. When the reply fails before the coach has said anything or given a card (the call to Claude, or the turn itself throwing), the stream ends with a `failed` event, so the asker's page says it did not answer instead of falling silent; a refusal stays silent. A card's More asks `POST /api/runs/<id>/cards/<card>/more`, which gives the ladder's next rung onto the same card in code, without a model call, when that rung is allowed; when it is not, the model is told More was pressed and chooses an Alt or asks what they see. More shows only on the newest card of its ladder while the ladder has a next rung, the full solution included, and never once its step or part is done; a poll after a step is done sends that step's cards again without it.

### Characters

The voice an attempt speaks with is one of the [voice worker's voices](../../../home/voice/explanation/voice.md#voices), `chatterbox` unless chosen, and each voice has a character, kept with the voices in `shared/voices.ts`: the coach's name, its wake word, the role and manner the system prompt gives the model, and its own wording of every fixed line code says in both languages (the round question, the three holding lines, the three lines asking to say it again and the three "one moment" lines for a spoken question the coach is too busy for, one picked at random each time, the revision's acknowledgement and the import's "From the last session."). The plain voices, `chatterbox`, `chatterbox-expressive` and `piper`, are Coach, a warm and wry teammate. `richtofen` is Richtofen as the Castilian dub of Black Ops 3 voices him: a brilliant, unhinged scientist with dry contempt, mock pity and sudden glee, Castilian with vosotros in Spanish, his colour in word choice and rhythm rather than catchphrases: a nickname for the players in at most one reply in four, no laugh written out, one German word in about one reply in four and a different one each time, never opening with "ja" or two replies alike, a short burst of glee or scorn at a win or a failure, and never talking about what he has, knows or notes down. A character also carries a few example lines (`examples`), given to the model to show the tone and dropped by code as `repeated-own` if it says one; his manner is told to colour his few words without adding any, and to name nothing from the games beyond what the players know.

The character changes only the words around the hints. Rungs, Alts, `onSuccess` lines and heads-ups are said as prepared, every sentence of the model's goes through the same filters, and the audit is the same. The coach takes the character's name wherever players see it: each of its lines is stored under the name it was said under, so lines from before a change keep the old name, and the slip's head, the transmitting row, the composer, the language line and the voice strip use the attempt's current name. The voice strip tells the players to say the character's wake word («bebé» or «richtofen»), the only word that wakes the attempt ([Voice](#voice)).

Each Claude call is a row in `coach_calls`, and each rung a row in `deliveries`, so a run's whole hint history can be read back against its marks.

Every reply is audited by code when it ends. The findings are every violation its calls recorded, plus three checks on the reply as a whole: `unknown-in-reply` when the posted line names an entry still unknown, `rung-skipped` when a rung it handed over sits past a rung of its ladder never given in the run, and `optional-offered` when it handed over a rung of an optional step that was not open before the reply, which happens when the model records the step started itself and then hints it. A reply with any finding gets a row in `audits`, which `watch` prints; what code cannot judge (a paraphrased hint, a choice offered, the wrong ladder for the question) is judged by Claude while watching, in a subagent, on each reply of the live model's as it lands, and by the debrief ([ee-coach → Watch a run](../../../../.claude/skills/ee-coach/SKILL.md#watch-a-run-from-claude-code)).

## Voice

The coach hears and speaks in the group's Discord call through the bot's [zombies module](../../bot/explanation/bot.md); the site keeps the run and the coach, and the bot holds no run state and calls no model. The two talk over HTTP with the bot's `INTERNAL_SECRET` as the bearer token both ways, as the [DJ panel](../../../dj/panel/explanation/panel.md) calls the bot; `BOT_URL` is where the site finds the bot.

- **The offer:** the run page asks `GET /api/runs/<id>/voice` when it opens, when its window takes focus or becomes visible, and then every 2 seconds while the reader is in no channel (or the bot does not answer) and every 10 seconds once they are, never with a request still out and never while hidden, and the function asks the bot's `GET /zombies/voice` for the reader. When the reader is in a voice channel, the voice strip across the top of the chat names it with a **Bring Coach in** button (named for the attempt's character), or says why the coach cannot join it (the bot's role cannot reach it, or it is full); once the coach is in that channel it says so with a steady red dot and shows the character's wake word to say before the question, and "<coach> is answering <name>" with a small meter while a spoken question is being answered ([Site Design System → Voice strip](../reference/design-system.md#components)). A check that finds the bot unreachable or the reader in no channel changes the strip only when the next check agrees, so one slow answer does not make it blink. With the reader in no channel, or the bot unreachable or not configured, it is not shown.
- **Bringing the coach in:** the button posts `POST /api/runs/<id>/voice/join`, which calls the bot's `POST /zombies/join` for the reader with the attempt's `voice`, so the bot can load it at once, and answers `{ channelId }`, 409 with the bot's refusal (`notInVoice`, `noAccess`, `full`), or 503 `botOffline`. Both routes are for the run's members only.
- **Hearing:** the bot listens for every character's wake word, «bebé» and «richtofen», and sends what follows it to `POST /api/voice` as `{ userId, text, word }`, `word` being the wake word heard and required. The function finds the run the speaker is in (a person is in one run at a time); The answer is newline-delimited JSON, streamed as the coach speaks: `{ type: "voice", voice }` first, the run's voice, which the bot speaks in, sent only once the line has reached a run and the run's character's wake word; then one `{ type: "say", text }` per sentence, the coach's own sentences as they pass the filters and each rung's text the moment it is handed over on a card, in the order they were said; then `{ type: "done" }`. A speaker in no run, or a word that is not the wake word of the run's character, gets only `done`, nothing is posted and the bot stays silent. Otherwise the function posts the words in its chat as the speaker's line, marked `spoken`, and runs the coach's turn on it as if typed, so every member sees both in the chat. `POST /api/voice/coach` with `{ userId }` answers `{ voice }`, the voice of the speaker's run or `null`, for `/coach` in Discord, which brings the coach in without the site. A line starting `revisión:` or `revision:` is stored as a revision and its acknowledgement is the one `say`. Since a spoken wake word is always addressed to the coach, a turn in which the coach said nothing and handed over no card, including one whose model call failed, says the character's fixed line asking them to say it again ("No te he entendido bien, ¿me lo repites?" as Coach) rather than silence ([The Coach](#the-coach)). A reply that is only a parenthetical, such as "(silencio)", counts as its silence and is neither posted nor spoken. The bot speaks each sentence as it arrives, so the first is heard while the coach is still writing the next (locally the first `say` arrived about 1.2 s after the request); if its request fails or times out before any sentence came, or the answer named a voice and said nothing, the bot says that line itself, in the character whose voice it holds ([bot](../../bot/explanation/bot.md)). A voice turn waits up to 12 seconds for the run's lease while another reply runs; when it cannot get it, it posts nothing and says one of the character's "one moment" lines, asking them to say it again, so a question asked while another reply runs is either taken within 12 seconds or turned away aloud. The 12 seconds and a turn's own time can still pass the bot's 25 (a turn may take up to 45 when its calls are slow), and then the answer lands in the chat unspoken and the bot says its own "say that again" line; a usual turn takes a few seconds.
- **Speaking Claude's lines:** a line that reaches the chat without a voice turn asking for it, Claude's step-in or a held question answered when a version lands, is sent to the bot's `POST /zombies/say` as `{ text, voice, userIds }`: what the coach said followed by the text of the hint card it carries, as a voice reply speaks them, which the bot splits into sentences and speaks one by one so the first is heard while the rest are made, the run's voice, and the Discord IDs of the run's current members. The bot speaks it only while it holds a call that one of those members is in, and answers 409 `notInCall` otherwise. The request is sent in the background, handed to Vercel's `waitUntil` so the function stays alive for it after the post has answered, with a 5 second limit; a failed or slow one is logged as `bot say` and never delays or fails the post. Replies to typed lines are not spoken ([Decisions](#decisions)).
- **Keeping the call:** while the coach is in the call the bot sends every line said there to `POST /api/voice/transcript` as `{ userId, name, text, at, ms, broughtBy }`, with the same bearer token. The function files it under the speaker's run, or, for a speaker in no run, under the run of `broughtBy`, the member who brought the coach in, and stores it in `call_lines`; it answers `{ kept }`, `false` when neither is in a run. No route returns them: not the watch stream, the Claude read of the run, the run page, the coach model or the audits ([Decisions](#decisions)), so the live model still gets only what follows the wake word and the group's talk never leaves the site.

Typed questions get text answers only, even with the coach in the call, and a run keeps working without the bot: the site never needs it to answer in the chat.

## Moving Between Pages

The page moves between screens itself, with `history.pushState` (`src/route.ts`), so the header stays and only the content under it changes. Each page's last reply is kept in memory (`src/store.ts`), keyed by what it shows: the maps list, a map, an attempt. Coming back to a page shows that reply at once and asks for it again in the background, swapping the fresh one in place when it lands, so what another member did since shows up as soon as that reply is in, without a skeleton; if the refresh fails the page keeps what it showed, and the next visit asks again. A page shows its skeleton only when nothing about it is known yet. A reply less than a second old, or one already on its way, is used rather than asked for twice. Pointing at a link, focusing it or pressing it starts that page's request, unless its reply is under 15 seconds old, so it is usually in by the time the page opens. A map opened from the maps list draws its banner from the list's row while its attempts load, and an attempt's log keeps the lines it already polled, so coming back to it asks only for lines after them. The maps list keeps the scroll position it was left at, whether the reader comes back by Back or by a link to it.

The store lives only in the open tab and empties on every full load. Signing in, switching members with the dev sign-in route and switching the language all reload the page, so one member's replies never reach another, and an attempt the reader joins or starts is asked for afresh rather than shown from a copy taken before they were in it.

## Load Time

Every API reply carries a `Server-Timing` header (Hono's `timing` middleware): `viewer` for the root lookup, `db` for opening the Postgres connections, one entry per query (`maps`, `runs`, `run`, `members`, `mine`), `names` for the bot's name lookup on the map view and the attempt's sheet ([Attempts](#attempts)) and `total`. A browser's network panel shows it, as does `curl -sD - -o /dev/null` with the reader's cookie.

A page load makes one API call. `/api/maps` runs three queries side by side (`maps`, `runs`, `mine`): the published maps, every run with whether it is beaten, and the reader's current run; `/api/maps/<game>/<map>` runs four (`maps`, `runs`, `members`, `mine`), its map's runs and everyone who joined them in place of every run; `/api/runs/<id>` runs its four (the run, the published maps, its members, the reader's current run) side by side. The map view and the attempt's sheet then look up the members' names (`names`), which costs nothing when every name was looked up in the last minute. Before 2026-11-15 a page load also called `/api/me`, so it made two root lookups and `/api/maps` ran four queries one after another over every run ever made; from 2026-11-15 to the attempts list it listed only runs with someone in them.

## Hosting

Vercel serves the site from the owner's Hobby team as the project `enbraile-zombies`: the Vite build's static `dist/` and the API as one Vercel Function in the same project. Unlike pkmn's and the DJ panel's, the function is not built by Vercel from a file in `api/`: `npm run build` ends with `scripts/build-api.ts`, which bundles `api/_lib/handler.ts` (the Hono app as a Node request listener) with esbuild into `.vercel/output/functions/api.func/`, copies `dist/` to `.vercel/output/static/` and writes the routes, and Vercel deploys that [Build Output API](https://vercel.com/docs/build-output-api) directory as it stands. The routes serve a file when one matches, send every `/api/` path to the function and every other path but `/assets/` to `/index.html`. The coach and the Anthropic SDK, over a third of the bundle, sit in chunks of their own that the function loads on the first reply that asks the coach, so a page load never parses them ([Load Time](#load-time)). The function runs in Frankfurt (`fra1`, in `vercel.json` and the function's `.vc-config.json`), beside its Neon database (`aws-eu-central-1`). The database is the Neon project `zombies`, its own and never pkmn's, with a `main` branch for production and a `dev` branch for previews. The `zombies-site.yml` workflow deploys pushes to `main` touching `zombies/site/` to production, and other branches' pushes as previews, which Vercel's Deployment Protection keeps to the team ([How to Deploy the Vercel Projects](../../../general/how-to/deploy-vercel-projects.md)). The function also reads `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `COACH_MODEL`, set in Production. `zombies.enbraile.vip` is on the project's domains and has, since the owner added it on 2026-11-15, a DNS-only (grey-cloud) `CNAME` to the Vercel target in the `enbraile.vip` zone on the owner's Cloudflare account. It is DNS-only for the reason pkmn's is: Cloudflare's proxied IPs are blocked by Spanish ISPs during LaLiga matches ([The pkmn Site → Decisions](../../../pkmn/site/explanation/site.md#decisions)). The root site lists the domain in its `trustedOrigins`, so sign-in returns to it. Setting it up is in [How to Run and Deploy the Site](../how-to/run-and-deploy-the-site.md#9-set-up-production-once).

## Decisions

- **A spoken question the coach is too busy for gets "one moment":** when a wake word comes while another member's reply holds the run, the voice turn waits up to 12 seconds, then posts nothing and says a fixed line in the character's voice asking them to say it again, rather than the bot waiting longer and speaking an answer late mid-round, or the answer reaching the chat only, unspoken. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **Claude's calls are bounded:** the SDK's defaults (a 10-minute timeout and 2 retries) let one hung call hold the run's lease for its full 60 seconds; its timeout covers only the wait for an answer to start, not a stream that stalls, so each call also carries a 20 second deadline and the turn one of 45 seconds. Measured on the local stack, 2026-11-17: a call given 1 ms failed into the page's "didn't answer" at once and left the lease and the queue empty. _(the agent's choice, measured, 2026-11-17)_
- **The coach's queue is shown:** the attempt page shows who the coach is answering and whose questions wait, with their lines and whether they came by voice, and marks each spoken line in the log, since a second member's question looked ignored while another's reply ran. The waiting turns are rows of their own (`coach_queue`) rather than read from the lease, since only the turns know who asked. _(owner asked, the design the agent's, 2026-11-17)_
- **Claude's lines spoken sentence by sentence:** the bot splits a step-in or a held question's answer into sentences before speaking it, as a voice reply already comes, since the voice worker makes a whole text before playing any of it and a hint with the coach's line took seconds more to start. _(owner reported it, the fix the agent's, 2026-11-17)_
- **The 2026-11-17 round, after the first live night:** the group found the coach "not really useful": it was hard to know whether they were doing things right (the Pack-a-Punch reported done six times and never recorded), it felt hardcoded, and the page pinned a slip per open thread. Read from the run's log, 9 progress reports went unrecorded and 3 threads stayed open: done criteria were shown only on open or ready threads, so a step still blocked behind an unrecorded one could not be matched, and the model praised in its own words instead of calling `record_progress` 4 times when the criterion was in front of it. Changed: a separate progress check per line ([The Coach](#the-coach)), the reply model no longer seeing any `Done when` (a replay showed it rewording one aloud, a leak), the next rung after a step done chosen after all of a reply's records, `judging` dropping only its sentence and off when confirming a step recorded, a sentence of an earlier reply dropped as `repeated-own`, `echoes-unhanded` ignoring done steps, the hint request no longer opening an unrelated thread when the latest one is spent, hints explained in plain words and general Zombies questions answered, step-ins recording progress, one pinned slip, and «revisión» spoken without a colon counted as a revision. Measured by replaying the night's 35 lines against the code before (Richtofen, Haiku 5.5, reasoning off): steps recorded on the line that reported them went from 2 to 8, prepared confirmations from 2 to 7, refused hint requests from 3 to 1, exact repeats from 1 to 0, major leaks 0 in both; first words went from a median 0.69 s to 0.99 s, the progress check's cost. Still seen: the coach rewording one nudge across many replies, level 4 given on a complaint since the full solution is a hint like any other, and a question about a finished step still declined. _(owner asked for the round, the design the agent's, measured, 2026-11-17)_
- **Richtofen varies himself:** his manner caps laughter and German words and asks for varied openings, after the owner found him answering "ja" every time; a Richtofen replay of the recorded run went from 42 "ja" and 12 of 27 replies opening with it to one, with 10 German words over five different ones. _(owner reported it, the wording the agent's choice, measured, 2026-11-16)_ On 2026-11-17 the owner still heard too much «ratitas» and laughing. The model's own text held no «ratitas» in the night's 32 replies or in a replay: it was the fixed "say that again" line, «¿Qué? ¡Hablad más claro, ratitas!», which the site and the bot say on every voice turn that ends with nothing spoken, so the same sentence came back again and again. The model's own text did call the players test subjects in 8 of 30 replayed replies, since the manner listed nicknames as things to do. The fixed lines now come in threes without the nickname, and the manner describes him instead of listing tics, with example lines. A replay went from 8 nicknames, 7 German words and 21 exclamations in 30 replies to 0, 4 and 17 in 29. A middle version with no exclamation guidance was judged flat and helpdesk-like. _(owner reported it and asked for examples, the wording the agent's, measured, 2026-11-17)_
- **The coach feels real time and answers what is not the easter egg itself:** the coach should answer about as fast as a teammate on the radio and answer trivial questions (whether it hears them, greetings, small talk, general Zombies knowledge) without prepared lines, without the hints getting worse or anything spoiled; everything that moves the players toward the solution stays prepared hints. If reasoning turned out to be needed for quality, it was to stay. _(owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **Reasoning off, measured:** the coach calls Haiku 5.5 with `thinking` disabled and `effort` low. Replaying the owner's recorded run (23 player lines) with `npm run replay`, the first words came after a median 2.0 s (max 4.4 s) before the change, when 400–600 output tokens of each call were hidden reasoning and no call read the cache; after it, a median of 0.83 s and 0.89 s in two replays (max about 1.1 s). Reasoning on with the new prompt took 1.0–1.3 s and was not safer: in review each mode had one replay with a major leak, and reasoning off's was then closed in code (the filters below). The final review of the two reasoning-off replays, by a subagent reading them without context, found 0 major leaks in each (baseline 0), 2–4 minor ones (baseline 4) and 3 unhelpful turns each (baseline 6). So the owner's "keep reasoning if it has to stay" did not apply. _(the agent's choice, measured, 2026-11-16)_
- **The prompt cached:** a cache breakpoint on the system prompt, the same for every turn of a character, and the request's top-level automatic caching for the conversation, so a turn reads most of its input from the cache instead of none. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **No second call when the tools end the reply:** when every tool the model called ends the reply (a hint, `record_progress` done, a heads-up, `hold`, `ask_round`, `repeat_hint`), code ends the turn rather than sending the results back for a call that, with the reply complete, could only add words. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **What the coach may say in its own words:** greetings, hearing checks, small talk, general Zombies knowledge and a hint already given explained in that hint's words only; never its own knowledge of the map, and directions only through hints. From 2026-11-16 the long list of what it may and may not say is cut to a few lines; code's filters on its sentences are unchanged. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16; shortened by the owner, 2026-11-16)_ From 2026-11-17 a hint already given is explained plainly in the coach's own words, adding nothing it does not say, since the owner asked that the group can ask about hints and in general; the control is code's filters on every sentence (unknown names, five-word echoes of unhanded prepared text, numbers) and `hold` for any question about the map's easter egg no hint covers. _(owner asked, the agent's design, 2026-11-17)_
- **`repeat_hint` says a hint again, and only that:** a hint said again word for word comes from code, not the model, so it is never misquoted. Until 2026-11-16 a third request for the most recent hint handed over the next rung instead; that went with the owner's simplification, since a request for the next hint now gets it from code before the model is called. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16; the third-ask step removed by the owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **The most recent hint named in the state, Alts as calls:** the state names the most recent hint so "the hint" needs no question, and writes each Alt as the `give_hint` call that gives it with its `when`. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **No hint records in the history:** the coach's past turns are its words alone, since the model copied the `[hint given: …]` records as bracketed text; what was given is in the state. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **Spoken lines marked:** a member's line from voice is stored with `spoken` and shown to the model as `(spoken)`, so it reads a misheard line by what was most likely said rather than asking them to repeat it. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **More sentence filters, and the reply muted after one:** `meta`, `judging`, `echoes-unhanded` and `round-asked` catch what the replays showed the model saying aloud: handles and tool words, English in a Spanish run, praise and confirmation (whether something matters is solution content), five-word runs of prepared text not yet handed over, and the round asked in its own words. After a `meta`, `judging` or `echoes-unhanded` drop the rest of the model's own sentences in the reply are dropped too, since what followed referred to what was cut. _(the agent's choice, measured, 2026-11-16)_ From 2026-11-17 a `judging` drop no longer mutes the rest, `judging` is off in the call that confirms a step just recorded, and `echoes-unhanded` ignores the prepared text of steps recorded done, after the first live night showed a confirmation cut to one word and a question about a finished step muted ([2026-11-17 round](#decisions)).
- **The voice answer streamed sentence by sentence:** `POST /api/voice` streams newline-delimited JSON, as the chat reply does, and the bot speaks each sentence as it arrives, rather than waiting for the whole turn and a joined reply, so the first words are heard about a second after the request. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **A coach change is checked by replaying a recorded run:** `npm run replay` runs the changed coach on every line of a run already played, against the local database, and a context-free subagent judges the replay file for spoilers and quality, since the file holds hint text and the main conversation may be read by the players. It calls the real model, cents per run. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **Claude's lines are spoken in the call, replies to typed lines are not:** a step-in and a held question answered when a version lands reach the call too, since the owner noticed the group missed Claude's lines when playing by voice; the coach's replies to typed chat stay text only, since the typed chat is often members talking among themselves or someone not on the call, and a voice reading it back would talk over the call. _(owner reported it, the reach the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **Speaking Claude's lines waits on nothing:** the site hands the bot request to Vercel's `waitUntil` by the request context `@vercel/functions` reads (`Symbol.for("@vercel/request-context")`), rather than adding that package, whose CommonJS index bundles its OIDC client: 278 KB minified for the one function, measured with esbuild. Locally there is no context and the request simply runs. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **A spoken wake word is never met with silence:** a voice turn in which the coach said nothing and gave no card answers a fixed line asking them to repeat, and a reply that is only a parenthetical (Haiku wrote "(silencio)" for the `(silence)` token) is treated as silence, after the owner got no answer and then a spoken "(silencio)" in a call. _(owner reported it, the fix the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **A step done earlier is dated from what the players told the coach:** its time and author come from the chat line in which they reported it, found by a call of its own, rather than from the later step that marked it, and never from the call's transcripts. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **The call's transcripts are still kept:** the bot keeps sending every line said in the call and the site keeps storing it in `call_lines`, though no route returns them. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **Kowakujo's zones may come from a guide site's marker data:** prepare may fetch a guide publisher's interactive-map markers into a map's gitignored `sources/` to place zones its unlabeled survey image cannot show. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **Claude Code never reads the call's transcriptions:** watching a run or debriefing it reads only the group's interactions with the coach (the chat, which carries what they ask it by voice, its replies, rungs, gaps, feedback and audits). **Why:** the owner; the call is the group talking among themselves, not to the coach. _(owner, 2026-11-16)_ From 2026-11-17 the machine API does not return them at all, rather than the scripts dropping what it sent, and the watch wakes Claude only on the coach's own events and the group's feedback, never on a member's line, after the owner raised it as a privacy matter. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **The site is the brain, the bot only an interface:** the site's function holds map bundles, run state and chat, calls Claude and enforces the hint rules in code; the bot turns a wake word and what follows it into a call to the site's API and speaks the answer. **Why:** the bot runs on the homelab and has other jobs, so the site must work with the bot down. A bot-module brain was proposed first and rejected. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **No numbers, no step list, the UI is never a hint:** no step or hint numbers anywhere (the terminal's "Step 5 · hint 2/4" is not shown), no list of steps, and no buttons or suggestions that tell players what to try. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **Hints as cards:** one shared chat with the coach, where hints arrive as cards with a generic "more" control and the three feedback controls (too obvious, right, too vague); a level-3 zone shows under its card. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **The coach's voice only:** no chimes or clips on a new hint, since a cue would itself carry meaning. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **One `bundle.json` per map dir:** `publish` takes the dir that holds a map's files, and `compile` writes the bundle there. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **Unpublishing deletes for good:** a map is removed with its versions and every attempt on it, rather than hidden, and Harrow Station, the made-up test map, is removed from production while staying in the repo for local checks. _(owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **Deleting one attempt:** `delete-run` removes a single run and everything under it while the map stays published, after the owner asked to delete his group's Blood of the Dead attempt; `unpublish` and publishing again would have done it only by dropping every version and image of the map. _(owner asked, the agent's implementation, 2026-11-16)_
- **Unpublish by name, images only when unshared:** `unpublish` takes `<game>/<map>` rather than a map's dir, since a map to remove may have no bundle on the machine; an image is deleted with the map only when no other map's version names it, since images are stored once by digest. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **Bundle IDs from the prepare files' numbers:** steps become `step-<n>`, a step's part ladders `part-<n>` in order, and knowledge entries their `K` IDs in lowercase, so the bundle needs no second set of names to keep in step with `hints.md`. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **`reveals` written by hand in the hints:** each line lists after its `Mentions` the IDs it is meant to give away (`Reveals:`), rather than `compile` guessing them from step order, since players choose the order. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **Several Alts per rung, each with its `when`:** prepared hints hold more than one Alt for a step, each replacing one named rung in one situation. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **L4 stays in the dossier:** `compile` takes level 4 from the dossier's `L4 as spoken`, so `hints.md`, which the blind check reads, never holds an answer; the Spanish copy holds it as `L4`. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **A bundle's baseline marks:** a knowledge entry is `known` in the bundle only when `knowledge.md` marks it `known`; `revealed` counts as `unknown`, since it records what one terminal session gave away rather than what the players brought. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **Names per language, art as a drawn plan:** the map's name is stored in both languages, since in-game names differ by language, and its row shows one of three drawn plans chosen in the bundle's `art`, since no game's art went on the site; from 2026-11-15 a map's announcement image replaces the plan wherever it has one, and the plan stays for a map without one. The keys stay `teal`, `rust` and `furnace` from the first take's tinted silhouettes, standing for the boards' Harrow Station, Saltmarsh Asylum and Kessel Works plans. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **The radio log look:** the owner found the first boards "very AI", so the site was redrawn as the group's radio log (transcript rows, dispatch slips, survey sheets, condensed type and five flat inks on paper), which replaces the first take. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_ The third take replaced its look and kept its identity, below.
- **A map's label is its game:** the boards label the made-up maps "Sample"; a published map that is not the test map shows its `game` instead, as the bundle holds no other short line about it. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **A rung's `reveals`:** the discovery lets a rung mention an unknown thing when it is the rung meant to reveal it; the bundle says which rung that is with `reveals`, next to `mentions`. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **Versions numbered per map, deduplicated by digest:** the SHA-256 of the bundle's JSON with sorted keys, so key order in the file never makes a new version. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **Start an attempt on the map's view, not its row:** the boards first drew Start a run on every map's row, but a button that did nothing until runs existed would have been a dead control, so it was left off; since the third take a map's row opens its view, which holds Start an attempt ([Attempts](#attempts)). _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15; rewritten 2026-11-16)_
- **Several local members:** `DEV_USER_ID` takes a list and each browser picks one of them, so a Claude-run check can be two members of one run; it exists only where `DEV_USER_ID` is set, never in production, which keeps Discord through the root as the DJ panel does. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **The player picks the map:** a run is started from the map the players are playing, picked from the maps list as the boards draw it. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **A run never ends by itself:** not even when its last required step is done. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_ Closing is built from 2026-11-17 (below). _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **A run ends only when the players say so:** a run ends only when a member asks for it and confirms. _(owner, 2026-11-15)_
- **A closed attempt is read-only and can be reopened:** closing releases every member and the coach stops answering; the attempt stays on its map's list marked closed, its chat and roadmap still readable, and any member of it can reopen it, so a close by mistake costs nothing. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_
- **Progress kept in `run_steps`, never shown:** what the players started and finished lives on the run for the coach and later for debriefs, and no route a browser calls returns it, since a list of threads would be a hint. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_ From 2026-11-17 the steps done are shown as the roadmap (below); what is started, open or still to do stays unshown.
- **A roadmap of done steps only:** the attempt shows a tree of the steps recorded done, branched by their `requires`, so the players see what the coach thinks they have finished and can correct it with a revision; nothing about what is next, how many steps are left or which threads are open reaches the page, which keeps the rule above for everything not done. Once the attempt is beaten, the whole tree is revealed. A node is labelled with a prepared milestone label per step and language, blind-checked at prepare like the hints, rather than its title (English, may name unknown things) or its `On success` line (a spoken sentence); the tree must be visually interesting. _(owner, 2026-11-17)_ It is a column beside the chat, as the owner expected it, rather than a panel in the rail (2026-11-17), and is drawn as a history graph, one row per step in the order done with branches as lanes, rather than a top-down tree, since a row per step reads at any width and tells the night in order; it was built straight in code rather than as boards first, as the pinned slips were, since the owner made it the night's priority. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-17)_
- **The L1 after a step done is chosen in code:** `record_progress` hands it over itself, from the ready steps, so a step done always gets its `On success` line and an L1 in the same reply even when the model forgets, and an optional step is never among them. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **Handles only, never titles:** a step's or part's title is never shown to the model; it only decides whether a done, blocked or optional ladder is listed, once it names nothing unknown. Until 2026-11-16 the title was shown beside the handle, and the model paraphrased titles aloud. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15; titles taken out 2026-11-16)_
- **Done implies its `requires`:** a step reported done marks the steps it requires done, since the bundle's `requires` say they had to come first. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **The blocked ladder from prepared lines:** the skill's blocked-thread ladder has no lines of its own in the bundle, so a hint asked for on a blocked step is the missing step's own ladder, which keeps every word prepared and checked. The 10 seconds is a starting value until the `bot names` log is read in production: Discord's forced fetches could not be timed without the real bot. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **A step-in looks like the coach:** members see a step-in as one of the coach's lines; only `posted_by`, the watch stream and the run's log say Claude wrote it. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-15)_
- **A character per voice, beside the voices in the site:** each worker voice the site offers maps to a character (name, wake word, prompt manner, fixed lines) in `shared/voices.ts`, read by the coach, the API and the pages alike, since the site owns the coach and the bot holds no run state; the plain voices share Coach. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **The voice is chosen when starting and changeable in the attempt:** Start an attempt offers it beside the language, Coach by default as before, and any member can change it from the rail or the phone's strip, since a group may want to try a voice mid-night and nothing about the run depends on it. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **Hint text never takes the character:** rungs, Alts, `onSuccess` lines and heads-ups stay as prepared; only the model's own words and the fixed lines code says take it, so the audit and the knowledge marks are untouched. Whether hints get each voice's own wording is still open in [Zombies Coach](../../../work-in-progress/zombies-coach/tasks.md). _(owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **The coach takes the voice's name:** "Richtofen" when it is Richtofen, wherever players see the coach's name and in the prompt's name for itself; plain voices are "Coach". _(owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **The plain coach is Coach:** «cari» was only the trigger word and had become the coach's name; the plain voices' character is now Coach, each attempt answers only its own character's wake word («coach» or «richtofen»), and the `/cari` command is `/coach`. _(owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **Coach is woken by «bebé», not «coach»:** Whisper, transcribing in Spanish, wrote the English «coach» as "Boa" and "Koha" in the owner's call and never woke it; «bebé» is a Spanish word it spells reliably. Coach keeps its name and `/coach`; only the wake word changed, and it can wake on «bebé» said in passing. _(owner, 2026-11-16)_
- **A line keeps the name it was said under:** after a voice change, earlier lines still read "Coach" or "Richtofen" as they were said, since the stored name is the record; the live chrome follows the current voice. _(the agent's choice, 2026-11-16)_
- **The coach never ends a turn silent when addressed,** extending the fix above from the voice route to the coach itself: a voice turn always follows its wake word, and a typed line with a question mark or a character's name asks it something, so either gets a hint, the hold or a short line of its own; when the model still ends the turn with nothing, code says the character's "say that again" line and the audit records `silent-to-addressed`. The prompt now tells the model to answer whenever it is spoken to, which was the root cause; a spoken turn whose model call failed, or whose request the bot could not get through, gets the same line in the character's wording too. Deliberate silence stays only for typed chat not addressed to it. **Why:** on production a spoken «qué te parece …» after the wake word got a turn with no text, no hint and no hold, nothing was spoken, and the coach looked broken. _(owner reported it, 2026-11-16)_
- **A theory put to the coach is a request:** when the players voice a theory or guess about an open thread to the coach, it hands over that thread's next rung, or the Alt whose `when` fits, at once, and never confirms, denies or comments on the theory itself; the prepared hint does that. `judging` also catches a claim that something is what the coach gave before. **Why:** on production a voiced theory was neither a hint request nor "stuck", so it fell under "anything else" and got an answer in the model's own words that was false, half judged the theory and pointed somewhere, while the next rung was there to give. On a local replay of that line the model handed over a hint in fifteen tries of fifteen, and the fitting Alt in all nine tries once that Alt's `when` described what players would say. The prompt also asks `mark_known` for the players' whole line, a theory included, since a fragment of it can miss an entry they named. _(owner reported it, the agent's design, 2026-11-16)_
- **Aliases for knowledge entries:** an entry may carry aliases per language, in `knowledge.md`'s `Aliases (English)` and `Aliases (Spanish)` columns, and `namedIn` matches them exactly like names, so `mark_known`, the sentence filter that drops a sentence naming an unknown entry, and every check that hides an Alt, heads-up, condition or title naming one all use them. Players say things in their own words (a translation, an article or preposition, a plural), and an entry they named stayed unknown because only its exact name matched. An alias is a phrase or a name specific to the map, never a common word on its own, since it also drops sentences. _(owner reported it, the agent's design, 2026-11-16)_
