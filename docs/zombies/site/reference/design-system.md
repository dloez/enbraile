# Zombies Site Design System

The visual system of `zombies/site/`: its tokens, type, layout shell, components and recurring patterns. For a screen not built yet the frozen boards in `zombies/site/design/` are the source of truth and this doc describes them; for one that is built the code is, and this doc changes in the same commit as it does, as pkmn's does ([pkmn Site Design System](../../../pkmn/site/reference/design-system.md)). Players call a run an attempt ([The Zombies Site](../explanation/site.md)); the boards drawn before 2026-11-15 still say run. Built so far: the header with the "Your attempt" pill, the maps list, a map's view with its banner, its attempts and Start an attempt, the join view, and an attempt's rail, transcript, composer with its Revision toggle and revision mode, dispatch slips with their feedback, the pinned slips of the steps in progress, sent revisions, transmitting row, Copy link, the voice picker and the voice panel and bar (without the members' in-voice marks), a level-3 slip's zone, and members' Discord avatars on their chips, with their loading, empty and error states, the tokens in `src/index.css` and the fonts bundled through `@fontsource`. The rules these screens follow are in [The Zombies Site → Decisions](../explanation/site.md#decisions).

## Boards

The third take keeps the radio log's identity (a run is a timestamped log, a hint is a typed dispatch slip, a level-3 zone is a marked survey sheet) and redraws it for reading at a glance mid-game: a warm dark theme, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body text at 16–18px in sentence case, soft rounded cards, and the slip and survey sheet as the light cards on it. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-15, after finding the radio log's flow awkward and its UI hard to read ([The Zombies Site → Decisions](../explanation/site.md#decisions)), and does not review the boards themselves, to see the site as a surprise. They follow the `.dc.html` board format of `pkmn/site/design/`: one fixed-size frame per board, 1440×900 for desktop, 1920×1080 for a wide screen (the `-wide` boards, drawn on 2026-11-15 when every page went full width) and 390×844 for a phone. The same boards sit on the "Third take" page of the Claude design canvas [Zombies Site](https://claude.ai/artifact/1C4DeEgm1B7vsMmP4DsXSu) under PascalCase names in `v3/` (`v3/Maps.dc.html`, `v3/RunZonePhone.dc.html`), which the boards' links use; the canvas's "First take" and "Radio log" pages keep the replaced boards. They never change; a later revision is drawn on the canvas and copied in again. The boards still name the coach "Cari", which the built site shows as the attempt's character name ("Coach" or "Richtofen"). Their maps (Harrow Station, Saltmarsh Asylum, Kessel Works), people, messages and hint text are made up, the plans are drawn shapes rather than any game's images, and their Google Fonts link stands for the bundled fonts.

## Rules

- **The UI is never a hint.** No step or hint numbers, no count of hints given, no list of steps, no progress bar, and no button or suggestion that tells players what to try. A slip's only action toward the next hint is a generic **More**; its rungs are told apart by order and fading, never by number. The only counts shown are of maps on file and of a map's attempts and beaten attempts.
- **One dark theme**, warm near-black ground with raised `surf` cards; the dispatch slip and the survey sheet are the only light cards. No glows, gradients or shadows; edges are 1px lines and inset rings.
- **Rounded, soft cards**: 12px for cards, large plans and attempt rows, 8px for buttons, options, a small plan's tile and the slip, 10px for the Revision toggle and the composer's field, 6px for a slip's survey sheet, a full pill for the "Your attempt" pill and the beaten mark, and circles for chips.
- **Tokens only** in the site's code, as in pkmn: every colour from a variable, no hex in components. The boards themselves write some values raw.
- **Sentence case** for every label, heading and button; condensed uppercase only for the wordmark and the big map titles.
- **No helper text** explaining the UI; labels name things. The composer's placeholder is the one sentence of prompt, and the line naming the attempt the reader leaves is the one sentence of consequence.
- **A log, no game's branding**: drawn plans, log rows, dispatch slips and survey sheets. No logos, characters, round counters or tally marks from any game in the chrome; the one piece of a game's art is a map's own announcement image, which stands in for its plan where it has one (built; the boards draw only plans).
- **Red is the coach, your attempt and the main action, blue is a revision, slip yellow is a hint.** An attempt being played is ringed `text` and a beaten one marked with a filled `text` pill, so the marks differ in lightness and shape as well as hue.
- **en/es chrome**, the switch in the header; the coach speaks the run's language.
- **Desktop and phone both designed**; the phone is not a squeezed desktop.

## Layout Shell

- **Header**: `surf`, 64px (60px phone), a 1px `line` under it: the wordmark (a 10px `red-tx` square and `ZOMBIES`), then at the right the "Your attempt" pill, the language switch (`ES` `EN` in an 8px-rounded ring, the current one filled `text`) and the reader's initials chip. The pill shows on every screen outside an attempt once the reader's attempt is known: a live dot, "Your attempt" and the map's name with an arrow on desktop, "Your attempt" alone on a phone. The phone header has no name chip. Inside an attempt on a phone the wordmark gives way to a back arrow and the map's name, cut with an ellipsis; the wordmark's cell stays blank while a run's link loads, so nothing swaps once drawn, unless the site already knows the run (seen this visit, or the reader's own from the pill), when the back arrow and name show at once.
- **Full width, one scroller**: every page spans the screen inside its page edge, with no fixed container width. The page itself never scrolls: the header stays and the content under it scrolls (a page's `main`, or an attempt's log and rail), and the app takes the visual viewport's height so a phone's keyboard shrinks it rather than scrolling it.
- **Maps list**: 64px under the header (32px phone): the title with "N maps on file" at its right, then the map cards in as many columns of at least 560px as fit, 16px apart (12px phone): three at 1920, two at 1440, one from about 1232px down.
- **Map view**: under a 32px top (12px phone), back to the maps, then the banner across the full width (Map banner below), then 48px under it (24px phone) the attempts and Start an attempt in two columns at 3:2, 48px apart (32px under 1180px), stacked under 960px and on a phone, 24px apart.
- **Join view**: two equal columns 64px apart (32px under 1180px), under a 32px top (24px phone): at the left back to the maps, the label, the big name and the plan; at the right, starting level with the name, the join card and its lines. On a phone one column, the plan full width, then the sections 20px apart.
- **A run, desktop**: a 340px `surf` rail at the left (the map, the coach's language, the voice picker, the members with "you", Copy link, and the voice panel at its foot while the reader is in a voice channel), the log to its right with the newest at the bottom on an 88px / rest-of-the-column grid, the pinned slips above the log (built; no board), and the composer in a `surf` bar under it; the log, the trouble line, the revision-mode line and the composer's field span the main column, and every message, sent revision, acknowledgement and slip spans the log's column after the time column.
- **A run, phone**: the run's header, a 52px strip with the member chips, the voice picker and the coach's language and the copy-link icon, the voice bar while the reader is in a voice channel, the pinned slips folded to their newest rung (built; no board), the log with each name and time on one line, and the composer at the bottom.

## Spacing

Every padding, margin and gap is a step of one 4px scale, as `--sp-<n>` tokens in `src/index.css`, `<n>` counting fours: `--sp-1` 4px, `--sp-2` 8, `--sp-3` 12, `--sp-4` 16, `--sp-5` 20, `--sp-6` 24, `--sp-8` 32, `--sp-12` 48, `--sp-16` 64, `--sp-20` 80. The boards' in-between values (6, 10, 14, 18, 22px) are drawn at the nearest multiple of 8. Their few values under 4px, the language's 2px under "Coach speaks" on the join card and the chips' 3px gap, are drawn at 4px. Sizes (heights, widths, a button's minimum) are not spacing and stay in pixels, and so do the few offsets that line a baseline up: a message's or the transmitting line's 2px under its name, a log row's time, and the right column's 68px top on the map and join views, which starts it level with the name.

| Where | Desktop | Under 1180px | Phone |
|-------|---------|--------------|-------|
| Page edge | 48px | 24px | 16px |
| Between cards in a list | 16px | 16px | 12px |
| Between sections, and blocks inside a card | 48px, 24px | the same | 24px, 16px |
| Inside a card | 24px | 24px | 16px |
| The run's rail, the log's sides | 24px, 48px | 24px, 24px | —, 16px |

## States

Every fetch, post and poll has a loading, an empty and an error state in the place the content will take: loading as skeletons, the rest as plain `mute` sentences.

| State | Where | Shape |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Loading | The maps list, a map's view, a run's link, a log's first poll, each only the first time this visit | Skeletons, as the DJ panel's: `line`-coloured blocks with no text, built from the screen's own classes so each sits where its content will (map rows with plan tile, eyebrow, name and runs line; the map view's banner, three attempt rows and the Start an attempt card; the run's rail, three transcript rows and the composer; the join view's head and card), pulsing to 45% opacity every 1.4 s. A run's link shows the run skeleton unless the site already knows the reader is in another run or none, when it shows the join skeleton. The content fades in over 0.35 s when it lands; a log's first rows fade in together, later lines do not. The maps title keeps its line and the count is a bar while it waits. A map opened from the maps list shows its banner from the list at once, with only its attempts and Start an attempt card as skeletons |
| Seen before | The maps list, a map's view, a run's link, a log | A page already seen this visit shows what it last showed at once, with no skeleton and no fade, and its fresh reply replaces it in place when it lands; the maps list comes back at the scroll position it was left at ([The Zombies Site → Moving Between Pages](../explanation/site.md#moving-between-pages)) |
| Empty | The maps list, a map's attempts, a log | A dashed 12px card: "No maps on file yet.", "No attempts on this map yet."; a log says "Nothing on the log yet." On a map row, "No attempts yet" |
| Can't load | The maps list, a map's view, a run's link | "Can't reach the site right now." with a ghost Retry; a map or attempt that does not exist says "No such map on file." or "No such attempt on file." with a ghost Maps |
| Trouble in a run | Above the composer, under a 1px `line`, laid over the log on `bg` while the log's foot gains room for it, so the composer and the log's box never move | One line and a ghost Retry: "Can't reach the site right now." while polls fail (Retry polls at once), "Your line didn't go through." when a line was not stored (the line goes back into the composer, Retry sends it), "Coach didn't answer." (the attempt's character's name) when its reply failed (Retry sends the line again, or presses More again when the failed reply was a More) |
| Starting or joining failed | The map view's Start an attempt card and its attempts, the join view's card | "Can't reach the site right now." above the action; the action works again |
| Sending | The log | The reader's line shows at once in `mute` until the run stores it, then the coach's transmitting row follows |
| Copied | Copy link | The button reads "Link copied" (or "Couldn't copy") for 2 seconds, also said to screen readers |

Each request gives up after 10 seconds (20 for a reply to start streaming), so a hung server reads as an error rather than a page waiting forever. Buttons that post are disabled while their post runs, so a double click sends once. The log follows new lines only while the reader is at its foot; scrolled up to read, it stays put.

## Focus and Fit

- **Focus**: a 2px outline in the element's own text colour, 2px out, on keyboard focus only; a language option shows it around its whole label.
- **Touch targets**: every control is at least 44px in both directions.
- **Title fit**: a map's big name on its view and the join view, and the run's rail title, keep their board sizes and shrink, only when needed, until their longest word fits the column; words never break.
