# The DJ Panel

The web panel for the [DJ bot](../../bot/explanation/bot.md), in `dj/panel/`, for `dj.enbraile.vip`. It shows what is playing and offers the player's controls, the queue, playlists, history and, for users with Manage Server, the DJ role, in English or Spanish.

To add music, a user searches and picks from a list of songs, each with its cover, artist, album, play count and length; the play count tells the original from covers and live versions. A pasted YouTube or Spotify link is added directly. The same search adds songs to a playlist. Results come from the bot's YouTube Music song search ([The DJ Bot → Sources](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)), fetched 250 ms after typing stops. While a new search runs, the old results dim and cannot be added, so a quick click never adds the song from the previous search. In the player's search, the song the user is about to pick is warmed: the top result once it has shown for 600 ms, and any row they point at, touch or focus, each at most once. Warming asks the bot to resolve it ahead ([The DJ Bot → How It Runs](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#how-it-runs)).

The look comes from the [DJ Player Redesign](https://claude.ai/artifact/Pbb3XsptEtoiqnzs7eWoks) canvas in Claude Design: a wide desktop board and a phone board of the player tab. The page is near-black with Inter (bundled through `@fontsource-variable/inter`), and it takes its colour from the song: the playing cover, blurred and slowly drifting, fills the background, and its dominant hue, read from a 32 px copy on a canvas (both cover hosts allow it), becomes the accent of every button, bar and label, fading over 1.4 s when the song changes. A grey or unreadable cover, or nothing playing, falls back to mint. The cover sits over its own blurred glow. Pressing a control never moves the layout: paused, the cover dims under a pause badge instead of shrinking, the label reads "Paused" and the equalizer bars settle, and the play button's icon morphs. A new song's cover fades in over the old one once it has loaded, so it never flashes blank, and its title and artist slide in; two lines of title are always reserved below the artist, so a longer or shorter title, or the bot renaming the song when it starts, leaves the controls where they were. The search, queue and suggestions sit on translucent panels; YouTube thumbnails are scaled to crop their letterbox bars. Sizes are in rem, so the whole page grows a step on windows of 1920 × 980 and again on 2400 × 1250, which keeps a 2K screen from looking sparse. From 861 px wide the page is exactly the window's height and each column scrolls on its own, so the controls never scroll away; the now-playing column grows with the window up to 42 rem with the player at its top, its cover sized by height too, so the cover, title, progress and controls always fit. On a desktop window under 761 px tall, as on a 720p screen, the cover drops beside the title instead. That column is the player alone. From 1440 px wide the search and "Suggested next" share one panel and "Up next" and "Played before" (the last songs from History, one click to queue again) another, side by side, so "Played before" takes more room as the queue empties; below that width one panel holds search, queue, suggestions, then "Played before", so the queue stays near the top, and below 860 px everything stacks, the cover beside the title. On a phone, once the controls scroll away a mini player docks at the bottom with the song, its progress, pause and skip. Playlists are tiles coloured from their ID, and History is grouped by day with each song's cover. The loading outline uses the same layout, so nothing jumps when the session arrives. The canvas is a reference, not a signed-off spec: the owner chose not to review it.

A signed-out reader is sent to the [root site](../../../home/site/explanation/site.md) and back. A Vercel function (`api/_lib/app.ts`) reads the session from the root's `/api/me` and forwards calls to the bot's API at `BOT_URL` with `INTERNAL_SECRET`, so the Discord user ID comes from the session, never the browser. Locally, `DEV_USER_ID` stands in for a session.

Until that call answers, the page shows the header and a pulsing outline of the player, so a cold function start reads as loading rather than a blank page. If the bot cannot be reached, as during each of its deploys, `/api/me` answers `botUnavailable` rather than a reader with no access, and the outline says the bot is probably restarting and retries after 1, 3, 10 and then every 30 seconds until it answers. On load the page makes one function call, `/api/me`, which returns the reader's access with the player's state and a live ticket, read from the bot at once, so the player shows and the socket opens without another round trip. Live updates arrive by a WebSocket straight to the bot, through Tailscale Funnel, opened with a ticket that names the user and lasts a minute. While the socket is open, every call except `/api/me` and the ticket goes over it instead of through the function, and the bot checks the ticket's user's access on each one ([The DJ Bot → How It Runs](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#how-it-runs)). Spanish ISPs block Cloudflare's IPs during LaLiga matches ([The pkmn Site → Decisions](../../../pkmn/site/explanation/site.md#decisions)), so the page itself is on Vercel, and when the socket fails, calls go through the function again and the page polls through it every 10 seconds, retrying the socket after 1, 3, 10 and then every 30 seconds, and says so once a retry fails.

### Feeling Live

A control changes the page as soon as it is pressed: pause, resume, skip, previous, seek, loop, autoplay and queue edits and adding a song from the player's search, apply a prediction of the bot's next state (an added song shows at once as starting or at the end of the queue, and its result is ticked; if the bot refuses, both are undone and the reason shows), which the bot's reply or broadcast then replaces; a failed command reloads the state. While nothing is being searched, "Suggested next" lists up to twenty songs from the bot's session radio, one per artist, each added like a search result; the bot computes them again only when the session's songs change ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)). The panel reloads "Suggested next" and "Played before" only when the playing song changes, not when something is queued, so a song just added stays in place with its tick instead of the rows shifting under the pointer. A reload that arrives while the pointer or focus is in the list, or within 4 s of a tap on it, waits until the reader leaves and then fades in. When a song fails to play and the bot moves on, the player says which song was skipped for eight seconds instead of letting it vanish. Queueing from History ("Queue again") or a playlist ("Play") reads "Adding…" while it runs and "Queued ✓" for a few seconds after, so a click is never silent. The tab's title names the song and artist, marked ▶ playing, ❚❚ paused or … starting, so a background tab shows what is on. On a keyboard, Space or K pauses and resumes, N skips, P goes back and / jumps to the search, except while typing in a field. Each view the bot sends carries the position at that moment, and the page counts on from when it arrived. While a song plays the progress ball moves every frame, not once a second; the arrow keys on it jump 5 s and Page Up and Down 30 s. Previous restarts the song once it has played more than 3 s, and otherwise goes back to the song before, which the bot still holds in its track list ([The DJ Bot → How It Runs](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#how-it-runs)); it is on the panel only, not a slash command. "Up next" is reordered by dragging: a mouse drags a row from anywhere but its buttons, and a touch from its grip so the list still scrolls. The row lifts and follows the pointer, the others slide aside, the numbers show where each will land, the list scrolls near its edges, and Escape cancels; the arrow buttons stay for the keyboard. While a song loads (`loading`: picked but no audio yet), the clock stops at its start and reads "Starting…", the progress bar pulses and the play button spins, so the time never runs ahead of the audio. Vercel project `enbraile-dj`, root directory `dj/panel`, deploys every push to `main` that touches `dj/panel/`, set up as the [root site's](../../../home/site/how-to/deploy-the-site.md) is; its Production variables are `ROOT_URL`, `BOT_URL` and `INTERNAL_SECRET`.

## Decisions

- **On Vercel with `dj` as a DNS-only record:** the page and its controls stay reachable during a block. Polling is not the normal path because pkmn shares Vercel Hobby's 1M calls and 4 CPU-hours a month. _(2026-11-06)_
- **Redesigned in Claude Design, without review:** the owner asked for a minimalist modern player and did not want to review the design. This replaces the first plain page, which had no Claude Design boards. It stays plain CSS rather than shadcn, since the panel has few controls. _(owner, 2026-11-07; the look the agent's choice)_
- **Colour from the playing cover, a docked mini player on phones:** the owner asked for a visually stunning panel that works from phones to 2K, allowed layout changes, and did not want to review the design before it shipped. _(owner, 2026-11-07; the look the agent's choice)_
- **Suggestions in the space under the search:** the owner noticed it sat empty on wide screens and asked for songs to play next; on narrow screens they go below the queue. _(owner, 2026-11-07; placement the agent's choice)_
- **Full-window layout:** the owner found the centred 1200 px column cramped. _(owner, 2026-11-07; the layout the agent's choice)_
- **Full-height columns, the player alone on the left, "Played before" under the queue:** the owner asked to use the vertical space, then wanted the left column to be just the player and recent songs below "Up next", where they gain room as the queue shortens. On a phone the page scrolls as before. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Twenty suggestions:** the owner asked for more than eight. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **No volume control on the panel:** each listener sets the bot's volume in Discord, and changing it on the bot restarts the stream. The bot's `/volume` command remains. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Search, then pick:** typed text lists matching songs to choose from, never adds the top hit blindly; links still add directly. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Starting needs the caller in voice:** the bot joins their channel; if a session is running, anything added goes to its queue. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Calls over the live socket, and controls that answer before the bot:** a click went browser → function → root `/api/me` → Funnel → bot and the page waited for all of it. Measured against a stand-in bot on 2026-11-07, a control now changes the page in 31–36 ms. The function stays the fallback, so a LaLiga block still leaves the controls working. _(owner asked for a more responsive player, 2026-11-07; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **Warm the likely pick:** a song picked with nothing playing waited the whole resolve, 8.7 s on `tom`. Against a stand-in with that resolve time, reading results for 3 s before adding the top one cut the wait to 6.5 s on 2026-11-07. Warming costs a yt-dlp run for songs nobody adds, so the bot caps it. _(owner asked for faster starts, 2026-11-07; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **One call to load:** `/api/me`, then the state and a ticket, were two rounds through the function, each checking the session with the root site again. `/api/me` now carries both; on 2026-11-07 the root's `/api/me` took about 0.23 s warm and the function about 0.11 s, so the saved round is roughly half a second, and two function calls a visit. _(owner asked for a more responsive site, 2026-11-07; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **Playlists:** one owner edits each, and anyone allowed can browse and play it. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
