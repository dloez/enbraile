# The DJ Panel

The web panel for the [DJ bot](../../bot/explanation/bot.md), in `dj/panel/`, for `dj.enbraile.vip`. It shows what is playing and offers the player's controls, the queue, playlists, history and, for users with Manage Server, the DJ role, in English or Spanish.

To add music, a user searches and picks from a list of songs or opens an artist, each with its cover, artist, album, play count and length; the play count tells the original from covers and live versions. A pasted YouTube or Spotify link is added directly. The same search adds songs to a playlist. Results come from the bot's YouTube Music song search ([The DJ Bot → Sources](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)), fetched 250 ms after typing stops. While a new search runs, the old results dim and cannot be added, so a quick click never adds the song from the previous search. In the player's search, the song the user is about to pick is warmed: the top result once it has shown for 600 ms, and any row they point at, touch or focus, each at most once. Warming asks the bot to resolve it ahead ([The DJ Bot → How It Runs](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#how-it-runs)).

In the player's search, up to three matching artists show as cards above the songs, as the canvas's "Artists" boards draw them. Every artist name is a link: in search results, "Suggested next", "Up next", the autoplay picks, "Played before", and under the playing song. Opening one replaces the search results and suggestions with the artist: a banner with the name and monthly audience, "Queue top 10" and "Shuffle 10", the top ten songs (then "Show all N songs", up to 100), their albums and singles, and "Fans also like". An album opens the same way, with its tracks and "Queue". Back steps through artists and albums opened from one another, and typing in the search closes the artist view. Songs from search and suggestions name their artists by ID; others open the top artist hit for their artist's name ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)).

The look comes from the [DJ Player Redesign](https://claude.ai/artifact/Pbb3XsptEtoiqnzs7eWoks) canvas in Claude Design: a wide desktop board and a phone board of the player tab, and boards for History, Autoplay, Artists, Playlists and the idle player. The page is near-black with Inter (bundled through `@fontsource-variable/inter`), and it takes its colour from the song: the playing cover, blurred and slowly drifting, fills the background, and its dominant hue, read from a 32 px copy on a canvas (both cover hosts allow it), becomes the accent of every button, bar and label, fading over 1.4 s when the song changes. A grey or unreadable cover, or nothing playing, falls back to mint. The cover casts only a tight dark shadow: a blurred glow behind it was cut off at the column's edge, since that column scrolls, and showed as a faint rectangle against the side panels. Pressing a control never moves the layout: paused, the cover dims instead of shrinking, the label reads "Paused" and the equalizer bars settle, and the play button's icon morphs. A new song's cover fades in over the old one once it has loaded, so it never flashes blank, and its title and artist slide in; the title is always one line, so a longer or shorter title, or the bot renaming the song when it starts, leaves the artist line and the controls where they were. A title too long for the line scrolls sideways at 50 px a second, pausing 0.9 s at each end, fading out at the right edge, and comes back the same way; with reduced motion it ends in an ellipsis instead. Two lines were reserved for the title until 2026-11-08, which the owner found left too much space before the artist. The search, queue and suggestions sit on translucent panels; YouTube thumbnails are scaled to crop their letterbox bars. Sizes are in rem, so the whole page grows a step on windows of 1920 × 980 and again on 2400 × 1250, which keeps a 2K screen from looking sparse. From 861 px wide the page is exactly the window's height and each column scrolls on its own, so the controls never scroll away; the now-playing column grows with the window up to 42 rem and centres the player in it together with the autoplay countdown, an error or the skipped-song note; those slide open below the controls, and while one shows the cover gives up its height, so the player glides rather than jumps and the column stays balanced, its cover sized by height too, so the cover, title, progress and controls always fit. On a desktop window under 761 px tall, as on a 720p screen, the cover drops beside the title instead. That column is the player alone. From 1440 px wide the search and "Suggested next" share one panel and "Up next" and "Played before" (the last songs from History, one click to queue again) another, side by side, so "Played before" takes more room as the queue empties; below that width one panel holds search, queue, suggestions, then "Played before", so the queue stays near the top, and below 860 px everything stacks, the cover beside the title. On a phone, once the controls scroll away a mini player docks at the bottom with the song, its progress, pause and skip. Playlists follow the canvas's "Playlists" boards (2026-11-08): the Playlists tab splits into "Yours", each card with its song count and length, and a lock if it is private (public is the default, so it goes unmarked; owner found the labels verbose, 2026-11-08), and "From the group", each with its owner; a card's cover is a 2 × 2 grid of its first four songs' covers (one cover for fewer, a tile coloured from its ID for none), and a play button on the cover queues it without opening it. "Find a playlist" filters both by name or owner, and "New playlist" opens a one-row form: the name, a Public/Private toggle whose tooltip says who can play it, the same toggle the open playlist uses, as wide as its longer word so flipping it never resizes it, Cancel and Create, all one height. An open playlist shows its cover, the name (its owner renames it in place), a lock if it is private, the owner, count and length, then Play, Shuffle and, for its owner, the Public/Private toggle and Delete. Its songs sit beside an "Add songs" search, which leaves out songs the playlist already has; an added song shows its tick, then slides out of the results, as in "Suggested next". Under that search, while nothing is typed, "Sounds like this playlist" lists up to twenty songs from a radio of the playlist, added the same way; it loads once per visit, so adding a song never reshuffles it ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions); owner asked for suggestions from what a playlist holds, 2026-11-08). Below 1100 px the search goes above the songs, and on a phone the cover and buttons centre. History lists listening sessions, newest first, in a column of at most 75 rem centred in the window (owner, 2026-11-08; full width would stretch each card's song list across a wide screen), as the canvas's "History · desktop" and "History · phone" boards draw them (owner, 2026-11-08): each card shows four covers, when it ran ("Today, 21:10–now" with a Now badge for the running one), how long, how many songs, who asked for them and how many autoplay picks, with Queue again, Save as playlist and Add to playlist. The first session opens with its songs, newest first: when it played, who asked for it or an Autoplay tag with the icon of the mode that picked it, and its own Queue again; eight show, then "Show all N songs". Add to playlist opens a menu of the user's own playlists with a search field, and says that songs a playlist already has are skipped; Save as playlist names the new playlist after the session's date and times. The rest of a card's song list loads when it opens ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)). The loading outline uses the same layout, with placeholder rows in each side panel, so nothing jumps when the session arrives. The bot's state only arrives with that first reply, so the outline draws the state the panel last showed, which the browser remembers: the crate with nothing playing, the cover and controls with a song on. A wrong guess costs one jump, as before (owner, 2026-11-08). With the bot idle, that first reply also carries the sessions and playlists the crate needs, read by the panel's function beside the state, so the crate arrives whole instead of showing its own placeholders after the outline. The canvas is a reference, not a signed-off spec: the owner chose not to review it.

A signed-out reader is sent to the [root site](../../../home/site/explanation/site.md) and back. A Vercel function (`api/_lib/app.ts`) reads the session from the root's `/api/me` and forwards calls to the bot's API at `BOT_URL` with `INTERNAL_SECRET`, so the Discord user ID comes from the session, never the browser. Locally, `DEV_USER_ID` stands in for a session.

Until that call answers, the page shows the header, with the Player, Playlists and History tabs dimmed, the language switch and a placeholder avatar so it does not change size when the reader's tabs arrive (on a phone the tabs take their own row), and a pulsing outline of the player, so a cold function start reads as loading rather than a blank page. If the bot cannot be reached, as during each of its deploys, `/api/me` answers `botUnavailable` rather than a reader with no access, and the outline says the bot is probably restarting and retries after 1, 3, 10 and then every 30 seconds until it answers. On load the page makes one function call, `/api/me`, which returns the reader's access with the player's state and a live ticket, read from the bot at once, so the player shows and the socket opens without another round trip. Live updates arrive by a WebSocket straight to the bot, through Tailscale Funnel, opened with a ticket that names the user and lasts a minute. While the socket is open, every call except `/api/me` and the ticket goes over it instead of through the function, and the bot checks the ticket's user's access on each one ([The DJ Bot → How It Runs](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#how-it-runs)). The Playlists, History and Settings tabs keep what they loaded for the rest of the visit, so switching back to one shows it at once while it reloads behind; a load already running is shared, never repeated. Once the live socket is open, the panel loads them 1.2 s after the player, and the History tab's first session's songs with them, so the first visit to a tab is usually instant too; pointing at a tab, or at a playlist, loads it as well. Spanish ISPs block Cloudflare's IPs during LaLiga matches ([The pkmn Site → Decisions](../../../pkmn/site/explanation/site.md#decisions)), so the page itself is on Vercel, and when the socket fails, calls go through the function again and the page polls through it every 10 seconds, retrying the socket after 1, 3, 10 and then every 30 seconds, and says so once a retry fails.

### Feeling Live

A control changes the page as soon as it is pressed: pause, resume, skip, previous, seek, loop, autoplay and queue edits and adding a song from the player's search, apply a prediction of the bot's next state (an added song shows at once as starting or at the end of the queue, and its result is ticked; if the bot refuses, both are undone and the reason shows), which the bot's reply or broadcast then replaces; a failed command reloads the state. While nothing is being searched, "Suggested next" lists up to twenty songs from the bot's session radio, one per artist, each added like a search result; the bot computes them again only when the session's songs change ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)). The panel reloads "Suggested next" and "Played before" only when the playing song changes, not when something is queued, so a song just added stays in place with its tick instead of the rows shifting under the pointer. A reload that arrives while the pointer or focus is in the list, or within 4 s of a tap on it, waits until the reader leaves and then fades in. A song added from "Suggested next" or "Played before" shows its tick, then slides out and its row collapses, since it is now in the queue. Until the first suggestions arrive, "Suggested next" shows placeholder rows under its label, and a small spinner beside the label while a new set loads. "Played before" shows placeholder rows the same way, so with nothing playing on a wide screen its panel holds its place instead of appearing once History loads and squeezing the search panel from two columns' width to one. When a song fails to play and the bot moves on, the player says which song was skipped for eight seconds instead of letting it vanish. Queueing from History ("Queue again") or a playlist ("Play") reads "Adding…" while it runs and "Queued ✓" for a few seconds after, so a click is never silent. The tab's title names the song and artist, marked ▶ playing, ❚❚ paused or … while starting, waiting for audio or reconnecting to the bot, so a background tab shows what is on; the play button spins in those same three states. On a keyboard, Space or K pauses and resumes, N skips, P goes back and / jumps to the search, except while typing in a field. Each view the bot sends carries the position at that moment, and the page counts on from when it arrived. While a song plays the progress ball moves every frame, not once a second; the arrow keys on it jump 5 s and Page Up and Down 30 s. Previous restarts the song once it has played more than 3 s, and otherwise goes back to the song before, which the bot still holds in its track list ([The DJ Bot → How It Runs](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#how-it-runs)); it is on the panel only, not a slash command. Under the queue, "Then autoplay" lists the bot's next three autoplay picks, faded, so what plays after the queue is never a surprise. Where a queued song has its number, a pick has the icon of the mode that chose it (a radio for Session, a compass for Explore), and where a queued song has its remove button, a pick has a swap button that replaces it with another. A pick being swapped stays in its row, dimmed and shimmering with its swap icon spinning, until the new pick fades in in the same row. A pick the bot plans again after the queue changes, or in Explore after someone joins or leaves the call, looks the same while it is replaced ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)). Autoplay is run from the queue, not the now-playing column, so turning it on never changes that column's height (owner, 2026-11-08, from the canvas's "Autoplay in the queue" boards). Under "Up next" a "Then autoplay" block holds the autoplay switch; on, it shows two cards, Session ("Like what's been playing") and Explore ("From everyone's taste"), each with its mode's icon, the chosen one in the accent ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)), then the "Autoplay stops in N min · Keep going" pill, then the picks; off, it reads "Off · the music stops when the queue ends". The now-playing column ends in one row: the listener count, with the avatars of up to four people in the call, and Disconnect ("Desconectar"), which makes the bot leave the call and clears the queue; it was labelled Stop until 2026-11-08, which read like pause. Clicking the avatars opens "Who's listening" with everyone's name. The repeat button cycles off, Session and Track: repeating the session replays every song since the bot joined, so with it on, "Up next" goes on into "Then from the top", the session's first three songs and how many more follow before it starts over, and the autoplay block turns into a dashed box saying it is on hold while repeat is on, since the bot never reaches autoplay then; repeating a track holds autoplay the same way. Each queued song and each History row names who asked for it, with their avatar, after the artist, under the playing song's title the artist line names the album too ("Artist · Album", the album dimmer), each a link that opens the artist or the album in the search panel, and under that line the now-playing column reads "Asked for by" with their avatar and name, or an Autoplay tag with the icon of the mode that picked it; the line is always there, blank for a song with no requester on record, so the controls never shift. "Up next" is reordered by dragging: a mouse drags a row from anywhere but its buttons, and a touch from its grip so the list still scrolls. The row lifts and follows the pointer, the others slide aside, the numbers show where each will land, the list scrolls near its edges, and Escape cancels; the arrow buttons stay for the keyboard. While a song loads (`loading`: picked but no audio yet), the clock stops at its start and reads "Starting…", the progress bar pulses and the play button spins, so the time never runs ahead of the audio. While the bot reports audio stalled mid-song (`stalled`), the bar pulses the same way, the clock holds its time and the label reads "Waiting for audio…". A live socket that has heard nothing for 25 s, not even the bot's 10 s beat, is treated as dropped: the panel closes it, reloads the state and reconnects, rather than counting on from a view that may be long stale. From the moment the socket is lost until a fresh state arrives, by the socket or the HTTP API, the clock holds, the bar pulses and the label reads "Reconnecting to the bot…", so a bot restart, which every deploy is, no longer shows a song playing on. Vercel project `enbraile-dj`, root directory `dj/panel`, set up as the [root site's](../../../home/site/how-to/deploy-the-site.md) is, is deployed by the `dj-panel.yml` workflow on every push to `main` that touches `dj/panel/` ([How to Deploy the Vercel Projects](../../../general/how-to/deploy-vercel-projects.md)); its Production variables are `ROOT_URL`, `BOT_URL` and `INTERNAL_SECRET`.

## Decisions

- **On Vercel with `dj` as a DNS-only record:** the page and its controls stay reachable during a block. Polling is not the normal path because pkmn shares Vercel Hobby's 1M calls and 4 CPU-hours a month. _(2026-11-06)_
- **Redesigned in Claude Design, without review:** the owner asked for a minimalist modern player and did not want to review the design. This replaces the first plain page, which had no Claude Design boards. It stays plain CSS rather than shadcn, since the panel has few controls. _(owner, 2026-11-07; the look the agent's choice)_
- **Colour from the playing cover, a docked mini player on phones:** the owner asked for a visually stunning panel that works from phones to 2K, allowed layout changes, and did not want to review the design before it shipped. _(owner, 2026-11-07; the look the agent's choice)_
- **Suggestions in the space under the search:** the owner noticed it sat empty on wide screens and asked for songs to play next; on narrow screens they go below the queue. _(owner, 2026-11-07; placement the agent's choice)_
- **Full-window layout:** the owner found the centred 1200 px column cramped. _(owner, 2026-11-07; the layout the agent's choice)_
- **Full-height columns, the player alone on the left, "Played before" under the queue:** the owner asked to use the vertical space, then wanted the left column to be just the player and recent songs below "Up next", where they gain room as the queue shortens. On a phone the page scrolls as before. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Twenty suggestions:** the owner asked for more than eight. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **No volume control on the panel:** each listener sets the bot's volume in Discord, and changing it on the bot restarts the stream. The bot's `/volume` command remains. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Search, then pick:** typed text lists matching songs to choose from, never adds the top hit blindly; links still add directly. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Starting needs the caller in voice:** the bot joins their channel; if a session is running, anything added goes to its queue. _(owner, 2026-11-07)_
- **Calls over the live socket, and controls that answer before the bot:** a click went browser → function → root `/api/me` → Funnel → bot and the page waited for all of it. Measured against a stand-in bot on 2026-11-07, a control now changes the page in 31–36 ms. The function stays the fallback, so a LaLiga block still leaves the controls working. _(owner asked for a more responsive player, 2026-11-07; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **Warm the likely pick:** a song picked with nothing playing waited the whole resolve, 8.7 s on `tom`. Against a stand-in with that resolve time, reading results for 3 s before adding the top one cut the wait to 6.5 s on 2026-11-07. Warming costs a yt-dlp run for songs nobody adds, so the bot caps it. _(owner asked for faster starts, 2026-11-07; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **One call to load:** `/api/me`, then the state and a ticket, were two rounds through the function, each checking the session with the root site again. `/api/me` now carries both; on 2026-11-07 the root's `/api/me` took about 0.23 s warm and the function about 0.11 s, so the saved round is roughly half a second, and two function calls a visit. _(owner asked for a more responsive site, 2026-11-07; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **Artists open inside the search panel, not a tab:** the queue and the player stay in view while browsing an artist, as they do while searching, so songs are added the same way and land where the user can see them. _(owner asked for artists, 2026-11-08; the placement and the canvas boards the agent's choice, without review)_
- **Added suggestions leave their list:** a song just queued no longer belongs among songs to pick, so "Suggested next" and "Played before" drop it after showing the tick. _(owner, 2026-11-08)_
- **Cover thumbnails sized to whole pixels:** the list covers were sized in rem, which on some windows came to fractional pixels, so the edge of the rounded image and its 1 px ring blurred and the placeholder's gradient showed as a faint line. Their sizes now round to whole pixels and the placeholder's background goes once an image is there. _(owner reported uneven borders, 2026-11-08)_
- **Playlists, public or private:** one owner edits each. A public playlist is listed to, and played by, anyone allowed to use the bot; a private one only to its owner, in the panel and in `/playlist`. Existing playlists stay public, as do the ones saved from History. Replaces every playlist being open to everyone (2026-11-07). _(owner, 2026-11-08; the canvas boards and their layout the agent's choice, without review)_
- **Tabs load ahead, only over the live socket:** a tab's data waited for its first click, and was fetched again on every visit. Loading ahead goes over the socket only, so it never spends the function calls pkmn shares on Vercel Hobby. _(owner asked for faster Playlists, History and Settings, 2026-11-08; the mechanism the agent's choice)_
- **A song queued from a playlist names it:** under the playing song after who asked for it, and in "Up next" after the artist, with a playlist icon. _(owner, 2026-11-08; the placement the agent's choice)_
- **The idle player offers a way to start:** with the bot in no call the player showed only an empty cover and the search. It now follows the canvas's "A · The crate" boards (owner chose A over "Quiet decks", 2026-11-08): the last finished session's covers fanned like records pulled from a crate, "Last time" with its day and times, "Pick up where you left off", who asked for its songs, its length and count, then Queue it again and Shuffle. Under those, "Start a radio for you" queues the top "For you" suggestion and turns autoplay on, so autoplay carries on from it (owner asked for an autoplay button, 2026-11-08). With no session yet, the crate shows a playlist's covers and the radio is the main button. "Or put on" shows up to three playlists, the user's own first, as on the Playlists tab: the cover opens the playlist there and its play button queues it. A dashed note says to join a voice channel, since queueing needs it and the bot then joins. Beside it, "For you" lists suggestions from the user's own requests ([The DJ Bot → Decisions](../../bot/explanation/bot.md#decisions)), and "Played before" lists the last songs from History. A first version (2026-11-08) put the cover beside "Nothing is playing" above a plain list, which the owner found dull. _(owner, 2026-11-08)_
