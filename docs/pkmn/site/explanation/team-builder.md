# Team Builder

The team builder is where a player of the group builds their teams, stored in their account on [the pkmn site](site.md): `/teams` lists every team, and `/teams/<id>` builds one from any number of snapshots, any player's ([Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)), and "Any Pokémon", which allows free members, six member cards by default and up to eight, edited in place beside the team's analysis of every member or a pick of six, each member limited by its own snapshot's items and Mini Setas, and copies it to and pastes it from Showdown; the team list's Compare puts two to four teams side by side. Every signed-in reader sees every team; only its owner changes it. It is the second half of Site v2's team building, scoped with the owner on 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23, and replaced Open Sheet, which the group used for series 1's open-sheet fights.

**Where it came from.** Open Sheet (scoped 2026-10-15) was a route of the site, `/open-sheet`, that read the JSON a player's export wrote, one browser tab per import, showed each in a PC with a summary and a bag, and built named teams in that PC, stored in the reader's browser (a Dexie database over IndexedDB). Series 1's final tournament is played open sheet ([Series 1 Rules → Final Tournament](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#final-tournament)): each player shares their Pokémon with their stats, abilities and the bag items that matter in a fight, and each builds from there. Snapshots stored on the site took over the imports, and the team builder took over the teams: on 2026-10-25 `/open-sheet`, its import tabs and Dexie went, and the site deletes the browser's old `open-sheet` database on load (`src/main.tsx`). Its PC, summary and bag became the site's PC page; its team rules, analysis and Showdown text are the team builder's, below.

**Not yet:** "Add to team" on another player's PC and a size fixed by a tournament are still to come in Site v2's work in progress.

## What It Reads

A team's sources are snapshots, each the [Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md)'s export, read in the browser as `snapshotSchema` in `shared/snapshot.ts`, whose first three keys (`pokemon`, `items`, `tms`) are the `Sheet` every piece reads. In a Pokémon every key but `species` reads as absent when it is missing or not of its type, as for an old export ([Snapshot Format → Old Exports](../reference/snapshot.md#old-exports)). Each Pokémon, item and TM is looked up by `shared/describe.ts`, which reads `@pkmn/dex`, into the shapes of `shared/mon.ts`, which loads no game data; `src/teams/dex.ts` keeps what only the builder needs (the natures, items and moves to pick from, a species' abilities, free members, and a member's Pokémon).

Names, descriptions, base stats and the type chart come from `@pkmn/dex`, Showdown's data, Gen 9. Fights are Showdown "[Gen 9] Custom Game" ([Series 1 Rules → The League](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#the-league)), and several Gen 1–5 Pokémon gained base stats in Gen 6+, so Showdown's Gen 9 data is what the fight runs; PokéAPI's cartridge data is not, and the export's IDs already follow Showdown.

Showdown's types and base stats drive the analysis, since Showdown sets a species' types from its own data whatever the save says. The summary shows the export's `types` and flags a species whose export types differ from Showdown's; the mod's types are modern (Clefairy is `FAIRY` in the mod's `types.txt`), so a mismatch should be rare. An ID `@pkmn/dex` does not know is shown by its raw ID with a marker and kept out of the analysis; `tinymushroom` is the one expected case and gets a hand-kept label, "Mini Seta (Tiny Mushroom)", without the marker.

Sprites are Showdown's Gen 5 static set, which fits the Gen 1–5 roster, shiny and female where they apply, with a "?" in place of an unknown species'; item icons come from `@pkmn/img` too.

## Stored Teams

A team is a `team` row (`api/_lib/schema.ts`, migrations `drizzle/0008_teams.sql`, `0009_team_size.sql`, `0010_any_pokemon.sql` and `0011_pools.sql`): its owner (a player; deleting the player deletes their teams), its name, its size (6 to 8, 6 by default), whether "Any Pokémon" is one of its sources (`any_pokemon`), its slots as jsonb, one per place, its pool as jsonb ([The Pool](#the-pool)), and when it was made and last changed; the database holds the size between 6 and 8 and the slots to that many, and the API sets the size from the slots it is given (`teamSlots` in `shared/team.ts` takes 6 to 8). Its snapshot sources are `team_source` rows, the team and a snapshot with a position, so a team names its snapshots explicitly, any player's, in the order they were added; a team has at least one source, a snapshot or "Any Pokémon". A slot is empty or a member (`teamMember` in `shared/team.ts`), from a snapshot or free. A snapshot's member (`ownMember`) is the snapshot it comes from (`source`), the Pokémon's index in that snapshot's `pokemon` and its species, its item, its picked ability (null while it keeps its current one) and four move IDs. A free member (`freeMember`) is a whole Showdown set marked `free`: its species, item, ability, level (1–100), nature, EVs (0–252 each) and IVs (0–31 each) and four move IDs; it names no snapshot and counts against no bag or Mini Setas, and a team holds one only while "Any Pokémon" is among its sources. A member whose index no longer holds that species, after its snapshot was replaced by a new upload, or whose source is not one of the team's, is treated as an empty slot holding no item, and is dropped on the next save. Deleting a snapshot deletes its `team_source` rows, and a team left with no snapshot and without "Any Pokémon" shows "This team's snapshot was deleted".

The routes are `api/_lib/teams.ts`, schemas in `shared/team.ts`:

- `GET /api/teams` (`?owner=<id>` for one player's) lists every team, last changed first, each with its owner, its sources (player, series, milestone), whether it allows "Any Pokémon" (`anyPokemon`) and its members' species.
- `GET /api/teams/snapshots` lists every snapshot a team may draw from, those of every series but a draft, newest series first, then by player and latest milestone first, each with its player, series and milestone.
- `GET /api/teams/<id>` answers the team with its sources' snapshots and missing-field reports, `anyPokemon`, and whether the reader may change it.
- `POST /api/teams` makes a team for the signed-in player, on the snapshots it names (`sources`, any player's, which may be none when `anyPokemon` is true) and "Any Pokémon" when `anyPokemon` is true, or else on their latest snapshot of the running series, named the lowest free "Team n", empty or with the slots it carries. It answers 403 to an admin (admins have no teams), 400 for a snapshot that does not exist or is in a draft series, 400 "A team needs a source" for no snapshot and no "Any Pokémon", and 400 when it names none and the player has no snapshot in the running series ("Upload a snapshot to the running series first").
- `PUT /api/teams/<id>` changes the name, the slots, and with the slots the size, the sources, a list replacing the team's in that order, or `anyPokemon`, refusing with 400 a change that leaves the team no source; `DELETE /api/teams/<id>` deletes the team; both answer 403 to anyone but the owner. A slot whose member's source is not one of the team's, or a free member on a team without "Any Pokémon", the slots sent or else the stored ones, is refused with 400, so a source a member still uses cannot be removed.

`npm run seed` makes Red a "Seed team" of the first three Pokémon of Red's latest Series 1 snapshot, when there is one.

Each team also counts on the series page and the profile: a trainer row's Teams tile is how many teams the trainer owns, and opens the team list filtered to them ([The Team List](#the-team-list)), and a profile banner's Teams box how many of the player's teams have a source in that series (`trainersOf` in `api/_lib/series-data.ts`, `api/_lib/profiles.ts`).

## The Team List

`/teams` (`src/pages/teams.tsx`, after `teams-list.dc.html` and `teams-list-phone.dc.html`), the header's Teams tab, lists the signed-in player's teams under "Yours" and everyone else's under "Other trainers" ("No teams yet" when a group is empty); an admin sees one list, "Every team". Each row is the owner's badge, the team's name, "<owner> · " on others' teams before its sources ("Series 1 · Post-League", or "<player>'s <milestone>" for a snapshot not the owner's, then "Any Pokémon" when the team allows it, alone on a team with no snapshot source), a strip of its slots, one per place, six to eight (each member's sprite, an empty slot dashed, shorter on a phone above six), how many slots are filled ("4/6", "8/8", green when full, amber otherwise) and when it last changed ("Changed today", "yesterday", "n days ago", else "in <month>"); the whole row opens the team. "New team" makes one on the player's latest snapshot of the running series and opens it; when it cannot, a menu under it says why and offers every snapshot to start from instead ("Or start from:"), and "Any Pokémon", so a player with no snapshot of their own can build on another's or on free members alone. **Compare**, beside it, opens [Compare](#compare).

`/teams?player=<id>`, where a trainer row's Teams tile on the series page leads, lists only that player's teams (`GET /api/teams?owner=<id>`), under "Yours" when they are the reader and otherwise under "Other trainers" (or "Every team" for an admin), with a chip above the list naming them (their badge and name; when they have no team, the name comes from `GET /api/profiles/<id>`) whose close button goes back to the whole list. The header's Teams tab always opens the whole list.

## The Team Page

`/teams/<id>` (`src/pages/team.tsx`, after `team.dc.html`, `team-member.dc.html`, `team-phone*.dc.html`) shows one team, editable by its owner and read-only to everyone else.

**Its head.** A "Teams" link back to the list; the team's name, which its owner renames in place by clicking it (1 to 60 characters, Enter or leaving the field saves, Escape cancels); on another player's team a chip "<owner>'s team" in their colour; and a chip per source, "<player>'s PC · <milestone>", each with that snapshot's Mini Setas left. For the owner a source's chip has a remove button while no member of the team comes from it and it is not the team's only source, and **+ Source** opens "Add a source", a menu of the snapshots not yet sources (`GET /api/teams/snapshots`, grouped by series when there are several, `src/teams/components/snapshot-menu.tsx`); adding or removing one writes the sources with `PUT`. The menu ends in "Any Pokémon" while the team does not allow it; then "Any Pokémon" is a chip of its own, with no Mini Setas, its remove button there while no free member is on the team and it is not the team's only source, and a snapshot's chip may likewise go when "Any Pokémon" is the other source. Adding or removing it writes `anyPokemon` with `PUT`. Beside them, for the owner, the **Members 6 · 7 · 8** switch, setting the team's size, its sizes below the last filled slot disabled, so taking a size down never drops a member; then Paste and Delete (confirmed in a dialog) for the owner, and Copy for Showdown for anyone.

**The cards.** The slots are a two-column grid filling the page's height from `lg`, three rows for six and four for seven or eight, the column scrolling (one column below `sm`). A member's card has a header row (its sprite, name, Showdown types, level and nature, the source's tag "<player>'s PC" in their colour, or a dashed "Free" on a free member, and a chevron) that opens the member's summary in the panel, or closes it back to the analysis; on a free member, for the owner, **Set**, which opens its level, nature, EVs and IVs in the panel; a button to take it out; its item and its ability (with a Mini Seta mark when the picked ability is not its current one); and its four moves, each with its type, name and power, a move it cannot learn in this save in the warning colour. On a read-only team the item, ability and moves do nothing and the take-out button is gone. An empty slot is a dashed "Add a Pokémon" for the owner and "Empty" for anyone else.

**The panel.** Beside the cards (from `lg`) the panel shows what is being looked at: the team's analysis by default ([The Analysis](#the-analysis)); the member's summary, the PC's Info · Stats · Moves over that member's set (its picked ability shown as in use, its moves as its current ones), under a link back to the analysis; or an editor, opened by the card's item, ability or a move, whose back button and whose choice return to where it was opened from, the summary or the analysis. The card being edited is outlined in cyan, and a member just put in a slot in yellow until the next action. Below `lg` the page has a Members · Analysis switch, and the summary and the editors open full screen.

- **Item:** the member's own source's bag (`<player>'s bag`), Items and Berries pockets with a search, each item with the members holding it and how many are left; the selected item's description, "Give to <member>" (disabled as "<member> holds it" or "None left") and "Take its item". Only items Showdown knows are listed, so not `tinymushroom`.
- **Item** of a free member: "Any item", every item of Showdown's Gen 9 data that is not marked non-standard, except the Mini Seta, none limited, the selected one reading "Any item: free member".
- **Ability:** every one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, base or hidden, each marked "In game" (its current one) or "1 Mini Seta", with its description; a Mini Seta ability is disabled while the member keeps its current one and none are left. A free member's are its species' abilities in Showdown's data, unmarked and free.
- **Move:** "Replace <move>" lists, when the export carries the Pokémon's learnable moves, its current moves, then the Move Relearner's with the level each is learned at, then its TMs, each move once; without them, the bag's TMs then every move Showdown gives generations 1–5; a free member, having no export and no bag, lists every move Showdown gives generations 1–5. Each row shows the type, name, category, power and Showdown's short description, a move already in another slot is disabled, a search narrows them, and "Leave the slot empty" clears it.
- **Set** of a free member: its level and nature, then one row per stat with its base, its EVs and IVs to type and the stat at its level, and the EV total, coral above 510. Each change is written as it is made.

**Seven or eight.** At a size above six the panel's analysis heads with **Analyse: All <n> · Pick 6**, open to every reader since it only changes what they look at. Pick 6 analyses six of the members, the first six filled until the reader changes them: each card carries **In** or **Out**, an Out card fades, Out is disabled while six are in, and "<n> of 6 in" turns amber below six. The switch and the pick are the reader's own, kept per team in the browser's `localStorage` (`team-pick:<id>`, `src/teams/pick.ts`); a team at six analyses every member.

**Limits.** Each source is its own stock, never pooled: a member can hold an item only while its own source's `quantity` of it is not used up by the members from that source. In the game an NPC switches an ability for one Mini Seta, to any of the Pokémon's abilities, hidden included, so each member whose picked ability is not its current one needs one, counted against its own source's `tinymushroom` quantity in that source's chip. A free member is counted against nothing.

**Adding a Pokémon.** An empty slot opens a full-screen dialog, "Add to slot <n>", with a "From" tab per source and the team's strip, the slot being filled marked: the picked source's PC box with its search and type filter, that source's Pokémon already in the team faded, and the selected one's summary beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) ending in "Put in slot <n>", or "In the team". A new member starts with its current moves in its slots, in the save's order, no item and its current ability. With "Any Pokémon" among the sources the dialog has its tab too: the pool's species (the analysis pool's, below) in a grid with a search and a type filter, and beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) the picked species' head and its set (Level 50, a neutral nature, no EVs, IVs 31, as it would join), editable before "Put in slot <n>"; a free member starts with its species' first ability, no item and no moves.

**Saving.** Every change writes the whole slots with `PUT`, one write after another in the order made, while the page keeps showing the latest slots; a failed write shows "Not saved: <reason>". A size change writes the slots, grown with empty places or cut back to the new size.

## Adding From the PC

The PC page ([Snapshots → The PC](site.md#snapshots)) stays a read-only view of a snapshot. On the signed-in player's own snapshot its summary ends in "Add to team", a menu "Add <Pokémon> to…" of the player's teams on that snapshot (each with its sprites and `<filled>/<size>`, or "Full", disabled) and "New team"; picking one adds the Pokémon to the team's first empty slot, or makes a team on that snapshot holding it, and opens the team (`src/teams/components/add-to-team.tsx`).

## The Analysis

The panel's Analysis, over every member or the pick of six ([The Team Page](#the-team-page)), has Summary, Graph, Archetype, Members, Matrices and Speed tiers, the shared module's views in one column ([The Analysis Module](#the-analysis-module)), and "Full analysis" opens the same views full screen, plus Pool, with the pool's chip and, on a Gen 9 pool, the Tera toggle ([The Pool](#the-pool)). The team reaches the shared module through its Showdown text, the one Copy for Showdown writes, ranked in the team's pool (`src/teams/components/sheet-analysis.tsx`). Summary adds the builder's own three panels (`src/teams/analysis.ts`, `src/teams/components/summary-panels.tsx`):

- **Speed order:** each member's Level 50 Speed from its IVs, as in the league's pokepastes: Level 50, real IVs, 1 EV in every stat ([Series 1 Rules → Pokepastes](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#pokepastes)). A member without IVs sorts by its IV 31 speed, then its IV 0 speed, and shows its range.
- **Defence:** for each of the 18 types, how many members are weak (above ×1), resist (below ×1) or are immune (×0), using Showdown's Gen 9 type chart over each member's Showdown types and its picked ability (its current one when none is picked). A type is a hole when more members are weak to it than resist or are immune to it.
- **Coverage:** which types the picked moves hit super-effectively on their own, and which types nothing covers.

A free member's stats are its own, at Level 50, from its IVs, EVs and nature. Members with an unknown species are left out. The abilities that change a matchup come from the site's one matchup-ability table ([The Analysis Module → Data](#data)), read in Gen 9 like the type chart. Coverage counts a member's damaging picked moves, leaving out status moves, fixed-damage moves (Seismic Toss, Sonic Boom…), one-hit KOs, moves whose damage ignores type matchups (Counter, Mirror Coat, Metal Burst, Bide, Final Gambit, Psywave, Super Fang, Endeavor) and Hidden Power, whose type depends on IVs the export does not hold. A member with no damaging move picked stands in with its STAB types.

## The Pool

Each team has its own pool, the Pokémon its analysis ranks it against and its free members are picked from: the shared module's pool rules ([The Analysis Module → Data](#data)), stored as the team's `pool` (`teamPool` in `shared/pool.ts`): the data's generation, the generation species go up to, no legendaries, no mythicals, fully evolved, and a BST cap. A new team takes the default pool of the series its first snapshot source belongs to, or, with "Any Pokémon" as its only source, of the running series, or else `DEFAULT_POOL`; the API sets it on `POST /api/teams` unless a pool is sent, as Paste sends the current team's so its new team keeps it. Series 1's default, every series' until the admin changes it in the series form ([The pkmn Site → Series](site.md#series)), is Showdown's Gen 9 data with species up to Gen 5, fully evolved, no BST cap: Añil's roster under the rules league fights run, since fights are a Gen 9 Custom Game.

Full analysis's Pool view opens with the pool bar (`src/components/pool-bar.tsx`, which the series form shares): Data and Species up to as rows of generations, the three toggles, the BST cap and the pool's size. The owner's changes write the team's pool (`PUT /api/teams/<id>` with `pool`) and re-rank the analysis at once, percentiles and all; another reader sees the bar disabled. Under it is the Pool view (`src/teams/components/pool-view.tsx`) over that pool. The chip in Full analysis's header names the pool and its size (`poolLabel` in `shared/pool.ts`).

Speed tiers is the shared module's panel (`SpeedTiers` in `src/components/analysis/charts.tsx`): the members' final Speed against the pool's median, 75th percentile and fastest at the team's level.

The Tera toggle shows in Full analysis's header while the pool's data is Gen 9. On, each member's Members card gets a Tera type picker, starting at its first type, and the defensive analysis reads each member as its Tera type alone; the toggle and the picks are the reader's, held only while the page is open.

## Compare

Compare puts two to four teams side by side, any the reader can see, theirs or other trainers' (`src/teams/components/compare-view.tsx`, after `compare.dc.html` and `compare-phone.dc.html`). On the team list, **Compare**, always on, switches the list to picking: the buttons become "<n> picked", **Cancel** and **Compare teams**, on from two; a click on a row picks it instead of opening it, ringed in cyan with a checked box, and at four picked the rest fade and cannot be picked, as does a team with no member. On a phone Compare runs the width of the page under the title. The comparison's code, which needs the game data, loads when Compare is first pressed, so the list itself loads none of it ([The pkmn Site → Performance](site.md#performance)). **Compare teams** opens the comparison full screen, titled "Compare <n> teams" with the pool's chip; closing it, or "Back to Teams" on a phone, returns to the list and ends the picking.

Each team is fetched whole (`GET /api/teams/<id>`) and read as the analysis reads it, through its Showdown text: every filled member, whatever the reader's Analyse switch on a team of seven or eight, ranked in the first picked team's pool so every team is measured on one. A team none of whose members the module can read is named above the comparison and left out of it.

**The scoreboard** (`scoreboard` in `src/analysis/compare.ts`) has one column per team, headed by its owner's badge and name, the team's name and its members' sprites, and one row per measure, each cell a figure and a note: Members ("<n> empty of <size>" while the team has empty places), Average Speed and Fastest (with its name), at the members' real stats; Average bulk, the mean of HP × (Def + SpD) / 2, in hundreds; Types hitting 3+ ×2 and Types none resists (with the types); Types hit ×2 ("of 18"); Coverage holes, the types nothing hits neutrally or better; Hazards · removal (how many set hazards and how many remove them); Speed control (Thunder Wave, Tailwind, Trick Room, Icy Wind, priority moves and the like); and Recovery. The best figure of a row is green on a green ground, none on a tie. On a phone the teams are numbered in a list above it, and each measure is a line of its own over one cell per team.

**Head to head** is a damage calc between any two of the compared teams' members: the Attacker and the Defender each pick a team and a Pokémon, shown with its sprite and its stats, and the attacker's damaging moves fill the Move picker. It shows the hit as a range of the defender's HP, a bar, its verdict ("Guaranteed OHKO", "Possible 3HKO", "No effect") and the move's power, multiplier, STAB and the attacker's level; the range turns amber from half the HP and coral at all of it. It is the module's `calcHit`: real sets at their level, with Choice Band and Specs, Life Orb, Expert Belt, Assault Vest, Huge Power, Multiscale and the like, a variable-power or multi-hit move counting as one hit. **⇄ Swap sides** swaps the two.

## Showdown Text

**Copy.** "Copy for Showdown" writes the team with `src/teams/showdown.ts` in the layout of Showdown's own export: `Nickname (Species) (M) @ Item`, with the nickname only when it differs from Showdown's species name, the export's gender whenever it is `M` or `F`, and the item only when one is held; then `Ability:` (the picked one, else the current one, left out when the export has none), `Level: 50`, `Shiny: Yes` when shiny, the league's `EVs: 1 HP / 1 Atk / 1 Def / 1 SpA / 1 SpD / 1 Spe`, `<Nature> Nature` when the export has one, `IVs:` with each IV below 31, and one `- Move` line per filled move slot. A free member writes its own set as Showdown does: its species, `@ Item`, `Ability:`, `Level:` unless it is 100, `EVs:` with each EV above 0, `<Nature> Nature`, `IVs:` and its moves. Members are separated by a blank line; empty slots, members whose index no longer holds their species and species Showdown does not know are left out. The button is disabled when nothing would be copied, and reads "Copied", or "Copy failed" when the browser refuses the clipboard, for a moment after a click.

**Paste.** The owner's Paste opens "Paste from Showdown", which the site's shared parser (`src/analysis/paste.ts`) reads and `src/teams/showdown.ts` matches to the team's sources, making a new team on the same sources and pool (`POST /api/teams` with the slots) and opening it. Each set is matched to a Pokémon of the sources, taken in their order, by its species, preferring one whose nickname matches and skipping those already placed. Its item is given when that Pokémon's own snapshot still has one left, its ability is picked when it is one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, and its moves fill the four slots whether or not the Pokémon can learn them; a typed Hidden Power reads as Hidden Power. Gender, shiny, nature, level, EVs and IVs are ignored, since the snapshot holds those; `===` team headers, a move's `/` alternatives after the first and any set past the eighth are skipped, and the new team's size is six or, for seven or eight sets matched, that many. The shared parser reads up to eight sets. When "Any Pokémon" is among the sources, a set no snapshot can back, its species in no source or with every copy already placed, comes in as a free member when its species is in the analysis pool, keeping its item when Showdown knows it (a non-standard one too), its ability when its species can have it (else its first), its level, nature, EVs (each held to 252, the total to 510) and IVs, and its known moves; the new team also allows "Any Pokémon". The dialog lists the sets that came in as free members. Whatever the sources cannot back is left out and listed in the dialog with its reason: a species in no source or with every copy already placed (or, with "Any Pokémon", a species not in the pool), an item none of is left in its bag or Showdown does not know, an ability the Pokémon cannot have, a nature or a move Showdown does not know. When nothing matches, no team is made. When every set came in from a snapshot whole, the new team opens at once; otherwise the dialog shows what came in free and what was left out, and its "Open the team", or closing it, opens the new team.

## The Analysis Module

PokeGraph (`~/pokegraph`, a local vanilla-JS app with a Python proxy) is ported to TypeScript as one analysis module the site shares, scoped with the owner on 2026-10-17: the synergy, weakness, resistance and coverage graph; archetype classification with its fingerprint radar and bulk × offense map; the teambuilding checklist; member cards with real stats, pool percentiles and roles; the defensive and offensive matrices, as type multipliers or weighted by the pool; and the pool analyzer. The team builder's [Analysis](#the-analysis), its [Pool](#the-pool) and [Compare](#compare) read it. Until 2026-10-25 it also served Team Analysis, a page of its own where a reader pasted a Showdown team, with its pasted team, compare slots and pool kept in the browser; Compare over stored teams replaced it, and the site deletes that tool's old drafts from the browser on load (`src/main.tsx`). pokepast.es links, PokeAPI, PokeGraph's `tools/build_data.py` and its Python server stayed out.

The module is `pkmn/site/src/analysis/`, with two of its files in `shared/` since the second performance pass (2026-10-25), so the PC route can read them on the server: `shared/dex.ts` (generations, availability; the 18 types themselves are in `shared/mon.ts`), `shared/chart.ts` (type matchups, matchup abilities, move special cases), `paste.ts` (the Showdown parser), `member.ts` (real stats, moves, move tags), `pool.ts` (the pool, its statistics and legality), `stats.ts` (the pool's distributions, histograms, type shares and stat correlations), `team.ts` (defence, synergy, offence, weighted values, archetype, roles, checklist), `compare.ts` (Compare's scoreboard and damage calc) and `sprite.ts` (members' sprite URLs). Its panels are shared components ([Site Design System](../reference/design-system.md)). The team builder's `src/teams/analysis.ts` keeps only what reads its own members (speed order, defensive chart, coverage), on the shared chart and types: those functions take the builder's members and slots, and moving them into the module would make it depend on the builder.

A team reaches the module through its Showdown text, the one Copy for Showdown writes, read by the shared parser as PokeGraph's `parser.js` does (header, ability, level, Tera type, EVs, IVs, nature, moves; level 100 when no `Level:` line) in the pool's generation, so members with an unknown species are left out, with the export's real IVs and the league's 1 EV in every stat, which changes no Level 50 stat.

### Data

Game data is `@pkmn/dex`, through `Dex.forGen(n)` for the pool's generation, in place of PokeGraph's PokeAPI data and its generated `pokedex.json` and `typecharts.json`. Checked 2026-10-17 against `@pkmn/dex` 0.10.11: `forGen` returns past typings and stats (Gen 5 Clefable is Normal with 85 SpA; Gen 9's is Fairy with 95), Gen 1's single Special stat, the type-based physical/special split (Gen 3 Crunch is Special), species `tags` for Sub-Legendary, Restricted Legendary and Mythical, and `nfe` for not fully evolved. It also keeps later content in earlier generations (Moonblast and the Fairy type exist in `forGen(5)`), so moves, abilities and types are filtered by their `gen`, as PokeGraph's `notInGen` did.

**Matchup abilities.** One table serves the whole site, PokeGraph's: Levitate and Earth Eater (Ground), Flash Fire and Well-Baked Body (Fire), Water Absorb and Storm Drain (Water), Volt Absorb, Lightning Rod and Motor Drive (Electric), Sap Sipper (Grass) as immunities; Dry Skin (immune to Water, ×1.25 from Fire); Thick Fat (Fire and Ice), Heatproof and Water Bubble (Fire) and Purifying Salt (Ghost) as halving; Fluffy (×2 from Fire); and Wonder Guard. An ability counts only from Gen 3 and in a generation that has it. The team builder reads it in Gen 9, which changes nothing there: the abilities it adds are Gen 7–9 ones, and the mod only rolls the 164 Gen 1–5 abilities ([Save Export](save-export.md)).

**The pool.** A pool is the species and formes Showdown lists for its generation that it has not marked as later content (`isNonstandard` "Future"; "Past" ones stay, since a Gen 9 Custom Game fields them), leaving out battle-only formes, Megas, Primals, Gigantamax and Totem formes, any forme whose types and base stats are its base species' (Gastrodon-East, the Deerling seasons), and any forme with its base species' stats that a held item makes (Arceus's and Silvally's types, Genesect's Drives, Ogerpon's masks). A pool can also hold species to an earlier generation than its data (`speciesGen`, the pool bar's Species up to row). Legendary is Showdown's Restricted Legendary or Sub-Legendary tag, mythical its Mythical tag, not fully evolved its generation's `nfe`. Moves follow the same line: a "Future" move is ignored, as PokeGraph's `notInGen` did, and a "Past" one counts. The pools come out a little larger than PokeGraph's PokeAPI-built ones (fully evolved, any BST: 647 against 601 in Gen 9, 363 against 346 in Gen 5), and PokeGraph's four sample teams got the same archetype ranking, within 0.01, in both generations, measured on 2026-10-17 against PokeGraph run on its own cached data.

### The Graph

The [Team Analysis Screens](https://claude.ai/artifact/4tyDvGNZXHqSRAvABaszfH) design, signed off by the owner on 2026-10-17, set the module's panels; the Site v2 Screens redrew them in the team builder ([The pkmn Site → Look](site.md#look)). Cytoscape.js, pinned, draws the team graph: dragging, its layouts and PNG export. The radar, archetype map, histograms and bars are the site's own SVG components, coloured from the theme tokens. The graph's PNG export holds the graph without sprites: it swaps them for the members' type-coloured rings and names, then puts them back. On screen the sprites load without a CORS request.
