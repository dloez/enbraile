# Team Builder

The team builder is where a player of the group builds their teams, stored in their account on [the pkmn site](site.md): `/teams` lists every team, and `/teams/<id>` builds one from any number of snapshots, any player's ([Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)), and "Any Pokémon", which allows free members, six member cards by default and up to eight, edited in place beside the team's analysis of every member or a pick of six, each member limited by its own snapshot's items and Mini Setas, and copies it to and pastes it from Showdown. Every signed-in reader sees every team; only its owner changes it. It is the second half of Site v2's team building, scoped with the owner on 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23, and replaced Open Sheet, which the group used for series 1's open-sheet fights.

**Where it came from.** Open Sheet (scoped 2026-10-15) was a route of the site, `/open-sheet`, that read the JSON a player's export wrote, one browser tab per import, showed each in a PC with a summary and a bag, and built named teams in that PC, stored in the reader's browser (a Dexie database over IndexedDB). Series 1's final tournament is played open sheet ([Series 1 Rules → Final Tournament](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#final-tournament)): each player shares their Pokémon with their stats, abilities and the bag items that matter in a fight, and each builds from there. Snapshots stored on the site took over the imports, and the team builder took over the teams: on 2026-10-25 `/open-sheet`, its import tabs and Dexie went, and the site deletes the browser's old `open-sheet` database on load (`src/main.tsx`). Its PC, summary and bag became the site's PC page; its team rules, analysis and Showdown text are the team builder's, below.

**Not yet:** "Add to team" on another player's PC, a size fixed by a tournament, and Compare are still to come in Site v2's work in progress.

## What It Reads

A team's sources are snapshots, each the [Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md)'s export, read in the browser as `snapshotSchema` in `shared/snapshot.ts`, whose first three keys (`pokemon`, `items`, `tms`) are the `Sheet` every piece reads. In a Pokémon every key but `species` reads as absent when it is missing or not of its type, as for an old export ([Snapshot Format → Old Exports](../reference/snapshot.md#old-exports)). Each Pokémon, item and TM is looked up by `src/teams/dex.ts`.

Names, descriptions, base stats and the type chart come from `@pkmn/dex`, Showdown's data, Gen 9. Fights are Showdown "[Gen 9] Custom Game" ([Series 1 Rules → The League](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#the-league)), and several Gen 1–5 Pokémon gained base stats in Gen 6+, so Showdown's Gen 9 data is what the fight runs; PokéAPI's cartridge data is not, and the export's IDs already follow Showdown.

Showdown's types and base stats drive the analysis, since Showdown sets a species' types from its own data whatever the save says. The summary shows the export's `types` and flags a species whose export types differ from Showdown's; the mod's types are modern (Clefairy is `FAIRY` in the mod's `types.txt`), so a mismatch should be rare. An ID `@pkmn/dex` does not know is shown by its raw ID with a marker and kept out of the analysis; `tinymushroom` is the one expected case and gets a hand-kept label, "Mini Seta (Tiny Mushroom)", without the marker.

Sprites are Showdown's Gen 5 static set, which fits the Gen 1–5 roster, shiny and female where they apply, with a "?" in place of an unknown species'; item icons come from `@pkmn/img` too.

## Stored Teams

A team is a `team` row (`api/_lib/schema.ts`, migrations `drizzle/0008_teams.sql`, `0009_team_size.sql`, `0010_any_pokemon.sql` and `0011_pools.sql`): its owner (a player; deleting the player deletes their teams), its name, its size (6 to 8, 6 by default), whether "Any Pokémon" is one of its sources (`any_pokemon`), its slots as jsonb, one per place, its pool as jsonb ([The Pool](#the-pool)), and when it was made and last changed; the database holds the size between 6 and 8 and the slots to that many, and the API sets the size from the slots it is given (`teamSlots` in `shared/team.ts` takes 6 to 8). Its snapshot sources are `team_source` rows, the team and a snapshot with a position, so a team names its snapshots explicitly, any player's, in the order they were added; a team has at least one source, a snapshot or "Any Pokémon". A slot is empty or a member (`teamMember` in `shared/team.ts`), from a snapshot or free. A snapshot's member (`ownMember`) is the snapshot it comes from (`source`), the Pokémon's index in that snapshot's `pokemon` and its species, its item, its picked ability (null while it keeps its current one) and four move IDs. A free member (`freeMember`) is a whole Showdown set marked `free`: its species, item, ability, level (1–100), nature, EVs (0–252 each) and IVs (0–31 each) and four move IDs; it names no snapshot and counts against no bag or Mini Setas, and a team holds one only while "Any Pokémon" is among its sources. A member whose index no longer holds that species, after its snapshot was replaced by a new upload, or whose source is not one of the team's, is treated as an empty slot holding no item, and is dropped on the next save. Deleting a snapshot deletes its `team_source` rows, and a team left with no snapshot and without "Any Pokémon" shows "This team's snapshot was deleted".

The routes are `api/_lib/teams.ts`, schemas in `shared/team.ts`:

- `GET /api/teams` (`?owner=<id>` for one player's) lists every team, last changed first, each with its owner, its sources (player, series, milestone), whether it allows "Any Pokémon" (`anyPokemon`) and its members' species.
- `GET /api/teams/snapshots` lists every snapshot a team may draw from, those of every series but a draft, newest series first, then by player and latest milestone first, each with its player, series and milestone.
- `GET /api/teams/<id>` answers the team with its sources' snapshots and missing-field reports, `anyPokemon`, and whether the reader may change it.
- `POST /api/teams` makes a team for the signed-in player, on the snapshots it names (`sources`, any player's, which may be none when `anyPokemon` is true) and "Any Pokémon" when `anyPokemon` is true, or else on their latest snapshot of the running series, named the lowest free "Team n", empty or with the slots it carries. It answers 403 to an admin (admins have no teams), 400 for a snapshot that does not exist or is in a draft series, 400 "A team needs a source" for no snapshot and no "Any Pokémon", and 400 when it names none and the player has no snapshot in the running series ("Upload a snapshot to the running series first").
- `PUT /api/teams/<id>` changes the name, the slots, and with the slots the size, the sources, a list replacing the team's in that order, or `anyPokemon`, refusing with 400 a change that leaves the team no source; `DELETE /api/teams/<id>` deletes the team; both answer 403 to anyone but the owner. A slot whose member's source is not one of the team's, or a free member on a team without "Any Pokémon", the slots sent or else the stored ones, is refused with 400, so a source a member still uses cannot be removed.

`npm run seed` makes Red a "Seed team" of the first three Pokémon of Red's latest Series 1 snapshot, when there is one.

Each team also counts on the series page and the profile: a trainer row's Teams tile is how many teams the trainer owns, and opens the team list filtered to them ([The Team List](#the-team-list)), and a profile banner's Teams box how many of the player's teams have a source in that series (`trainersOf` in `api/_lib/series-data.ts`, `api/_lib/profiles.ts`).

## The Team List

`/teams` (`src/pages/teams.tsx`, after `teams-list.dc.html` and `teams-list-phone.dc.html`), the header's Teams tab, lists the signed-in player's teams under "Yours" and everyone else's under "Other trainers" ("No teams yet" when a group is empty); an admin sees one list, "Every team". Each row is the owner's badge, the team's name, "<owner> · " on others' teams before its sources ("Series 1 · Post-League", or "<player>'s <milestone>" for a snapshot not the owner's, then "Any Pokémon" when the team allows it, alone on a team with no snapshot source), a strip of its slots, one per place, six to eight (each member's sprite, an empty slot dashed, shorter on a phone above six), how many slots are filled ("4/6", "8/8", green when full, amber otherwise) and when it last changed ("Changed today", "yesterday", "n days ago", else "in <month>"); the whole row opens the team. "New team" makes one on the player's latest snapshot of the running series and opens it; when it cannot, a menu under it says why and offers every snapshot to start from instead ("Or start from:"), and "Any Pokémon", so a player with no snapshot of their own can build on another's or on free members alone.

`/teams?player=<id>`, where a trainer row's Teams tile on the series page leads, lists only that player's teams (`GET /api/teams?owner=<id>`), under "Yours" when they are the reader and otherwise under "Other trainers" (or "Every team" for an admin), with a chip above the list naming them (their badge and name; when they have no team, the name comes from `GET /api/profiles/<id>`) whose close button goes back to the whole list. The header's Teams tab always opens the whole list.

## The Team Page

`/teams/<id>` (`src/pages/team.tsx`, after `team.dc.html`, `team-member.dc.html`, `team-phone*.dc.html`) shows one team, editable by its owner and read-only to everyone else.

**Its head.** A "Teams" link back to the list; the team's name, which its owner renames in place by clicking it (1 to 60 characters, Enter or leaving the field saves, Escape cancels); on another player's team a chip "<owner>'s team" in their colour; and a chip per source, "<player>'s PC · <milestone>", each with that snapshot's Mini Setas left. For the owner a source's chip has a remove button while no member of the team comes from it and it is not the team's only source, and **+ Source** opens "Add a source", a menu of the snapshots not yet sources (`GET /api/teams/snapshots`, grouped by series when there are several, `src/teams/components/snapshot-menu.tsx`); adding or removing one writes the sources with `PUT`. The menu ends in "Any Pokémon" while the team does not allow it; then "Any Pokémon" is a chip of its own, with no Mini Setas, its remove button there while no free member is on the team and it is not the team's only source, and a snapshot's chip may likewise go when "Any Pokémon" is the other source. Adding or removing it writes `anyPokemon` with `PUT`. Beside them, for the owner, the **Members 6 · 7 · 8** switch, setting the team's size, its sizes below the last filled slot disabled, so taking a size down never drops a member; then Paste and Delete (confirmed in a dialog) for the owner, and Copy for Showdown for anyone.

**The cards.** The slots are a two-column grid filling the page's height from `lg`, three rows for six and four for seven or eight, the column scrolling (one column below `sm`). A member's card has a header row (its sprite, name, Showdown types, level and nature, the source's tag "<player>'s PC" in their colour, or a dashed "Free" on a free member, and a chevron) that opens the member's summary in the panel, or closes it back to the analysis; on a free member, for the owner, **Set**, which opens its level, nature, EVs and IVs in the panel; a button to take it out; its item and its ability (with a Mini Seta mark when the picked ability is not its current one); and its four moves, each with its type, name and power, a move it cannot learn in this save in the warning colour. On a read-only team the item, ability and moves do nothing and the take-out button is gone. An empty slot is a dashed "Add a Pokémon" for the owner and "Empty" for anyone else.

**The panel.** Beside the cards (from `lg`) the panel shows what is being looked at: the team's analysis by default ([The Analysis](#the-analysis)); the member's summary, the PC's Info · Stats · Moves over that member's set (its picked ability shown as in use, its moves as its current ones), under a link back to the analysis; or an editor, opened by the card's item, ability or a move, whose back button and whose choice return to where it was opened from, the summary or the analysis. The card being edited is outlined in cyan, and a member just put in a slot in yellow until the next action. Below `lg` the page has a Members · Analysis switch, and the summary and the editors open full screen.

- **Item:** the member's own source's bag (`<player>'s bag`), Items and Berries pockets with a search, each item with the members holding it and how many are left; the selected item's description, "Give to <member>" (disabled as "<member> holds it" or "None left") and "Take its item". Only items Showdown knows are listed, so not `tinymushroom`.
- **Item** of a free member: "Any item", every item of Showdown's Gen 9 data that is not marked non-standard, except the Mini Seta, none limited, the selected one reading "Any item: free member".
- **Ability:** every one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, base or hidden, each marked "In game" (its current one) or "1 Mini Seta", with its description; a Mini Seta ability is disabled while the member keeps its current one and none are left. A free member's are its species' abilities in Showdown's data, unmarked and free.
- **Move:** "Replace <move>" lists, when the export carries the Pokémon's learnable moves, its current moves, then the Move Relearner's with the level each is learned at, then its TMs, each move once; without them, the bag's TMs then every move Showdown gives generations 1–5; a free member, having no export and no bag, lists every move Showdown gives generations 1–5. Each row shows the type, name, category, power and Showdown's short description, a move already in another slot is disabled, a search narrows them, and "Leave the slot empty" clears it.
- **Set** of a free member: its level and nature, then one row per stat with its base, its EVs and IVs to type and the stat at its level, and the EV total, coral above 510. Each change is written as it is made.

**Seven or eight.** At a size above six the panel's analysis heads with **Analyse: All <n> · Pick 6**, open to every reader since it only changes what they look at. Pick 6 analyses six of the members, the first six filled until the reader changes them: each card carries **In** or **Out**, an Out card fades, Out is disabled while six are in, and "<n> of 6 in" turns amber below six. The switch and the pick are the reader's own, kept per team in the browser's `localStorage` (`team-pick:<id>`, `src/teams/pick.ts`); a team at six analyses every member.

**Limits.** Each source is its own stock, never pooled: a member can hold an item only while its own source's `quantity` of it is not used up by the members from that source. In the game an NPC switches an ability for one Mini Seta, to any of the Pokémon's abilities, hidden included, so each member whose picked ability is not its current one needs one, counted against its own source's `tinymushroom` quantity in that source's chip. A free member is counted against nothing.

**Adding a Pokémon.** An empty slot opens a full-screen dialog, "Add to slot <n>", with a "From" tab per source and the team's strip, the slot being filled marked: the picked source's PC box with its search and type filter, that source's Pokémon already in the team faded, and the selected one's summary beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) ending in "Put in slot <n>", or "In the team". A new member starts with its current moves in its slots, in the save's order, no item and its current ability. With "Any Pokémon" among the sources the dialog has its tab too: the pool's species (the analysis pool's, below) in a grid with a search and a type filter, and beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) the picked species' head and its set (Level 50, a neutral nature, no EVs, IVs 31, as it would join), editable before "Put in slot <n>"; a free member starts with its species' first ability, no item and no moves.

**Saving.** Every change writes the whole slots with `PUT`, one write after another in the order made, while the page keeps showing the latest slots; a failed write shows "Not saved: <reason>". A size change writes the slots, grown with empty places or cut back to the new size.

## Adding From the PC

The PC page ([Snapshots → The PC](site.md#snapshots)) stays a read-only view of a snapshot. On the signed-in player's own snapshot its summary ends in "Add to team", a menu "Add <Pokémon> to…" of the player's teams on that snapshot (each with its sprites and `<filled>/<size>`, or "Full", disabled) and "New team"; picking one adds the Pokémon to the team's first empty slot, or makes a team on that snapshot holding it, and opens the team (`src/teams/components/add-to-team.tsx`).

## The Analysis

The panel's Analysis, over every member or the pick of six ([The Team Page](#the-team-page)), has Summary, Graph, Archetype, Members, Matrices and Speed tiers, Team Analysis's views in one column ([Team Analysis](team-analysis.md)), and "Full analysis" opens the same views full screen, plus Pool, with the pool's chip and, on a Gen 9 pool, the Tera toggle ([The Pool](#the-pool)). The team reaches the shared module through its Showdown text, the one Copy for Showdown writes, ranked in the team's pool (`src/teams/components/sheet-analysis.tsx`). Summary adds the builder's own three panels (`src/teams/analysis.ts`, `src/teams/components/team-analysis.tsx`):

- **Speed order:** each member's Level 50 Speed from its IVs, as in the league's pokepastes: Level 50, real IVs, 1 EV in every stat ([Series 1 Rules → Pokepastes](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#pokepastes)). A member without IVs sorts by its IV 31 speed, then its IV 0 speed, and shows its range.
- **Defence:** for each of the 18 types, how many members are weak (above ×1), resist (below ×1) or are immune (×0), using Showdown's Gen 9 type chart over each member's Showdown types and its picked ability (its current one when none is picked). A type is a hole when more members are weak to it than resist or are immune to it.
- **Coverage:** which types the picked moves hit super-effectively on their own, and which types nothing covers.

A free member's stats are its own, at Level 50, from its IVs, EVs and nature. Members with an unknown species are left out. The abilities that change a matchup come from the site's one matchup-ability table ([Team Analysis → Data](team-analysis.md#data)), read in Gen 9 like the type chart. Coverage counts a member's damaging picked moves, leaving out status moves, fixed-damage moves (Seismic Toss, Sonic Boom…), one-hit KOs, moves whose damage ignores type matchups (Counter, Mirror Coat, Metal Burst, Bide, Final Gambit, Psywave, Super Fang, Endeavor) and Hidden Power, whose type depends on IVs the export does not hold. A member with no damaging move picked stands in with its STAB types.

## The Pool

Each team has its own pool, the Pokémon its analysis ranks it against and its free members are picked from: Team Analysis's pool rules ([Team Analysis → The pool](team-analysis.md#data)), stored as the team's `pool` (`teamPool` in `shared/pool.ts`): the data's generation, the generation species go up to, no legendaries, no mythicals, fully evolved, and a BST cap. A new team takes the default pool of the series its first snapshot source belongs to, or, with "Any Pokémon" as its only source, of the running series, or else `DEFAULT_POOL`; the API sets it on `POST /api/teams` unless a pool is sent, as Paste sends the current team's so its new team keeps it. Series 1's default, every series' until the admin changes it in the series form ([The pkmn Site → Series](site.md#series)), is Showdown's Gen 9 data with species up to Gen 5, fully evolved, no BST cap: Añil's roster under the rules league fights run, since fights are a Gen 9 Custom Game.

Full analysis's Pool view opens with the pool bar (`src/team-analysis/components/pool-bar.tsx`): Data and Species up to as rows of generations, the three toggles, the BST cap and the pool's size. The owner's changes write the team's pool (`PUT /api/teams/<id>` with `pool`) and re-rank the analysis at once, percentiles and all; another reader sees the bar disabled. Under it is Team Analysis's Pool view (`pool-view.tsx`) over that pool. The chip in Full analysis's header names the pool and its size (`poolLabel` in `shared/pool.ts`).

Speed tiers is Team Analysis's panel (`SpeedTiers` in `src/components/analysis/charts.tsx`): the members' final Speed against the pool's median, 75th percentile and fastest at the team's level.

The Tera toggle shows in Full analysis's header while the pool's data is Gen 9. On, each member's Members card gets a Tera type picker, starting at its first type, and the defensive analysis reads each member as its Tera type alone, as Team Analysis does; the toggle and the picks are the reader's, held only while the page is open.

## Showdown Text

**Copy.** "Copy for Showdown" writes the team with `src/teams/showdown.ts` in the layout of Showdown's own export: `Nickname (Species) (M) @ Item`, with the nickname only when it differs from Showdown's species name, the export's gender whenever it is `M` or `F`, and the item only when one is held; then `Ability:` (the picked one, else the current one, left out when the export has none), `Level: 50`, `Shiny: Yes` when shiny, the league's `EVs: 1 HP / 1 Atk / 1 Def / 1 SpA / 1 SpD / 1 Spe`, `<Nature> Nature` when the export has one, `IVs:` with each IV below 31, and one `- Move` line per filled move slot. A free member writes its own set as Showdown does: its species, `@ Item`, `Ability:`, `Level:` unless it is 100, `EVs:` with each EV above 0, `<Nature> Nature`, `IVs:` and its moves. Members are separated by a blank line; empty slots, members whose index no longer holds their species and species Showdown does not know are left out. The button is disabled when nothing would be copied, and reads "Copied", or "Copy failed" when the browser refuses the clipboard, for a moment after a click.

**Paste.** The owner's Paste opens "Paste from Showdown", which the site's shared parser (`src/analysis/paste.ts`) reads and `src/teams/showdown.ts` matches to the team's sources, making a new team on the same sources and pool (`POST /api/teams` with the slots) and opening it. Each set is matched to a Pokémon of the sources, taken in their order, by its species, preferring one whose nickname matches and skipping those already placed. Its item is given when that Pokémon's own snapshot still has one left, its ability is picked when it is one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, and its moves fill the four slots whether or not the Pokémon can learn them; a typed Hidden Power reads as Hidden Power. Gender, shiny, nature, level, EVs and IVs are ignored, since the snapshot holds those; `===` team headers, a move's `/` alternatives after the first and any set past the eighth are skipped, and the new team's size is six or, for seven or eight sets matched, that many. The shared parser reads six sets for Team Analysis and up to eight for the team builder. When "Any Pokémon" is among the sources, a set no snapshot can back, its species in no source or with every copy already placed, comes in as a free member when its species is in the analysis pool, keeping its item when Showdown knows it (a non-standard one too), its ability when its species can have it (else its first), its level, nature, EVs (each held to 252, the total to 510) and IVs, and its known moves; the new team also allows "Any Pokémon". The dialog lists the sets that came in as free members. Whatever the sources cannot back is left out and listed in the dialog with its reason: a species in no source or with every copy already placed (or, with "Any Pokémon", a species not in the pool), an item none of is left in its bag or Showdown does not know, an ability the Pokémon cannot have, a nature or a move Showdown does not know. When nothing matches, no team is made. When every set came in from a snapshot whole, the new team opens at once; otherwise the dialog shows what came in free and what was left out, and its "Open the team", or closing it, opens the new team.

## Decisions

- **`@pkmn/dex`'s Gen 9 data, bundled, for names, stats and the type chart; Showdown's sprite server through `@pkmn/img` for sprites.** **Why:** the owner asked for a good source of base stats and sprites; Showdown's Gen 9 data is what the fight runs. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Showdown's types drive the analysis,** with a flag on an export-type mismatch. **Why:** forced by where the fight runs. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
- **An unknown ID is shown raw with a marker and kept out of the analysis;** `tinymushroom` gets a hand-kept label. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
- **Item quantities limit a team.** _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Any of a member's `abilities` can be picked, counted against the snapshot's Mini Setas.** **Why:** an NPC switches an ability for one Mini Seta, to any of the Pokémon's abilities. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **For an export with moves, a member's moves are picked from its current, relearner and TM moves, its current moves fill a new member's slots, and a move it cannot learn is shown in the warning colour.** **Why:** a move kept from a previous evolution is in no other list, and the team should start from what the Pokémon actually knows. _(owner, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-17)_
- **The team shows Level 50 stats, a defensive chart and offensive coverage, beside the shared analysis.** **Why:** the owner's choice; with the export's IVs they are exact for the league's pokepastes. _(owner, 2026-10-15; shared analysis 2026-10-17)_
- **The analysis rules** (the weak, resist, immune and hole thresholds, the speed order, the moves coverage leaves out, STAB stand-ins): build choices made while building Open Sheet's team page; the matchup abilities come from the site's shared table ([Team Analysis → Data](team-analysis.md#data)). _(settled 2026-10-16; shared table 2026-10-17)_
- **A team copies as Showdown text in Showdown's export layout,** Level 50, with its real IVs and the league's 1 EV in every stat. **Why:** the owner; it is the league's paste format and reveals nothing. _(owner, 2026-10-15; layout 2026-10-16; EVs 2026-10-17; IVs 2026-10-21)_
- **A pasted Showdown team becomes a new team,** matched by species and nickname, keeping only what the snapshot backs and listing the rest. **Why:** the owner asked for it, so a team built in Showdown's teambuilder can be checked against the stock. _(owner, 2026-10-17)_
- **Teams are stored in the account, every team seen by everyone and changed only by its owner; a team lists its sources, and the PC is read-only with "Add to team" its one link to teams.** **Why:** the owner: the PC records what the save holds and the team is what is edited, so each page does one job. _(owner, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23)_
- **Open Sheet's imports, tabs and Dexie database go, and the browser's old database is deleted on load.** **Why:** greenfield; series 1's PCs come back as uploads and teams by Showdown paste. _(owner, 2026-10-22)_
- **The team builder's build choices:** sources as `team_source` rows rather than ids in the team's jsonb, so a deleted snapshot takes its rows with it; each member names its source snapshot, so several sources need no change of shape; the PC's "Add to team" shown only on the player's own PC until its entry settles what it does on another's; a team with no snapshot left kept and shown as such; whole-slot writes in order from the latest slots in memory, as Open Sheet wrote; the trainer row's Teams tile counting every team the trainer owns and the profile's Teams box those with a source in that series, as the entry worded them; the Teams tile's filter a search param on `/teams` reading the existing `?owner=` route, with the Yours or Other trainers heading kept over the filtered list and the chip's close button a link to `/teams`; the team's name renamed in place and Delete beside Copy, which the board leaves out but a stored team needs; a card's name kept on a row of its own, its level and nature beside the types, so a nickname is not cut short; the panel's analysis in one column with Team Analysis's existing views and a full-screen "Full analysis", rather than the board's redrawn panel. _(settled 2026-10-25)_
- **A team's sources may include "Any Pokémon", which allows free members: full Showdown sets of any species in the team's pool, marked as free and counted against no bag; a pasted set the snapshots cannot back comes in as a free member when "Any Pokémon" is a source, and is left out when it is not, listed in the dialog either way.** **Why:** the owner: fights, theorycrafting and scouting an opponent, and a tournament will fix a team's sources. _(owner, 2026-10-23)_ "Counted against nothing" is _proposed in discovery, built 2026-10-25_.
