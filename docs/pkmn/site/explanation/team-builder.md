# Team Builder

The team builder is where a player of the group builds their teams, stored in their account on [the pkmn site](site.md): `/teams` lists every team its reader may see, and `/teams/<id>` builds one from any number of snapshots, any player's ([Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)), and "Any Pokémon", which allows free members of every species and move of every generation, under the team's own Any Pokémon filter, six member cards by default and up to eight, edited in place beside the team's analysis of every member or a pick of six, each member limited by its own snapshot's items and Mini Setas, and copies it to and pastes it from Showdown; the team list's Compare puts two to four teams side by side. A free team is private by default, seen only by its owner and admins, until its owner makes it public for every signed-in reader; a locked team's, a round team's and a cup team's visibility is the site's ([Who sees a team](#stored-teams)). It is the second half of Site v2's team building, scoped with the owner on 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23, and replaced Open Sheet, which the group used for series 1's open-sheet fights.

**Where it came from.** Open Sheet (scoped 2026-10-15) was a route of the site, `/open-sheet`, that read the JSON a player's export wrote, one browser tab per import, showed each in a PC with a summary and a bag, and built named teams in that PC, stored in the reader's browser (a Dexie database over IndexedDB). Series 1's final tournament is played open sheet ([Series 1 Rules → Final Tournament](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#final-tournament)): each player shares their Pokémon with their stats, abilities and the bag items that matter in a fight, and each builds from there. Snapshots stored on the site took over the imports, and the team builder took over the teams: on 2026-10-25 `/open-sheet`, its import tabs and Dexie went, and the site deletes the browser's old `open-sheet` database on load (`src/boot.tsx`). Its PC, summary and bag became the site's PC page; its team rules, analysis and Showdown text are the team builder's, below.

**Not yet:** a size fixed by a tournament is still to come with the [league](../../../work-in-progress/league/discovery.md).

## What It Reads

A team's sources are snapshots, each the [Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md)'s export, read in the browser as `snapshotSchema` in `shared/snapshot.ts`, whose first three keys (`pokemon`, `items`, `tms`) are the `Sheet` every piece reads. In a Pokémon every key but `species` reads as absent when it is missing or not of its type, as for an old export ([Snapshot Format → Old Exports](../reference/snapshot.md#old-exports)). Each Pokémon, item and TM is looked up by `shared/describe.ts`, which reads `@pkmn/dex`, into the shapes of `shared/mon.ts`, which loads no game data; `src/teams/dex.ts` keeps what only the builder needs (the natures, items, abilities and moves to pick from, a species' abilities, free members, and a member's Pokémon).

Names, descriptions, base stats and the type chart come from `@pkmn/dex`, Showdown's data, Gen 9. Fights are Showdown "[Gen 9] Custom Game" ([Series 1 Rules → The League](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#the-league)), and several Gen 1–5 Pokémon gained base stats in Gen 6+, so Showdown's Gen 9 data is what the fight runs; PokéAPI's cartridge data is not, and the export's IDs already follow Showdown.

Showdown's types and base stats drive the analysis, since Showdown sets a species' types from its own data whatever the save says. The summary shows the export's `types` and flags a species whose export types differ from Showdown's; the mod's types are modern (Clefairy is `FAIRY` in the mod's `types.txt`), so a mismatch should be rare. An ID `@pkmn/dex` does not know is shown by its raw ID with a marker and kept out of the analysis; `tinymushroom` is the one expected case and gets a hand-kept label, "Mini Seta (Tiny Mushroom)", without the marker.

Sprites are Showdown's Gen 5 static set, which fits the Gen 1–5 roster, shiny and female where they apply, with a "?" in place of an unknown species'; item icons are the game's own sprites, read from its zip ([The pkmn Site → Games](site.md#games)), and `@pkmn/img`'s for an item the game has none of (an "Any item" outside Añil) or while the sheet loads.

## Stored Teams

A team is a `team` row (`api/_lib/schema.ts`, migrations `drizzle/0008_teams.sql`, `0009_team_size.sql`, `0010_any_pokemon.sql`, `0011_pools.sql`, `0013_moves_gen.sql`, `0022_team_private.sql`, `0026_team_free_pool.sql` and `0027_cup_teams.sql`): its owner (a player; deleting the player deletes their teams), its name, its size (6 to 8, 6 by default), whether "Any Pokémon" is one of its sources (`any_pokemon`), whether it is private (`private`, true by default, which the migration gave every team made before it), its slots as jsonb, one per place, its pool as jsonb ([The Pool](#the-pool)), its Any Pokémon filter as jsonb (`free_pool`, everything by default, which the migration gave every team made before it; [The Pool](#the-pool)), for a round or cup team its match (`match_id`, deleted with the match, one team per match and player), when and by whom it was locked (`locked_at`, `locked_by`, both empty while it is not), and when it was made and last changed; the database holds the size between 6 and 8 and the slots to that many, and the API sets the size from the slots it is given (`teamSlots` in `shared/team.ts` takes 6 to 8). Its snapshot sources are `team_source` rows, the team and a snapshot with a position, so a team names its snapshots explicitly, any player's, in the order they were added; a team has at least one source, a snapshot or "Any Pokémon". A slot is empty or a member (`teamMember` in `shared/team.ts`), from a snapshot or free. A snapshot's member (`ownMember`) is the snapshot it comes from (`source`), the Pokémon's index in that snapshot's `pokemon` and its species, its item, its picked ability (null while it keeps its current one), its picked nature (`nature`, null while it keeps its in-game one; read as null from a slot stored before 2026-10-27) and four move IDs. A free member (`freeMember`) is a whole Showdown set marked `free`: its species, item, ability, level (1–100), nature, EVs (0–252 each) and IVs (0–31 each) and four move IDs; it names no snapshot and counts against no bag or Mini Setas, and a team holds one only while "Any Pokémon" is among its sources. A member whose index no longer holds that species, after its snapshot was replaced by a new upload, or whose source is not one of the team's, is treated as an empty slot holding no item, and is dropped on the next save. Deleting a snapshot deletes its `team_source` rows, and a team left with no snapshot and without "Any Pokémon" shows "This team's snapshot was deleted".

**Who sees a team.** A private team is seen only by its owner and admins; a public one by every signed-in reader. An unlocked free or try team has the toggle, private by default. A locked team is the site's to manage, so its owner has no toggle on it ([discovery → Team Locking](../../../work-in-progress/league/discovery.md#team-locking)): a Pokémon League try's team is made public when the try locks it, picked, pasted or imported, its EVs and IVs still hidden from others ([Whose EVs and IVs a reader sees](#stored-teams)), and an admin's unlock gives it back to its owner's toggle, public as the try left it. While its try holds it locked, a try's team is never private; locked or unlocked, it follows the save rule as a milestone's PC does ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league), "Pokémon League tries"): another player sees it only once they have reached the Pokémon League themselves, someone outside the series once every player has, admins always, locked or unlocked, and its owner always. A round or cup team, locked or not, has no toggle: whatever its `private`, it is seen by its owner and admins alone until its mode reveals it ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league)): in an open cup once every cup player has locked, that is once every cup match made no later than its own and still without a result has both its teams locked; in an open round once both the match's players have locked; in a closed cup or round once its match has its result; and in every mode once its match has its result. The API enforces it on every read (`teamsSeenBy` in `api/_lib/series-data.ts`, a condition on the `team` table that is none for an admin and otherwise the reader's own team, a public team of no match that backs no try of a series whose Pokémon League the reader has not reached, or a round or cup team its mode reveals): a team the reader may not see is left out of every list and count and answers 404 by its id, as one that does not exist.

**Cup teams and locking.** Each cup match gives each of its players a team of eight (`CUP_TEAM_SIZE` in `shared/league.ts`) to build, made private, empty and on their snapshot at the series' tournament milestone, once both the match and that snapshot exist ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league)); its sources, size and visibility are its match's, so it has no "Any Pokémon" and cannot be deleted, and its owner builds it as any team until they lock it in. A locked team is frozen: its owner can change nothing of its build, the name included, and only an admin unlocks it. A round or cup team still unlocked when its match is decided is locked as it stands, by no one ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league)). The lock is a column of every team, and a Pokémon League try's team takes it too: logging a try locks the team it uses, picked from the player's teams or pasted, and editing or deleting the try leaves it locked, so the try always shows what was played; series 1's imported try teams come in locked ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league), [discovery → Standings, the Pokémon League and Eggs](../../../work-in-progress/league/discovery.md#standings-the-pokémon-league-and-eggs)). A locked team has no Seen by switch, since the site decides who sees it ([Who sees a team](#stored-teams)). **Clone** copies a locked team into a new, editable team of the reader's own, its members, sources, size, Any Pokémon, pool and Any Pokémon filter, named "<name> (copy)" ("(copia)" in Spanish, the name cut to fit 60 characters), private and of no league: a player clones only their own teams, an admin any, since a copy of another's team would carry the EVs and IVs hidden from them ([Whose EVs and IVs a reader sees](#stored-teams)). An admin moving the snapshot a round or cup team rests on, while its match has no result, makes the same copy for the team's owner, locked or not, with Any Pokémon turned on, unless it has no members, and the league team starts over; an admin deleting that snapshot does the same, each member of the copy from the deleted snapshot turned into a free member; and neither is allowed while the team's match has a game recorded or a ban or a six in, nor a delete while it has a result ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league), "Round teams").

**Whose EVs and IVs a reader sees.** A snapshot's IVs, EVs and stats are seen only by its player and admins, and a free member's EVs and IVs only by its team's owner and admins, on every team; everyone else gets the rest of the build ([discovery → Team Locking](../../../work-in-progress/league/discovery.md#team-locking)). The API leaves them out (`api/_lib/spreads.ts`): `GET /api/teams/<id>` answers each source's snapshot with every Pokémon's and fainted Pokémon's `ivs`, `evs` and `stats` null unless the reader is that snapshot's player or an admin, marking the source `spreadHidden`, and each free member with `evs` and `ivs` null unless the reader owns the team or is an admin (`shownSlots` in `shared/team.ts`); the PC route does the same for the PC ([The pkmn Site → Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)). The page then reads such a Pokémon as it reads an old export without IVs or EVs ([Snapshot Format → Old Exports](../reference/snapshot.md#old-exports)): its summary's Stats shows "—" for IV, EV and its stat at its level, and its Lv 50 view the range over IVs 0 to 31, under "Only its trainer and admins see its IVs, EVs and stats" (a Pokémon described with `spreadHidden`, `shared/mon.ts`) in place of the old export's warning; Copy for Showdown leaves its `IVs:` line out, and a free member's `EVs:` line, so the analysis and Compare, which read that text, work its stats out from its species' base stats at its level and nature with Showdown's defaults for what the text leaves out: IVs 31, and EVs 0 for a free member or the league's 1 in every stat for a snapshot's. A free member whose EVs and IVs the reader may not see has no Set.

**Whose saves a team draws on.** A team may draw only on a snapshot its builder may see, by the series' save rule ([The pkmn Site → Snapshots](site.md#snapshots), "Whose saves a reader sees"): `GET /api/teams/snapshots` lists only those, and `POST` and `PUT` refuse any other source as one that does not exist. A team a reader may see can still rest on a source they may not, another's public team or a revealed league team built from a save ahead of the reader's round: its page then answers that source with only the Pokémon the team's members come from, renumbered with the members, and nothing else of the save (an empty bag, no money, play time or records), so the members show as they are and the rest of the box does not. Its owner's own team is cut the same way, and is then read-only to them while it rests on a save they may not see (`canEdit` false), since the editor works on the whole box: `PUT` refuses its `slots` as one naming a hidden source, and the team list marks it `sourceHidden`, so the PC's "Add to team" leaves it out; that happens to a team built before the rule, when a player joining mid-series lowers what everyone outside the series sees, or when an admin moves or deletes one of the owner's snapshots.

The routes are `api/_lib/teams.ts`, schemas in `shared/team.ts`:

- `GET /api/teams` (`?owner=<id>` for one player's) lists every team the reader may see, last changed first, each with its owner, whether it is private (`private`), the series it files under (`series`), its league kind (`league`, `null` for a free team), its state (`state`), its sources (player, series, milestone), whether any of them is a snapshot the reader may not see (`sourceHidden`, [Whose saves a team draws on](#stored-teams)), whether it allows "Any Pokémon" (`anyPokemon`) and its members' species. A team that backs a Pokémon League try is a league try: `league` holds its series, the try's number among its player's tries in that series, in the order logged as the league's Pokémon League view numbers them, and when it was logged, the newest try's when a team backs several; it files under that series alone, whatever its sources, so an Any Pokémon try team still files under its league's. A round team's `league` holds its series, its match, its round's number and milestone and its match and its opponent's name, each `null` while a closed round hides the pair from the reader ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league)); it files under its round's series. A cup team's `league` holds its series, its match, its place in the bracket (`slot`), how many entered the cup (`entrants`, from which the page names its stage) and its opponent's name; it files under its cup's series. A free team files under its sources' series, and under none when it has no snapshot source. A team's state is "locked" (Locked) once locked, else "todo" (To build) for a round or cup team and "edit" (Editable) for the rest (`teamState` in `shared/team.ts`), and `lockedAt` says when it was locked.
- `GET /api/teams/snapshots` lists every snapshot a team may draw from, those of every series but a draft that the reader may see ([Whose saves a team draws on](#stored-teams)), newest series first, then by player and latest milestone first, each with its player, series and milestone.
- `GET /api/teams/<id>` answers the team with its sources' snapshots and missing-field reports, `anyPokemon`, `private`, `pool`, `freePool`, its `league` as the list gives it, `lockedAt`, whether the reader owns it (`mine`), may change it (`canEdit`: its owner while it is not locked), may lock it in (`canLock`: its owner, on an unlocked round or cup team), may unlock it (`canUnlock`: an admin, on a locked team) and may clone it (`canClone`: its owner or an admin, on a locked team); 404 "No such team" to a reader who may not see it. A snapshot whose IVs, EVs and stats the reader may not see comes without them and marked `spreadHidden`, and a free member whose EVs and IVs the reader may not see with them null ([Whose EVs and IVs a reader sees](#stored-teams)); a source the reader may not see comes cut to the team's own Pokémon, and the team is then not the reader's to change ([Whose saves a team draws on](#stored-teams)).
- `POST /api/teams` makes a team for the signed-in player, private, on the snapshots it names (`sources`, any player's, which may be none when `anyPokemon` is true) and "Any Pokémon" when `anyPokemon` is true, or else on their latest snapshot of the running series, named the lowest free "Team n", empty or with the slots it carries, and with the `pool` and `freePool` it carries, else its series' pool and the open filter. A Pokémon League try's pasted team and the league import's are made the same way (`insertTeam` in `api/_lib/teams.ts`) by their own routes ([The pkmn Site → League](site.md#league)). It answers 403 to an admin (admins have no teams but the clones they make), 400 for a snapshot that does not exist, is in a draft series or is one the player may not see, 400 "A team needs a source" for no snapshot and no "Any Pokémon", and 400 when it names none and the player has no snapshot in the running series ("Upload a snapshot to the running series first").
- `PUT /api/teams/<id>` changes the name, the slots, and with the slots the size, the sources, a list replacing the team's in that order, `anyPokemon`, `pool`, `freePool`, or `private`, refusing with 400 a change that leaves the team no source or names a snapshot as `POST` refuses one, and `slots` on a team resting on such a snapshot; `DELETE /api/teams/<id>` deletes the team; both answer 403 to anyone but the owner and 409 "This team is locked" on a locked team, `private` included, the change written only while the team is still unlocked. A slot whose member's source is not one of the team's, or a free member on a team without "Any Pokémon", the slots sent or else the stored ones, is refused with 400, so a source a member still uses cannot be removed. On a round or cup team `PUT` refuses sources, `anyPokemon`, `private` and slots of another size than the team's with 400 ("A league team's sources, size and visibility are set by its match"), and `DELETE` answers 409 ("A league team stays with its match").
- `GET /api/teams/waiting` lists the reader's round matches still to play that give them no team yet, since their snapshot at the round's milestone is missing: each one's series, match, round number, milestone and opponent's name, `null` in a closed round, whose pair the reader sees only once they lock.
- `GET /api/teams/to-build` answers how many of the reader's own round and cup teams are still to build, unlocked and so in state `todo` (`teamState` in `shared/team.ts`), as `{ count }`, for the header's Teams tab. A round match still waiting on its snapshot gives no team yet, so it is not counted; an admin, who has no teams, gets 0.
- `POST /api/teams/<id>/lock` locks a round or cup team in for its owner, 403 to anyone else, 400 on a team that is not a league team or short of members ("Fill all 8 slots before locking in"; a round team needs every slot filled, or, when its snapshot holds fewer living Pokémon it may field, every one of them, "Fill 4 slots before locking in", `lockNeeds` in `shared/league.ts`, which leaves out a species Showdown tags Restricted Legendary, Sub-Legendary or Mythical, its tags read by `speciesTags` in `shared/dex.ts`, which the route imports only when it runs), 409 once it is locked; `POST /api/teams/<id>/unlock` unlocks a team, 403 to anyone but an admin, 404 "No such team" for none, 409 on a team that is not locked or whose match has a game without a result with a ban or a six in ("A game of this team's match without a result has bans or picks in", [The pkmn Site → Decisions](site.md#decisions)); `POST /api/teams/<id>/clone` makes the reader a copy of a locked team ([Cup teams and locking](#stored-teams)) and answers its `{ id }`, 404 "No such team" to a reader who may not see it, 403 to anyone but its owner and an admin, 409 "This team is not locked" on an unlocked one.

`npm run seed` makes Red a "Seed team" of the first three Pokémon of Red's latest Series 1 snapshot, when there is one, and Series 2's seeded cup gives Red a cup team for its final, "Cup v Blue", on Red's Series 2 Tournament snapshot, when there is one; when that snapshot holds eight Pokémon or more, the seed also gives Blue a copy of it and so "Cup v Red", fills both with their snapshot's first eight and locks them to play the final's first game ([How to Run and Deploy the Site](../how-to/run-and-deploy-the-site.md)).

Each team also counts on the series page and the profile: a trainer row's Teams tile is how many of the trainer's teams the reader may see, and opens the team list filtered to them ([The Team List](#the-team-list)), which lists those same teams, and a profile banner's Teams box how many of the player's teams the reader may see have a source in that series (`trainersOf` in `api/_lib/series-data.ts`, `api/_lib/profiles.ts`).

## The Team List

`/teams` (`src/pages/teams.tsx`, after `teams-list.dc.html`, `teams-list-phone.dc.html`, `teams-list-filtered.dc.html` and `teams-list-phone-filters.dc.html`), the header's Teams tab, lists the signed-in player's teams under "Yours" and everyone else's they may see, the public ones, under "Other trainers"; an admin sees one list, "Every team", private teams included. Each row is the owner's badge, the team's name, then a line of its marks, when it has any: for a league team its kind as a chip ("League try 2", in `try` with the league mark; "Cup · <stage> v <opponent>", "Cup · Final v Blue", in `cup` with the cup mark, the stage named as the Cup view names it, `cupStage` in `src/league/cup.tsx`; "Round <n> v <opponent>", "Round 2 v Blue", or "Round <n>" alone while a closed round hides the opponent, in `round-team` with the flag), "To build" on `round` for a team still to build and "Locked" with a lock for a locked one, "Private" with a crossed-out eye on a dashed `cell-hi` border in `muted-foreground` on the reader's own private unlocked free and try teams, the only ones with the toggle (titled "Only you and admins see it"; others' teams the reader can see are public, and an admin sees no such mark on anyone's), then, on a line of its own from `sm` and after the marks on a phone, "<owner> · " on others' teams before its source line: the series it files under, named once ("Series 1, 2"), and its sources joined by " + " ("Series 1 · Post-League", "<player>'s <milestone>" for a snapshot not the owner's, "Any Pokémon" when the team allows it, so a try team reads "Series 1 · Any Pokémon"), only its sources for a team that files under no series; from three snapshots the line counts them rather than naming them ("Series 2 · 4 snapshots + Any Pokémon"), and it is titled with every source named, for a line still cut off at the column's 300px; then a strip of its slots, one per place, six to eight (each member's sprite, an empty slot dashed, shorter on a phone above six), packed to the left after the name's fixed column so the strips line up down the list, and across the row's width on a phone, how many slots are filled ("4/6", "8/8", green when full, amber otherwise) and when it last changed ("Changed today", "yesterday", "n days ago", else "in <month>"), or for a try team when the try was logged ("Logged in October"), and for any other locked team when it was locked ("Locked today"); the whole row opens the team. Above the reader's own teams, each round match still waiting on their snapshot is a dashed note: its Round chip, "Reach <milestone> and upload its snapshot to get your team" and "Upload it on the series page", a link to the series page (`GET /api/teams/waiting`; no board draws it), while the filters would show a round team to build in its series. "New team" makes one on the player's latest snapshot of the running series and opens it; when it cannot, a menu under it says why and offers every snapshot to start from instead ("Or start from:"), and "Any Pokémon", so a player with no snapshot of their own can build on another's or on free members alone. **Compare**, beside it, opens [Compare](#compare).

**Filters.** Under the title a row filters the list: **Series** (All and each series a listed team files under), **Kind** (All, Free, Round, Cup, League try) and **State** (All, To build, Locked, Editable), each a radio group of pixel-face options on an `ink` box, the chosen one on `border`; **Owner**, a select of Everyone, the reader "(you)" and every other owner of a listed team, on `border` while set; then the count shown ("8 teams") and, while any filter is set, **Clear filters**. On a phone the row is a **Filters** button with a `primary` pill counting the filters set, the count and **Clear**, and the button opens the same filters below it as a panel, each group's options wrapping under its label. The list is read once (`GET /api/teams`) and filtered in the browser, so a filter answers at once. Each filter lives in the URL, `?series=<n>`, `?kind=<free|round|cup|try>`, `?state=<todo|locked|edit>` and the owner as `?player=<id>`, so a reload or a shared link keeps them; a change replaces the history entry rather than adding one, and Clear drops them all. While the owner is set to the reader only "Yours" shows, and to anyone else only "Other trainers"; a group with nothing to show reads "No teams match these filters" while a filter is set and "No teams yet" otherwise. `/teams?player=<id>`, where a trainer row's Teams tile on the series page leads, opens the list with that owner set; a player with no team is named in the select from `GET /api/profiles/<id>`. The header's Teams tab always opens the whole list.

## The Team Page

`/teams/<id>` (`src/pages/team.tsx`, after `team.dc.html`, `team-sources.dc.html`, `team-member.dc.html`, `team-phone*.dc.html` and `team-locked.dc.html`) shows one team, editable by its owner while it is not locked and read-only to everyone else who may see it, and to its owner too once locked; to a reader who may not, it says "No such team".

**Its head.** A "Teams" link back to the list; the team's name, which its owner renames in place by clicking it (1 to 60 characters, Enter or leaving the field saves, Escape cancels); on a team its reader does not own, a chip "<owner>'s team" in their colour and, while the team is not locked, a warning chip "View only" with a lock, so a reader signed in as someone else, an admin above all, sees why nothing can be changed; on a locked team, "Locked · <stage>" for a cup team, "Locked · Round <n>" for a round team, or "Locked", in `yellow` with a lock on `trophy` with a `trophy-rim` border (`team-locked.dc.html`); and a chip per source, "<player>'s PC · <milestone>", each with that snapshot's Mini Setas left and its money left for mints ("$?" for a snapshot without money, such as an old export), either in `coral` once overspent. For the owner a source's chip has a remove button while no member of the team comes from it and it is not the team's only source, and **+ Source** opens "Add a source", a menu of the snapshots not yet sources (`GET /api/teams/snapshots`, grouped by series when there are several, `src/teams/components/snapshot-menu.tsx`); adding or removing one writes the sources with `PUT`. The menu ends in "Any Pokémon" while the team does not allow it; then "Any Pokémon" is a chip of its own, with no Mini Setas, its remove button there while no free member is on the team and it is not the team's only source, and a snapshot's chip may likewise go when "Any Pokémon" is the other source. Adding or removing it writes `anyPokemon` with `PUT`. Past three sources, "Any Pokémon" counted, the chips fold into one button (`SourcesFold`, after `team-sources.dc.html`): the sources' initials overlapped, "<n> sources" and a chevron, `border-coral` with a "!" while any source has spent more Mini Setas or money than it has, opening a popover of the same chips, remove buttons included; **+ Source** stays beside it. Beside them, for the owner of an unlocked free or try team, the **Seen by Private · Public** switch, the team's visibility, a segmented track like the Members switch, each option titled with who sees the team, a change written with `PUT` at once; the **Members 6 · 7 · 8** switch, setting the team's size, its sizes below the last filled slot disabled, so taking a size down never drops a member; the **Moves** switch, the team's move rule ([The Pool](#the-pool)), a menu below `sm`; then Paste and Delete (confirmed in a dialog) for the owner, Unlock with an open lock for an admin on a locked team, Copy for Showdown for anyone, **Clone** for the owner and an admin on a locked team, opening the new team, and **Lock in** in red for the owner of an unlocked round or cup team, off and titled "Fill all 8 slots to lock it in" while a slot is empty (on a round team while it holds fewer than its six or than its snapshot's living Pokémon it may field, legendaries and mythicals left out, "Fill 4 slots to lock it in"), asking "Lock in <name>?" in a dialog that warns only an admin can unlock it, and locking once the page's pending writes have landed. A round or cup team's head has no Seen by switch, no Members switch, no Paste, no Delete and no way to add or remove a source; a locked team is read-only to everyone, its owner included, as another's team is (`canEdit`), but for a locked try team's Seen by switch, and the page reloads on a lock or an unlock.

**The cards.** The slots are a two-column grid from `lg`, three rows for six and four for seven or eight, each row a member card's 246px, which an empty slot keeps too, so adding a member grows nothing; from `lg` the rows share the column's height when four of them do not fit at 246px, down to 188px, so seven or eight fill the screen on a 1080p or 1440×1000 screen, and the column scrolls past that on shorter screens (one column below `sm`, each card 330px, its moves one to a row, and the page scrolling). A card under 246px is compact (the `short` container variant in `src/index.css`, on each slot from `lg`): its sprite fitted to a 56×52 box, a 52px header, the item, ability and nature at 34px and the moves at 30px, its rows spread over the height it has. A member's card has a header row (its whole sprite, fitted to a 72×84 box, name, Showdown types and level, the source's tag "<player>'s PC" in their colour, or a dashed "Free" on a free member, and a chevron) that opens the member's summary in the panel, or closes it back to the analysis; on a free member, for the owner, **Set**, which opens its level, nature, EVs and IVs in the panel; a button to take it out; its item, its ability (with a Mini Seta mark when the picked ability is not its current one) and its nature (with a pixel mint when it is not the in-game one, and a `warning` "$" beside it when that mint is bought rather than taken from the bag), the three on one row, the nature's column at least 6rem; and its four moves, one to a row below `lg` and two to a row from it, each with its type, name, power and accuracy as a percentage, from `lg` its type as a dot and its power over its accuracy, so the name has room on every card ([Site Design System → Patterns](../reference/design-system.md#patterns)), a move it cannot learn in this save in the warning colour, as is a free member's move its team's Any Pokémon filter does not allow; a free member whose species the filter leaves out keeps its place, its dashed "Free" tag in `warning`, titled "<name> is outside the Any Pokémon filter: <filter>". On a read-only team the item, ability and moves do nothing and the take-out button is gone. An empty slot is a dashed "Add a Pokémon" for the owner and "Empty" for anyone else.

**The panel.** Beside the cards (from `lg`) the panel shows what is being looked at: the team's analysis by default ([The Analysis](#the-analysis)); the member's summary, the PC's Info · Stats · Moves over that member's set (its picked ability shown as in use, its moves as its current ones), under a link back to the analysis; or an editor, opened by the card's item, ability or a move, whose back button and whose choice return to where it was opened from, the summary or the analysis. The card being edited is outlined in cyan, and a member just put in a slot in yellow until the next action. Below `lg` the page has a Members · Analysis switch, and the summary and the editors open full screen.

- **Item:** the member's own source's bag (`<player>'s bag`), Items and Berries pockets with a search, each item with the members holding it and how many are left; the selected item's description, "Give to <member>" (disabled as "<member> holds it" or "None left") and "Take its item". Only items Showdown knows are listed, so not `tinymushroom`.
- **Item** of a free member: "Any item", every item of Showdown's Gen 9 data that is not marked non-standard, except the Mini Seta, none limited, the selected one reading "Any item: free member".
- **Ability:** every one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, base or hidden, each marked "In game" (its current one) or with its cost, "<n> Mini Setas" from the source's game, with its description, and the source's Mini Setas left in the head; an ability that costs is disabled while the source's Mini Setas left, not counting this member's own pick, are fewer than its cost. A free member's are every ability of Showdown's Gen 9 data that is not marked non-standard (`ANY_ABILITIES` in `src/teams/dex.ts`), unmarked and free, under a search: its species' own first, base then hidden, under "Can have", then every other under "Other abilities"; the Any Pokémon filter does not touch abilities.
- **Move:** "Replace <move>" lists, when the export carries the Pokémon's learnable moves, its current moves, then the Move Relearner's with the level each is learned at, then its TMs, each move once, as many of them as the team's move rule allows (`allowedMoves` in `shared/mon.ts`; [The Pool](#the-pool)); without them, the bag's TMs then every move up to the pool's Moves up to ([The Pool](#the-pool)); a free member, having no export and no bag, follows its team's Any Pokémon filter's TMs ([The Pool](#the-pool)): at "Any move", the default, it lists under "Can learn" the moves of its species' Showdown learnset, its pre-evolutions' included, then under "Other moves" every other move of every generation (`anyMoves` and `learnset` in `src/teams/dex.ts`); with TM generations picked, "Level up" lists the moves its line learns by level in any generation and "Gen <n> TMs" those it learns by machine in the picked generations, and nothing else (`freeMoves`). Each row shows the type, name, category, power and accuracy, under a head naming the Category, Pow and Acc columns, and Showdown's short description, a move already in another slot is disabled, a search narrows them, and "Leave the slot empty" clears it.
- **Nature:** the 25 natures, each with its effect ("+Atk −SpA" or "Neutral") and, on a snapshot's member, "In game" on its in-game nature, "Mint in bag ×<n>" while its source's bag holds a mint of it not taken by another member, or else "Mint $<price>", the source's game's mint price, disabled while the source's money left, not counting this member's own pick, is below it; the head shows that money left, and a line under the list says how a nature is paid for. Picking the in-game nature takes the change back. A free member's are unmarked and free, the same choice as in **Set**.
- **Set** of a free member: its level and nature, then one row per stat with its base, its EVs and IVs to type and the stat at its level, and the EV total, coral above 510. Each change is written as it is made.

**Seven or eight.** At a size above six the panel's analysis heads with **Analyse: All <n> · Pick 6**, open to every reader since it only changes what they look at. Pick 6 analyses six of the members, the first six filled until the reader changes them: each card carries a checkbox in its top-left corner, over the sprite's margin so the card keeps its size, ticked and green with a green border when the member is in, empty and faded when it is out, the empty ones disabled while six are in, and "<n> of 6 in" turns amber below six. A Pokémon or link picked in Teamwork belongs to the members it was picked among (`TeamGraph` keys it by the analysed members' ids), so switching All and Pick 6 or changing the pick clears it rather than leaving it on a member that moved or left; before 2026-10-28 it stayed, and a picked seventh or eighth member crashed the page once Pick 6 dropped it. The switch and the pick are the reader's own, kept per team in the browser's `localStorage` (`team-pick:<id>`, `src/teams/pick.ts`); a team at six analyses every member.

**Limits.** Each source is its own stock, never pooled: a member can hold an item only while its own source's `quantity` of it is not used up by the members from that source. In the game an NPC switches an ability for one Mini Seta, to any of the Pokémon's abilities, hidden included, so each member whose picked ability is not its current one needs one, counted against its own source's `tinymushroom` quantity in that source's chip; how many it needs is the source's game's `ability_setas`, 1 for Añil ([The pkmn Site → Games](site.md#games)). A nature works the same way with mints: each member whose picked nature is not its in-game one needs a mint of that nature, taken first from its source's bag (any pocket, by the item's game ID `<NATURE>MINT`), the earliest slots first, and the rest bought at the game's `mint_price`, $30,000 for Añil ([World → Item and Gift Events](../../games/anil/reference/world.md#item-and-gift-events)), out of the snapshot's `trainer.money`; a snapshot without money can only use its bag's mints (`natureSpend`, `moneyLeft` and `naturePrice` in `src/teams/team.ts`). A picked nature changes the member everywhere its nature counts: the card, the summary's stats (the in-game ones worked out again from its IVs, EVs and level, and the Level 50 ones), the analysis and Copy for Showdown (`withNature` in `src/teams/dex.ts`). A free member is counted against nothing.

**Adding a Pokémon.** An empty slot opens a full-screen dialog, "Add to slot <n>", with a "From" tab per source and the team's strip, the slot being filled marked: the picked source's PC box with its search and type filter, that source's Pokémon already in the team faded, and the selected one's summary beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) ending in "Put in slot <n>", or "In the team". A new member starts with its current moves in its slots, in the save's order, no item and its current ability. With "Any Pokémon" among the sources the dialog has its tab too: the species the team's Any Pokémon filter allows, every species of every generation by default ([The Pool](#the-pool)), in a grid with a search, a type filter and a **Filter** toggle that opens the filter's bar over the grid, the head naming the filter ("Gen 9 data · every generation"), and beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) the picked species' head and its set (Level 50, a neutral nature, no EVs, IVs 31, as it would join), editable before "Put in slot <n>"; a free member starts with its species' first ability, no item and no moves.

**Saving.** Every change writes the whole slots with `PUT`, one write after another in the order made, while the page keeps showing the latest slots; a failed write shows "Not saved: <reason>". A size change writes the slots, grown with empty places or cut back to the new size.

## The Analysis

The panel's Analysis, over every member or the pick of six ([The Team Page](#the-team-page)), has Overview, Teamwork (the graph), Playstyle (the archetype and its map), Roles (the member cards), Type matchups (the matrices) and Speed (the speed tiers), the shared module's views in one column ([The Analysis Module](#the-analysis-module)), each filling the panel's height, and "Full analysis" opens the same views full screen, plus Opponents (the Pool view), with the pool's chip and, on a Gen 9 pool, the Tera toggle ([The Pool](#the-pool)). The team reaches the shared module through its Showdown text, the one Copy for Showdown writes, ranked in the team's pool (`src/teams/components/sheet-analysis.tsx`). Overview adds the builder's own three panels (`src/teams/analysis.ts`, `src/teams/components/summary-panels.tsx`):

- **Speed order:** each member's Level 50 Speed from its IVs, as in the league's pokepastes: Level 50, real IVs, 1 EV in every stat ([Series 1 Rules → Pokepastes](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#pokepastes)). A member without IVs, or whose IVs the reader may not see, sorts by its IV 31 speed, then its IV 0 speed, and shows its range.
- **Defence:** for each of the 18 types, how many members are weak (above ×1), resist (below ×1) or are immune (×0), using Showdown's Gen 9 type chart over each member's Showdown types and its picked ability (its current one when none is picked). A type is a hole when more members are weak to it than resist or are immune to it.
- **Coverage:** which types the picked moves hit super-effectively on their own, and which types nothing covers.

A free member's stats are its own, at Level 50, from its IVs, EVs and nature, when the reader may see them. Members with an unknown species are left out. The abilities that change a matchup come from the site's one matchup-ability table ([The Analysis Module → Data](#data)), read in Gen 9 like the type chart. Coverage counts a member's damaging picked moves, leaving out status moves, fixed-damage moves (Seismic Toss, Sonic Boom…), one-hit KOs, moves whose damage ignores type matchups (Counter, Mirror Coat, Metal Burst, Bide, Final Gambit, Psywave, Super Fang, Endeavor) and Hidden Power, whose type depends on IVs the export does not hold. A member with no damaging move picked stands in with its STAB types.

## The Pool

Each team has its own pool, the Pokémon its analysis ranks it against: the shared module's pool rules ([The Analysis Module → Data](#data)), stored as the team's `pool` (`teamPool` in `shared/pool.ts`): the data's generation, the generation species go up to, the generation moves go up to (Moves up to, which limits only the move picker of a snapshot member whose export carries no learnable moves), no legendaries, no mythicals, fully evolved, a BST cap, and the move rule (`moves`), which of a snapshot member's moves the team may pick: **Known**, its current moves alone; **Its level**, with its learnset up to its own level and its bag TMs, what the game's relearner teaches; **Lv 50**, with its learnset up to level 50 and its bag TMs; **Any level**, with its whole learnset and its bag TMs. A learnset move with no level, a first move, counts at every rule but Known, and a snapshot without levels keeps its whole relearner list. A free member has no learnset of the save and no level, so the rule leaves it to the Any Pokémon filter, below. The owner sets the rule with the head's Moves switch, which writes the pool with `PUT`; a member holding a move outside it shows that move in the warning colour, its title naming the rule. Inside the team builder, the move picker, a member's summary and the summary in "Add a Pokémon" list only the moves the rule allows; the PC's summary lists them all. A new team takes the default pool of the series its first snapshot source belongs to, or, with "Any Pokémon" as its only source, of the running series, or else `DEFAULT_POOL`; the API sets it on `POST /api/teams` unless a pool is sent, as Paste sends the current team's so its new team keeps it. Series 1's default, every series' until the admin changes it in the series form ([The pkmn Site → Series](site.md#series)), is Showdown's Gen 9 data with species and moves up to Gen 5, fully evolved, no BST cap, and Any level, the final tournament's rule ([Rules → Final Tournament](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#final-tournament)): Añil's roster and moves under the rules league fights run, since fights are a Gen 9 Custom Game.

Full analysis's Opponents view opens with the pool bar (`src/components/pool-bar.tsx`, which the series form shares): Data, Species up to and Moves up to as rows of generations, each a menu of them below `sm` (a Data change lowering the other two only when they would pass it), the three toggles, the BST cap and the pool's size. The owner's changes write the team's pool (`PUT /api/teams/<id>` with `pool`) and re-rank the analysis at once, percentiles and all; another reader sees the bar disabled. Under it is the Pool view (`src/teams/components/pool-view.tsx`) over that pool, whose Distribution panel picks its stat from a row of them, a menu below `sm`. The chip in Full analysis's header names the pool and its size (`poolLabel` in `shared/pool.ts`).

**The Any Pokémon filter** is a team's second, separate setting: what its free members are picked from, never what the analysis ranks against, so a team can be built from Gen 9 and analysed against a Gen 5 field. Stored as the team's `free_pool` (`freePool` in `shared/pool.ts`), it holds a **Pokémon pool**, the species up to a generation, no legendaries, no mythicals, fully evolved and a BST cap (its top, 800, meaning no cap, so Eternatus-Eternamax stays in), and a **TM pool**, `tms`: "any", every move of every generation, or a list of generations, which leaves a free member its line's level-up moves of any generation and the moves it learns by machine (TM, TR or HM) in those generations; an empty list is level-up moves only. A new team's is everything (`DEFAULT_FREE_POOL`), and Paste's new team keeps the current team's. The owner sets it in "Add a Pokémon"'s Any Pokémon tab with **Filter**, which opens `FreePoolBar` (`src/components/pool-bar.tsx`, the pool bar's Species up to row and its toggles and BST cap, then a TMs row of "Any move" and a toggle per generation) and writes it with `PUT` as it changes. The species are Showdown's Gen 9 species the pool rules keep with every filter open (`selectable` in `src/analysis/pool.ts`): 1,127, with their legendaries, mythicals and unevolved Pokémon, without CAP, custom, future, battle-only, cosmetic and item-made formes (`freeSpecies` in `src/teams/dex.ts`). A TM generation's moves come from the learnsets' machine sources, `<gen>M`, in `@pkmn/dex`'s Gen 9 learnsets for Gen 3 to 9 and its Gen 2 learnsets for Gen 1 and 2, across the species' pre-evolutions. The filter limits picking only: a free member it leaves out, or a move it does not allow, stays on the team in the warning colour ([The Team Page](#the-team-page)), and Paste admits every species and move Showdown's Gen 9 data knows, CAP, custom, future and Let's Go species aside (`ANY_POKEMON` in `src/teams/showdown.ts`; another set is listed "not a species the site can give"). The analysis still reads every free member against the team's pool: a member outside it is ranked like any other, and Roles notes it, "Not in this pool: introduced in Gen 6", as it does a snapshot member's.

Speed is the shared module's speed tiers panel (`SpeedTiers` in `src/components/analysis/charts.tsx`): the members' final Speed against the pool's median, 75th percentile and fastest at the team's level.

The Tera toggle shows in Full analysis's header while the pool's data is Gen 9. On, each member's Members card gets a Tera type picker, starting at its first type, and the defensive analysis reads each member as its Tera type alone; the toggle and the picks are the reader's, held only while the page is open.

## Compare

Compare puts two to four teams side by side, any the reader can see, theirs or other trainers' public ones (`src/teams/components/compare-view.tsx`, after `compare.dc.html` and `compare-phone.dc.html`). On the team list, **Compare**, always on, switches the list to picking: the buttons become "<n> picked", **Cancel** and **Compare teams**, on from two; a click on a row picks it instead of opening it, ringed in cyan with a checked box, and at four picked the rest fade and cannot be picked, as does a team with no member. On a phone Compare runs the width of the page under the title. The comparison's code, which needs the game data, loads when Compare is first pressed, so the list itself loads none of it ([The pkmn Site → Performance](site.md#performance)). **Compare teams** opens the comparison full screen, titled "Compare <n> teams" with the pool's chip, which on a phone sits above the teams and wraps onto a second line, with squarer corners, rather than pushing the page sideways; closing it, or "Back to Teams" on a phone, returns to the list and ends the picking.

Each team is fetched whole (`GET /api/teams/<id>`) and read as the analysis reads it, through its Showdown text: every filled member, whatever the reader's Analyse switch on a team of seven or eight, ranked in the first picked team's pool so every team is measured on one. A team none of whose members the module can read is named above the comparison and left out of it.

**The scoreboard** (`scoreboard` in `src/analysis/compare.ts`) has one column per team, headed by its owner's badge and name and the team's name, with no sprites, and one row per measure, each cell a figure and a note: Members ("<n> empty of <size>" while the team has empty places), Average Speed and Fastest (with its name), at the members' real stats; Average bulk, the mean of HP × (Def + SpD) / 2, in hundreds; Types hitting 3+ ×2 and Types none resists (with the types); Types hit ×2 ("of 18"); Coverage holes, the types nothing hits neutrally or better; Hazards · removal (how many set hazards and how many remove them); Speed control (Thunder Wave, Tailwind, Trick Room, Icy Wind, priority moves and the like); and Recovery. The best figure of a row is green on a green ground, none on a tie. On a phone the teams are numbered in a list above it, and each measure is a line of its own over one cell per team.

**Head to head** is a damage calc between any two of the compared teams' members: the Attacker and the Defender each pick a team and a Pokémon, shown with its sprite and its stats, and the attacker's damaging moves fill the Move picker, each as "<move> · <type> · <power> · <accuracy>%". It shows the hit as a range of the defender's HP, a bar, its verdict ("Guaranteed OHKO", "Possible 3HKO", "No effect") and the move's power, multiplier, STAB and the attacker's level; the range turns amber from half the HP and coral at all of it. It is the module's `calcHit`: real sets at their level (a member whose EVs and IVs the reader may not see at its base stats, as its Showdown text reads, [Stored Teams](#stored-teams)), with Choice Band and Specs, Life Orb, Expert Belt, Assault Vest, Huge Power, Multiscale and the like, a variable-power or multi-hit move counting as one hit. **⇄ Swap sides** swaps the two.

**Matchups** sits under the scoreboard and the calc: the reader picks two of the compared teams as **Rows** and **Columns** (picking the other's team swaps them, and **⇄** swaps them outright), and the grid has a row per member of one and a column per member of the other, each named with its Speed. Each cell gives the best hit both ways (`bestHit` in `src/analysis/compare.ts`, the highest `calcHit` top of the attacker's damaging moves): **→** the row member's on the column member and **←** the column member's back, as a rounded share of HP in the calc's colours, with the move's name, a grey 0% when nothing lands and "—" without a damaging move; **1st** in cyan marks the line of the member that moves first at its real Speed, **tie** both on a Speed tie. A key reads "→ row hits column" over "← column hits row" in the grid's corner, and on one line under the team fields on a phone. A click on a cell loads that pair into Head to head, the row member attacking with that best move, and outlines the cell in cyan while the calc shows that pair either way round. A column is at least 120px wide, so a team of eight on a narrow desktop scrolls sideways in the grid's box rather than squeezing its cells. On a phone the grid scrolls sideways in its box with the name column stuck to the left, its cells drop the move's name, and a tap scrolls up to the calc.

## Showdown Text

**Copy.** "Copy for Showdown" writes the team with `src/teams/showdown.ts` in the layout of Showdown's own export: `Nickname (Species) (M) @ Item`, with the nickname only when it differs from Showdown's species name, the export's gender whenever it is `M` or `F`, and the item only when one is held; then `Ability:` (the picked one, else the current one, left out when the export has none), `Level: 50`, `Shiny: Yes` when shiny, the league's `EVs: 1 HP / 1 Atk / 1 Def / 1 SpA / 1 SpD / 1 Spe`, `<Nature> Nature` when the export has one, `IVs:` with each IV below 31, and one `- Move` line per filled move slot. A free member writes its own set as Showdown does: its species, `@ Item`, `Ability:`, `Level:` unless it is 100, `EVs:` with each EV above 0, `<Nature> Nature`, `IVs:` and its moves. A member whose IVs and EVs the reader may not see is copied without its `IVs:` line and, a free one, without its `EVs:` line. Members are separated by a blank line; empty slots, members whose index no longer holds their species and species Showdown does not know are left out. Every name is Showdown's English one, read from `@pkmn/dex` by ID, whatever language the site is read in ([The pkmn Site → Languages](site.md#languages)), so the text pastes into Showdown and the analysis reads it the same way; the analysis translates the names it shows. The button is disabled when nothing would be copied, and reads "Copied", or "Copy failed" when the browser refuses the clipboard, for a moment after a click.

**Paste.** The owner's Paste opens "Paste from Showdown", which the site's shared parser (`src/analysis/paste.ts`) reads and `src/teams/showdown.ts` matches to the team's sources, making a new team on the same sources, pool and Any Pokémon filter (`POST /api/teams` with the slots) and opening it. Each set is matched to a Pokémon of the sources, taken in their order, by its species, preferring one whose nickname matches and skipping those already placed. Its item is given when that Pokémon's own snapshot still has one left, its ability is picked when it is one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, and its moves fill the four slots whether or not the Pokémon can learn them; a typed Hidden Power reads as Hidden Power. Its nature line, when it has one and it differs from the Pokémon's in-game nature, is picked when the source can pay for it, a mint from the bag or its money, as the Nature editor would (else listed as left out); gender, shiny, level, EVs and IVs are ignored, since the snapshot holds those; `===` team headers, a move's `/` alternatives after the first and any set past the eighth are skipped, and the new team's size is six or, for seven or eight sets matched, that many. The shared parser reads up to eight sets. When "Any Pokémon" is among the sources, a set no snapshot can back, its species in no source or with every copy already placed, comes in as a free member when Showdown's Gen 9 data knows its species, CAP, custom, future and Let's Go species aside, whatever the Any Pokémon filter says, keeping its item when Showdown knows it (a non-standard one too), its ability when it is one of the ability editor's, whether or not its species can have it (else its species' first), its level, nature, EVs (each held to 252, the total to 510) and IVs, and its known moves; the new team also allows "Any Pokémon". The dialog lists the sets that came in as free members. Whatever the sources cannot back is left out and listed in the dialog with its reason: a species in no source or with every copy already placed (or, with "Any Pokémon", a species the site cannot give, "not a species the site can give"), an item none of is left in its bag or Showdown does not know, an ability a snapshot's Pokémon cannot have or, on a free member, one the ability editor does not offer, a nature or a move Showdown does not know. When nothing matches, no team is made. When every set came in from a snapshot whole, the new team opens at once; otherwise the dialog shows what came in free and what was left out, and its "Open the team", or closing it, opens the new team.

## The Analysis Module

PokeGraph (`~/pokegraph`, a local vanilla-JS app with a Python proxy) is ported to TypeScript as one analysis module the site shares, scoped with the owner on 2026-10-17: the synergy, weakness, resistance and coverage graph; archetype classification with its fingerprint radar and bulk × offense map; the teambuilding checklist; member cards with real stats, pool percentiles and roles; the defensive and offensive matrices, as type multipliers or weighted by the pool; and the pool analyzer. The team builder's [Analysis](#the-analysis), its [Pool](#the-pool) and [Compare](#compare) read it. Until 2026-10-25 it also served Team Analysis, a page of its own where a reader pasted a Showdown team, with its pasted team, compare slots and pool kept in the browser; Compare over stored teams replaced it, and the site deletes that tool's old drafts from the browser on load (`src/boot.tsx`). pokepast.es links, PokeAPI, PokeGraph's `tools/build_data.py` and its Python server stayed out.

The module is `pkmn/site/src/analysis/`, with two of its files in `shared/` since the second performance pass (2026-10-25), so the PC route can read them on the server: `shared/dex.ts` (generations, availability, a species' tags; the 18 types themselves are in `shared/mon.ts`), `shared/chart.ts` (type matchups, matchup abilities, move special cases), `paste.ts` (the Showdown parser), `member.ts` (real stats, moves, move tags), `pool.ts` (the pool, its statistics and legality), `stats.ts` (the pool's distributions, histograms, type shares and stat correlations), `team.ts` (defence, synergy, offence, weighted values, archetype, roles, checklist), `compare.ts` (Compare's scoreboard and damage calc) and `sprite.ts` (members' sprite URLs). Its panels are shared components ([Site Design System](../reference/design-system.md)). The team builder's `src/teams/analysis.ts` keeps only what reads its own members (speed order, defensive chart, coverage), on the shared chart and types: those functions take the builder's members and slots, and moving them into the module would make it depend on the builder.

A team reaches the module through its Showdown text, the one Copy for Showdown writes, read by the shared parser as PokeGraph's `parser.js` does (header, ability, level, Tera type, EVs, IVs, nature, moves; level 100 when no `Level:` line) in the pool's generation, so members with an unknown species are left out, with the export's real IVs and the league's 1 EV in every stat, which changes no Level 50 stat.

### Data

Game data is `@pkmn/dex`, through `Dex.forGen(n)` for the pool's generation, in place of PokeGraph's PokeAPI data and its generated `pokedex.json` and `typecharts.json`. Checked 2026-10-17 against `@pkmn/dex` 0.10.11: `forGen` returns past typings and stats (Gen 5 Clefable is Normal with 85 SpA; Gen 9's is Fairy with 95), Gen 1's single Special stat, the type-based physical/special split (Gen 3 Crunch is Special), species `tags` for Sub-Legendary, Restricted Legendary and Mythical, and `nfe` for not fully evolved. It also keeps later content in earlier generations (Moonblast and the Fairy type exist in `forGen(5)`), so moves, abilities and types are filtered by their `gen`, as PokeGraph's `notInGen` did.

**Matchup abilities.** One table serves the whole site, PokeGraph's: Levitate and Earth Eater (Ground), Flash Fire and Well-Baked Body (Fire), Water Absorb and Storm Drain (Water), Volt Absorb, Lightning Rod and Motor Drive (Electric), Sap Sipper (Grass) as immunities; Dry Skin (immune to Water, ×1.25 from Fire); Thick Fat (Fire and Ice), Heatproof and Water Bubble (Fire) and Purifying Salt (Ghost) as halving; Fluffy (×2 from Fire); and Wonder Guard. An ability counts only from Gen 3 and in a generation that has it. The team builder reads it in Gen 9, which changes nothing there: the abilities it adds are Gen 7–9 ones, and the mod only rolls the 164 Gen 1–5 abilities ([Save Export](save-export.md)).

**The pool.** A pool is the species and formes Showdown lists for its generation that it has not marked as later content (`isNonstandard` "Future"; "Past" ones stay, since a Gen 9 Custom Game fields them), leaving out battle-only formes, Megas, Primals, Gigantamax and Totem formes, any forme whose types and base stats are its base species' (Gastrodon-East, the Deerling seasons), and any forme with its base species' stats that a held item makes (Arceus's and Silvally's types, Genesect's Drives, Ogerpon's masks). A pool can also hold species to an earlier generation than its data (`speciesGen`, the pool bar's Species up to row). Legendary is Showdown's Restricted Legendary or Sub-Legendary tag, mythical its Mythical tag, not fully evolved its generation's `nfe`. Moves follow the same line: a "Future" move is ignored, as PokeGraph's `notInGen` did, and a "Past" one counts. The pools come out a little larger than PokeGraph's PokeAPI-built ones (fully evolved, any BST: 647 against 601 in Gen 9, 363 against 346 in Gen 5), and PokeGraph's four sample teams got the same archetype ranking, within 0.01, in both generations, measured on 2026-10-17 against PokeGraph run on its own cached data.
