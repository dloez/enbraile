# Team Builder

The team builder is where a player of the group builds their teams, stored in their account on [the pkmn site](site.md): `/teams` lists every team, and `/teams/<id>` builds one from one of the player's snapshots ([Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)), six member cards by default and up to eight, edited in place beside the team's analysis of every member or a pick of six, with the snapshot's items and Mini Setas as its limits, and copies it to and pastes it from Showdown. Every signed-in reader sees every team; only its owner changes it. It is the second half of Site v2's team building, scoped with the owner on 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23, and replaced Open Sheet, which the group used for series 1's open-sheet fights.

**Where it came from.** Open Sheet (scoped 2026-10-15) was a route of the site, `/open-sheet`, that read the JSON a player's export wrote, one browser tab per import, showed each in a PC with a summary and a bag, and built named teams in that PC, stored in the reader's browser (a Dexie database over IndexedDB). Series 1's final tournament is played open sheet ([Series 1 Rules → Final Tournament](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#final-tournament)): each player shares their Pokémon with their stats, abilities and the bag items that matter in a fight, and each builds from there. Snapshots stored on the site took over the imports, and the team builder took over the teams: on 2026-10-25 `/open-sheet`, its import tabs and Dexie went, and the site deletes the browser's old `open-sheet` database on load (`src/main.tsx`). Its PC, summary and bag became the site's PC page; its team rules, analysis and Showdown text are the team builder's, below.

**Not yet:** a team built from several snapshots or any player's, free members and "Any Pokémon", a size fixed by a tournament, a pool per team with Speed Tiers and Tera, Compare, and the trainer row's Teams tile opening a trainer's teams are still to come in Site v2's work in progress.

## What It Reads

A team's one source is a snapshot, the [Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md)'s export, read in the browser as `snapshotSchema` in `shared/snapshot.ts`, whose first three keys (`pokemon`, `items`, `tms`) are the `Sheet` every piece reads. In a Pokémon every key but `species` reads as absent when it is missing or not of its type, as for an old export ([Snapshot Format → Old Exports](../reference/snapshot.md#old-exports)). Each Pokémon, item and TM is looked up by `src/teams/dex.ts`.

Names, descriptions, base stats and the type chart come from `@pkmn/dex`, Showdown's data, Gen 9. Fights are Showdown "[Gen 9] Custom Game" ([Series 1 Rules → The League](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#the-league)), and several Gen 1–5 Pokémon gained base stats in Gen 6+, so Showdown's Gen 9 data is what the fight runs; PokéAPI's cartridge data is not, and the export's IDs already follow Showdown.

Showdown's types and base stats drive the analysis, since Showdown sets a species' types from its own data whatever the save says. The summary shows the export's `types` and flags a species whose export types differ from Showdown's; the mod's types are modern (Clefairy is `FAIRY` in the mod's `types.txt`), so a mismatch should be rare. An ID `@pkmn/dex` does not know is shown by its raw ID with a marker and kept out of the analysis; `tinymushroom` is the one expected case and gets a hand-kept label, "Mini Seta (Tiny Mushroom)", without the marker.

Sprites are Showdown's Gen 5 static set, which fits the Gen 1–5 roster, shiny and female where they apply, with a "?" in place of an unknown species'; item icons come from `@pkmn/img` too.

## Stored Teams

A team is a `team` row (`api/_lib/schema.ts`, migrations `drizzle/0008_teams.sql` and `0009_team_size.sql`): its owner (a player; deleting the player deletes their teams), its name, its size (6 to 8, 6 by default), its slots as jsonb, one per place, and when it was made and last changed; the database holds the size between 6 and 8 and the slots to that many, and the API sets the size from the slots it is given (`teamSlots` in `shared/team.ts` takes 6 to 8). Its sources are `team_source` rows, the team and a snapshot with a position, so a team names its snapshots explicitly; this entry gives every team exactly one. A slot is empty or a member (`teamMember` in `shared/team.ts`): the snapshot it comes from (`source`), the Pokémon's index in that snapshot's `pokemon` and its species, its item, its picked ability (null while it keeps its current one) and four move IDs. A member whose index no longer holds that species, after its snapshot was replaced by a new upload, or whose source is not one of the team's, is treated as an empty slot holding no item, and is dropped on the next save. Deleting a snapshot deletes its `team_source` rows, and a team left with no source shows "This team's snapshot was deleted".

The routes are `api/_lib/teams.ts`, schemas in `shared/team.ts`:

- `GET /api/teams` (`?owner=<id>` for one player's) lists every team, last changed first, each with its owner, its sources (player, series, milestone) and its members' species.
- `GET /api/teams/<id>` answers the team with its sources' snapshots and missing-field reports, and whether the reader may change it.
- `POST /api/teams` makes a team for the signed-in player, on the snapshot it names or else on their latest snapshot of the running series, named the lowest free "Team n", empty or with the slots it carries. It answers 403 to an admin (admins have no teams) and for a snapshot that is not the player's own, and 400 when the player has no snapshot in the running series ("Upload a snapshot to the running series first").
- `PUT /api/teams/<id>` changes the name or the slots, and with the slots the size, and `DELETE /api/teams/<id>` deletes the team; both answer 403 to anyone but the owner. A slot whose member's source is not one of the team's is refused with 400.

`npm run seed` makes Red a "Seed team" of the first three Pokémon of Red's latest Series 1 snapshot, when there is one.

Each team also counts on the series page and the profile: a trainer row's Teams tile is how many teams the trainer owns, and a profile banner's Teams box how many of the player's teams have a source in that series (`trainersOf` in `api/_lib/series-data.ts`, `api/_lib/profiles.ts`).

## The Team List

`/teams` (`src/pages/teams.tsx`, after `teams-list.dc.html` and `teams-list-phone.dc.html`), the header's Teams tab, lists the signed-in player's teams under "Yours" and everyone else's under "Other trainers" ("No teams yet" when a group is empty); an admin sees one list, "Every team". Each row is the owner's badge, the team's name, "<owner> · " on others' teams before its sources ("Series 1 · Post-League", or "<player>'s <milestone>" for a snapshot not the owner's), a strip of its slots, one per place, six to eight (each member's sprite, an empty slot dashed, shorter on a phone above six), how many slots are filled ("4/6", "8/8", green when full, amber otherwise) and when it last changed ("Changed today", "yesterday", "n days ago", else "in <month>"); the whole row opens the team. "New team" makes one on the player's latest snapshot of the running series and opens it, or says why not.

## The Team Page

`/teams/<id>` (`src/pages/team.tsx`, after `team.dc.html`, `team-member.dc.html`, `team-phone*.dc.html`) shows one team, editable by its owner and read-only to everyone else.

**Its head.** A "Teams" link back to the list; the team's name, which its owner renames in place by clicking it (1 to 60 characters, Enter or leaving the field saves, Escape cancels); on another player's team a chip "<owner>'s team" in their colour; and the source's chip, "<player>'s PC · <milestone>", with the Mini Setas left. Beside them, for the owner, the **Members 6 · 7 · 8** switch, setting the team's size, its sizes below the last filled slot disabled, so taking a size down never drops a member; then Paste and Delete (confirmed in a dialog) for the owner, and Copy for Showdown for anyone.

**The cards.** The slots are a two-column grid filling the page's height from `lg`, three rows for six and four for seven or eight, the column scrolling (one column below `sm`). A member's card has a header row (its sprite, name, Showdown types, level and nature, the source's tag "<player>'s PC" in their colour, and a chevron) that opens the member's summary in the panel, or closes it back to the analysis; a button to take it out; its item and its ability (with a Mini Seta mark when the picked ability is not its current one); and its four moves, each with its type, name and power, a move it cannot learn in this save in the warning colour. On a read-only team the item, ability and moves do nothing and the take-out button is gone. An empty slot is a dashed "Add a Pokémon" for the owner and "Empty" for anyone else.

**The panel.** Beside the cards (from `lg`) the panel shows what is being looked at: the team's analysis by default ([The Analysis](#the-analysis)); the member's summary, the PC's Info · Stats · Moves over that member's set (its picked ability shown as in use, its moves as its current ones), under a link back to the analysis; or an editor, opened by the card's item, ability or a move, whose back button and whose choice return to where it was opened from, the summary or the analysis. The card being edited is outlined in cyan, and a member just put in a slot in yellow until the next action. Below `lg` the page has a Members · Analysis switch, and the summary and the editors open full screen.

- **Item:** the source's bag (`<player>'s bag`), Items and Berries pockets with a search, each item with the members holding it and how many are left; the selected item's description, "Give to <member>" (disabled as "<member> holds it" or "None left") and "Take its item". Only items Showdown knows are listed, so not `tinymushroom`.
- **Ability:** every one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, base or hidden, each marked "In game" (its current one) or "1 Mini Seta", with its description; a Mini Seta ability is disabled while the member keeps its current one and none are left.
- **Move:** "Replace <move>" lists, when the export carries the Pokémon's learnable moves, its current moves, then the Move Relearner's with the level each is learned at, then its TMs, each move once; without them, the bag's TMs then every move Showdown gives generations 1–5. Each row shows the type, name, category, power and Showdown's short description, a move already in another slot is disabled, a search narrows them, and "Leave the slot empty" clears it.

**Seven or eight.** At a size above six the panel's analysis heads with **Analyse: All <n> · Pick 6**, open to every reader since it only changes what they look at. Pick 6 analyses six of the members, the first six filled until the reader changes them: each card carries **In** or **Out**, an Out card fades, Out is disabled while six are in, and "<n> of 6 in" turns amber below six. The switch and the pick are the reader's own, kept per team in the browser's `localStorage` (`team-pick:<id>`, `src/teams/pick.ts`); a team at six analyses every member.

**Limits.** A member can hold an item only while the source's `quantity` of it is not used up by the team. In the game an NPC switches an ability for one Mini Seta, to any of the Pokémon's abilities, hidden included, so each member whose picked ability is not its current one needs one, counted against the source's `tinymushroom` quantity in the head's chip.

**Adding a Pokémon.** An empty slot opens a full-screen dialog, "Add to slot <n>", with the source's name and the team's strip, the slot being filled marked: the source's PC box with its search and type filter, the Pokémon already in the team faded, and the selected one's summary beside it (a bottom sheet below `lg`) ending in "Put in slot <n>", or "In the team". A new member starts with its current moves in its slots, in the save's order, no item and its current ability.

**Saving.** Every change writes the whole slots with `PUT`, one write after another in the order made, while the page keeps showing the latest slots; a failed write shows "Not saved: <reason>". A size change writes the slots, grown with empty places or cut back to the new size.

## Adding From the PC

The PC page ([Snapshots → The PC](site.md#snapshots)) stays a read-only view of a snapshot. On the signed-in player's own snapshot its summary ends in "Add to team", a menu "Add <Pokémon> to…" of the player's teams on that snapshot (each with its sprites and "n/6", or "Full", disabled) and "New team"; picking one adds the Pokémon to the team's first empty slot, or makes a team on that snapshot holding it, and opens the team (`src/teams/components/add-to-team.tsx`).

## The Analysis

The panel's Analysis, over every member or the pick of six ([The Team Page](#the-team-page)), has Summary, Graph, Archetype, Members and Matrices, Team Analysis's views in one column ([Team Analysis](team-analysis.md)), and "Full analysis" opens the same views full screen with the pool's chip. The team reaches the shared module through its Showdown text, the one Copy for Showdown writes, ranked in a pool of Showdown's Gen 9 data with species up to Gen 5, fully evolved (`SHEET_POOL` in `src/teams/components/sheet-analysis.tsx`). Summary adds the builder's own three panels (`src/teams/analysis.ts`, `src/teams/components/team-analysis.tsx`):

- **Speed order:** each member's Level 50 Speed from its IVs, as in the league's pokepastes: Level 50, real IVs, 1 EV in every stat ([Series 1 Rules → Pokepastes](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#pokepastes)). A member without IVs sorts by its IV 31 speed, then its IV 0 speed, and shows its range.
- **Defence:** for each of the 18 types, how many members are weak (above ×1), resist (below ×1) or are immune (×0), using Showdown's Gen 9 type chart over each member's Showdown types and its picked ability (its current one when none is picked). A type is a hole when more members are weak to it than resist or are immune to it.
- **Coverage:** which types the picked moves hit super-effectively on their own, and which types nothing covers.

Members with an unknown species are left out. The abilities that change a matchup come from the site's one matchup-ability table ([Team Analysis → Data](team-analysis.md#data)), read in Gen 9 like the type chart. Coverage counts a member's damaging picked moves, leaving out status moves, fixed-damage moves (Seismic Toss, Sonic Boom…), one-hit KOs, moves whose damage ignores type matchups (Counter, Mirror Coat, Metal Burst, Bide, Final Gambit, Psywave, Super Fang, Endeavor) and Hidden Power, whose type depends on IVs the export does not hold. A member with no damaging move picked stands in with its STAB types.

## Showdown Text

**Copy.** "Copy for Showdown" writes the team with `src/teams/showdown.ts` in the layout of Showdown's own export: `Nickname (Species) (M) @ Item`, with the nickname only when it differs from Showdown's species name, the export's gender whenever it is `M` or `F`, and the item only when one is held; then `Ability:` (the picked one, else the current one, left out when the export has none), `Level: 50`, `Shiny: Yes` when shiny, the league's `EVs: 1 HP / 1 Atk / 1 Def / 1 SpA / 1 SpD / 1 Spe`, `<Nature> Nature` when the export has one, `IVs:` with each IV below 31, and one `- Move` line per filled move slot. Members are separated by a blank line; empty slots, members whose index no longer holds their species and species Showdown does not know are left out. The button is disabled when nothing would be copied, and reads "Copied", or "Copy failed" when the browser refuses the clipboard, for a moment after a click.

**Paste.** The owner's Paste opens "Paste from Showdown", which the site's shared parser (`src/analysis/paste.ts`) reads and `src/teams/showdown.ts` matches to the team's snapshot, making a new team on it (`POST /api/teams` with the slots) and opening it. Each set is matched to a Pokémon of the snapshot by its species, preferring one whose nickname matches and skipping those already placed. Its item is given when the snapshot still has one left, its ability is picked when it is one of the Pokémon's `abilities`, and its moves fill the four slots whether or not the Pokémon can learn them; a typed Hidden Power reads as Hidden Power. Gender, shiny, nature, level, EVs and IVs are ignored, since the snapshot holds those; `===` team headers, a move's `/` alternatives after the first and any set past the eighth are skipped, and the new team's size is six or, for seven or eight sets matched, that many. The shared parser reads six sets for Team Analysis and up to eight for the team builder. Whatever the snapshot cannot back is left out and listed in the dialog with its reason: a species not in the snapshot or with every copy already placed, an item none of is left or Showdown does not know, an ability the Pokémon cannot have, a move Showdown does not know. When nothing matches, no team is made.

## Decisions

- **`@pkmn/dex`'s Gen 9 data, bundled, for names, stats and the type chart; Showdown's sprite server through `@pkmn/img` for sprites.** **Why:** the owner asked for a good source of base stats and sprites; Showdown's Gen 9 data is what the fight runs. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Showdown's types drive the analysis,** with a flag on an export-type mismatch. **Why:** forced by where the fight runs. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
- **An unknown ID is shown raw with a marker and kept out of the analysis;** `tinymushroom` gets a hand-kept label. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
- **Item quantities limit a team.** _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Any of a member's `abilities` can be picked, counted against the snapshot's Mini Setas.** **Why:** an NPC switches an ability for one Mini Seta, to any of the Pokémon's abilities. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **For an export with moves, a member's moves are picked from its current, relearner and TM moves, its current moves fill a new member's slots, and a move it cannot learn is shown in the warning colour.** **Why:** a move kept from a previous evolution is in no other list, and the team should start from what the Pokémon actually knows. _(owner, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-17)_
- **The team shows Level 50 stats, a defensive chart and offensive coverage, beside the shared analysis.** **Why:** the owner's choice; with the export's IVs they are exact for the league's pokepastes. _(owner, 2026-10-15; shared analysis 2026-10-17)_
- **The analysis rules** (the weak, resist, immune and hole thresholds, the speed order, the moves coverage leaves out, STAB stand-ins): build choices made while building Open Sheet's team page; the matchup abilities come from the site's shared table ([Team Analysis → Data](team-analysis.md#data)). _(settled 2026-10-16; shared table 2026-10-17)_
- **A team copies as Showdown text in Showdown's export layout,** Level 50, with its real IVs and the league's 1 EV in every stat. **Why:** the owner; it is the league's paste format and reveals nothing. _(owner, 2026-10-15; layout 2026-10-16; EVs 2026-10-17; IVs 2026-10-21)_
- **A pasted Showdown team becomes a new team,** matched by species and nickname, keeping only what the snapshot backs and listing the rest. **Why:** the owner asked for it, so a team built in Showdown's teambuilder can be checked against the stock. _(owner, 2026-10-17)_
- **Teams are stored in the account, every team seen by everyone and changed only by its owner; a team lists its sources, and the PC is read-only with "Add to team" its one link to teams.** **Why:** the owner: the PC records what the save holds and the team is what is edited, so each page does one job. _(owner, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23)_
- **Open Sheet's imports, tabs and Dexie database go, and the browser's old database is deleted on load.** **Why:** greenfield; series 1's PCs come back as uploads and teams by Showdown paste. _(owner, 2026-10-22)_
- **The team builder's build choices:** sources as `team_source` rows rather than ids in the team's jsonb, so a deleted snapshot takes its rows with it; each member names its source snapshot, so several sources need no change of shape; a new team only on the player's own snapshot (the PC's "Add to team" shows only on the player's own PC) until sources may be any player's; a team with no snapshot left kept and shown as such; whole-slot writes in order from the latest slots in memory, as Open Sheet wrote; the trainer row's Teams tile counting every team the trainer owns and the profile's Teams box those with a source in that series, as the entry worded them; the team's name renamed in place and Delete beside Copy, which the board leaves out but a stored team needs; a card's name kept on a row of its own, its level and nature beside the types, so a nickname is not cut short; the panel's analysis in one column with Team Analysis's existing views and a full-screen "Full analysis", rather than the board's redrawn panel. _(settled 2026-10-25)_
- **A team holds six members by default and up to eight, its size free for its owner, and its analysis reads every member or a pick of six.** **Why:** the owner: six may not be enough when a format bans before the fight. _(owner, 2026-10-25)_
- **The team size's build choices:** a `size` column held by checks to 6–8 and to the slots' length, set from the slots on every write rather than sent on its own; the size switch disabled below the last filled slot, as `team.dc.html` does, rather than moving members up; the Analyse switch and the pick kept per reader and per team in `localStorage`, since the board opens them to any reader and they change only what that reader sees, with the first six filled members as the default pick; a pasted team of seven or eight sets made at that size. _(settled 2026-10-25)_
