# Save Export

The export reads one `Partida N.rxdata` slot of a Pokémon Añil save zip a player uploads to [the pkmn site](site.md#snapshots) and makes its snapshot ([Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md)). Its code is the site's own, in `pkmn/site/api/_lib/export/`. This doc explains the snapshot's first three keys, `pokemon`, `items` and `tms`, which hold what a player shares with their rival for an open-sheet fight, the fainted Pokémon and the save details kept beside them ([Everything Else It Keeps](#everything-else-it-keeps)), and the TM rolls the export makes; the rest of the snapshot, what it reads of the save beyond them, is in [The pkmn Site → Snapshots](site.md#snapshots). Every name those keys carry comes from an outside source, Showdown, and never from the game's text, because the game's descriptions do not hold on other platforms, Showdown included, where the fights are played.

The export began as part 1 of Open Sheet, which the [team builder](team-builder.md) replaced: a Python script a player ran on their own save in WSL, or the owner ran on the save zips players sent, which wrote those three keys as one JSON file. It was scoped with the owner on 2026-10-15, from the leads of a Wonder session where the export was first discussed (2026-10-13) and a probe of the owner's own save (`Partida 1.rxdata`, 11.7 MB). The site ported it to TypeScript, and the script and its command line were retired on 2026-10-23, once players uploaded on the site.

## What Goes Out

**Pokémon.** The party and every PC box, in party then box order. Eggs are left out (the 16 mod eggs among them), and so are permafainted Pokémon (`@perma_faint` true): neither can be fielded. A permafainted Pokémon goes out in the snapshot's `fainted` instead, read the same way. Each Pokémon carries its species-form, nickname, gender, shiny, level, nature, its current ability, the abilities it can have under the randomizer, its types, its IVs, EVs and stats, its moves (below), and its personal ID: what sets its strength in a fight. Everything else the save holds of it goes in its `details` ([Everything Else It Keeps](#everything-else-it-keeps)).

**Abilities.** The ones it can have are the randomized ones, never the vanilla ones: its species-form's entry in `$randomized_data[:abilities]`, with `base` and `hidden` kept apart. A Pokémon whose Random Ability Capsule rolled it a list of its own (`$PokemonGlobal.random_abs_pokes[pokemon.id]`) gets that list, and `@forced_ability` overrides the current ability, as the game's `getAbilityList` and `ability_id` do (`P/Random Pokemon & Moves + Randomizer EX (Abilities)/008_RandomAbilityCapsule.rb:66-93`, `002_Abilities.rb:230-234`). Shedinja keeps its real abilities by design (`SPECIES_WITHOUT_RANDOM_ABS`, `000_Config.rb:219`, which also names `MEWTWO_5`, not an allowed species-form); they ship in the export's data and are used with no notice.

The current ability follows the game's load order: a Random Ability Capsule roll first (`008_RandomAbilityCapsule.rb` wraps `ability_id` last), then `@forced_ability`, then `@ability`, then the table. A capsule list that is empty is skipped, as the game skips it (`008_RandomAbilityCapsule.rb:75-93`), so the ability comes from `@forced_ability` or `@ability`; the possible abilities stay that empty list, which is what the game's `getAbilityList` returns, and the table step reads that empty list. The capsule only rolls an empty list for a Pokémon whose randomized abilities are empty, and then clears `@ability` and `@ability_index` (`008_RandomAbilityCapsule.rb:16-53`), so the game's own table step finds nothing either. `@ability` stays nil until the game first calls `ability_id`; the export then works it out the same way the game would, from the species-form's table entry, `@ability_index`, and `@personalID & 1` ([Randomizer](../../games/anil/reference/randomizer.md), "A starter's ability" and "A trainer Pokémon's ability", the second matched on 77 trainer Pokémon).

**Other Pokémon fields.** The form is `@forced_form`, else `@form`, as the game's `species_data` reads it. `shiny` is `@shiny`, or worked out from the personal ID and the owner's ID as `shiny?` does when it is nil. The nature is the one that sets the stats, `@nature_for_stats` when a mint set it, else `@nature`, as `nature_for_stats` reads it: a Showdown nature only changes stats, so the minted one is the one that holds in the fight (two Pokémon of the probed save are minted). `@level`, `@gender` and `@nature` are filled when the game makes or registers a Pokémon, and none was nil in the probed save; one that is nil goes out as null with a notice, since working it out needs growth rates and gender ratios the export's data does not hold.

**IVs, EVs and stats.** The IVs are the ones its stats use, as the game's `calcIV` reads them: `@iv`, with 31 for a stat `@ivMaxed` marks (Hyper Training; none in the probed save). The EVs are `@ev`. The stats are the ones the game stores and its summary shows, `@totalhp`, `@attack`, `@defense`, `@spatk`, `@spdef` and `@speed`, at the Pokémon's own level. The game only recalculates them on events such as a level-up, so they can lag EVs gained since: the probed save's Poliwrath, its EVs gained at the level cap, stored stats a few points below what its EVs give. They are what the player sees in game, so they go out as stored; the fight's Level 50 stats are worked out by the site from the IVs and nature. A Pokémon whose IVs, EVs or stats the save lacks gets that field as null, with a notice (none in the probed save).

**Items.** Pockets 1 (Objetos) and 5 (Bayas) whole, with their quantities, and the TMs of pocket 4, without HMs. The held item of each party and box Pokémon that is not an egg, permafainted ones included, is added to the items as if it were in the bag, under the same pocket rule, so a held Mega Stone (pocket 6) is left out. Pocket 1 carries some noise (evolution stones), but taking it whole needs no hand-kept list of what counts as competitive, and Showdown fights have no bag use, so Obj. Batalla never matters. An item's pocket is the one `items.dat` gives it, the same table for bag and held items, so a held pocket 4 item goes out only when it is a TM. Items are merged by Showdown ID with their quantities summed, and a TM listed twice goes out once.

**TMs.** Each carries its TM item and the move it teaches: the save's `tm_move_map` entry when it has one, otherwise the item's own move in `items.dat`, as `GameData::Item#move` does ([Game Data](../../games/anil/reference/game-data.md), "Data the Mod Changes"). Which Pokémon can learn each TM is left out of the TM list on purpose: the owner dropped TM compatibility on 2026-10-13. Each Pokémon carries the bag TMs it can learn instead (below).

**Moves.** Each Pokémon carries the moves it knows now, its current four (`@moves`) in slot order, and the moves it can learn at the game's move relearner and from the bag's TMs, as the relearner's `generate_move_list` builds them (`scripts/409_BetterMoveRelearner.rb:315-339`). The relearner moves are its `@first_moves` (the relearner teaches them since `MECHANICS_GENERATION` is 9), then its rolled learnset's moves at or below its level, each move once, with the lowest level each is learned at (`levels`), so the summary can show it. The learnset is the species' entry in `$PokemonGlobal.random_moves`, under the Pokémon's form when the save's `@different_movesets_per_form` is true (the Randomlocke sets it) and form 0 otherwise, as the plugin's `getMoveList` reads it (`P/Random Pokemon & Moves + Randomizer EX (Abilities)/001_Main.rb:566-591`). The TM moves are those of each TM in pocket 4 the Pokémon is compatible with and that the relearner list does not already hold, as `pbGetTMMoves` lists them (`SHOW_MTS_MOS_IN_MOVE_RELEARNER` is true), each TM's move read as in TMs above. Unlike the relearner, neither list drops the moves the Pokémon already knows, since they stay learnable. A current move can be in neither list: one learned before evolving, which the evolved species' learnset lacks, or the outside move a new Pokémon can be given; the current four are exported for that reason, so the site can offer them too.

Compatibility is rolled per species, without form, the first time the game checks a TM against it (70% for a move of its own type, 40% otherwise, `001_Main.rb:593-616`), and the roll is kept in `$PokemonGlobal.tm_compatibility_random` and written at once to a side file beside the save, `tm_compatibility_<slot>.dat`, named after `$player.save_slot` with every character but letters and digits turned into `_` (`001_Main.rb:221-253`). On load the game takes the side file over the save's copy when the file exists (`001_Main.rb:817-844`), so the export does the same, falling back to the save's copy, with a notice, when the side file cannot be read. A TM whose move the Pokémon already knows counts as compatible, since it can already use the move; the game keeps no roll for such a pair (in the owner's save, every TM Kingdra, Staraptor and Dewgong lacked a roll for was one of their current moves). Every other bag TM has a roll by the time the moves are listed, since the export rolls the missing ones first ([Rolling TMs](#rolling-tms)). A learnset the save has not rolled gets a notice too, and its relearner moves are then its first moves only (none in the probed save).

## Rolling TMs

The game rolls a TM's compatibility with a species only the first time it checks one against the other, so a save holds rolls only for the pairs its player happened to check: the owner's save of 2026-10-21 lacked 1,793 of the pairs between its 117 bag TMs and 56 Pokémon. The export rolls them itself every time, with no option: for each Pokémon it exports, permafainted ones included, each TM of the bag whose move has no roll for the Pokémon's species, at the game's odds, 70% when the move's type is one of the Pokémon's types and 40% otherwise (`001_Main.rb:593-616`). The types are its species-form's, as the game's `self.types` reads them, and the move's type ships in the export's data. A pair whose move the Pokémon already knows is rolled too: the game rolls it the first time it checks another Pokémon of the species, and the export counts a known move as compatible whatever the roll.

Nothing else is rolled. The game rolls the whole ability table the first time it reads any ability, and a species' learnset when a Pokémon of it is made or evolves, so the Pokémon a save holds have both already; a learnset found missing keeps its notice. Evolutions are not randomized in the Randomlocke, and the export never evolves a Pokémon: players are expected to evolve theirs, and one that has not keeps its notice.

A roll counts only once the game keeps it: the game's random numbers cannot be foreseen, so a pair it rolled again could come out the other way, and the sheet would promise a TM the Pokémon cannot learn in game. The export hands its rolls over through the side file: the rolled zip it makes holds, in the slot's `tm_compatibility_<slot>.dat`, the slot's existing rolls followed by its own. The game loads that file over the save's copy and carries it into the save the next time the player saves. The export never writes the save itself, which would need a writer that round-trips all of it; the side file is only a hash of species to `[move, compatible]` pairs, and the export's small Marshal writer writes it byte for byte as Ruby does (checked on the owner's side file, 2026-10-21). The game must be closed while the player extracts the rolled zip: a running game keeps its own copy and writes it whole over the file on its next roll. The site keeps the rolls with each snapshot, hands the rolled zip back for download, and never rolls a pair again for a zip it has already exported ([The pkmn Site → Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)).

## Everything Else It Keeps

The owner asked on 2026-10-27 for the export to keep as much of the save as it can, shown or not, so a later feature can use it without a new upload. So besides what a fight needs it keeps:

- **The fainted Pokémon whole.** `fainted` holds each permafainted Pokémon with every field a living one has, its game species-form beside it, and a Showdown species of null rather than being left out when it has none. Nothing about them is a notice: a missed evolution or an ability it cannot work out does not matter for a Pokémon that cannot be fielded. Their species' TMs are rolled like any other's, so their `tms` mean the same as a living Pokémon's.
- **Each Pokémon's `details`:** its game species-form and form, its place (party slot, or box and slot), its held item, current HP and status, EXP, happiness, Poké Ball, the nature a mint replaced, its ability index, its current moves with their PP and PP Ups, its first moves, its original trainer, where, when and at what level it was obtained and hatched, Pokérus, markings, ribbons, contest stats, its Exp. Share flag and whether it is a super shiny.
- **The save's `details`:** the trainer's gender, trainer type, Coins, Battle Points and soot; each box's name and size; the player's map and position; when the game was started and last saved (Ruby `Time`s, read from their marshalled form as UTC); the step and save counts; the game's version; the species the Pokédex marks seen and owned; every number `GameStats` holds; the challenge's rules and outcomes; the Pokévial's charges; each Hall of Fame team; and the items found (`item_log`).

The field paths are in [Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md#details). These keep the save's own IDs (game species, item and move IDs), with a Showdown ID beside an item or move where one exists, since nothing reads them in a fight. A snapshot exported before 2026-10-27 has neither `details`, and its `fainted` holds only species, nickname, level and personal ID; the site reads it as before.

## Showdown IDs

The export names species-forms, abilities, items, moves, natures and types only by their Showdown IDs: the English Showdown name with everything but letters and digits removed, lowercased (`Choice Band` → `choiceband`), so any Showdown data source can be looked up by them. Showdown's data is what actually runs in the fights. PokéAPI was the alternative; its descriptions are the cartridge games', and carrying multilingual names was weighed and given up. The game's own names and descriptions are never exported.

Anything with no Showdown counterpart is left out with a notice naming its game ID, since it cannot exist in a Showdown fight and nothing may vanish silently. An item has a Showdown ID only when that ID is in Showdown's own item data, `data/items.ts`: the mod's names table (`showdown.txt`) covers 790 of the game's 887 items, and 550 of them keep their ID once checked against that list, the 125 TMs and Tiny Mushroom among them. TMs are exempt from the check, since Showdown has no TM items; a TM's item ID (`tm01`) is a label, and what the rival looks up is its move. Tiny Mushroom (`tinymushroom`, the Showdown-style ID of its English name) is exempt too: Showdown does not model it, but in the game an NPC changes a Pokémon's ability for one Tiny Mushroom, so the site's team builder counts a player's stock. Every ability the mod can roll has a Showdown name, since the mod limits them to the 164 Gen 1–5 ones (the mod's `data/abilities.txt`), and so does every allowed species-form but one: `ARCEUS_9`, the ???-type Arceus (`QMARKS`), which Showdown lacks (the mod's `showdown.txt` goes from `ARCEUS_8` to `ARCEUS_10`).

## Notices

The export keeps a notice, in the snapshot's `notices` ([Snapshot Format](../reference/snapshot.md#top-level)), for:

- each item or Pokémon left out for having no Showdown ID, naming an item once however many times it appears, and each TM whose move has no Showdown ID (none in the probed save);
- each Pokémon whose rolled abilities it cannot find, which then goes out without abilities;
- each Pokémon whose current ability it cannot work out, or whose current ability has no Showdown ID (a `@forced_ability` or stored `@ability` outside the rolled Gen 1–5 set), naming that ability; the Pokémon goes out with its `ability` null;
- each Pokémon whose level, gender, nature, IVs, EVs or stats the save has not filled in;
- each Pokémon that has not evolved into a species its own species can evolve into, whatever the method, naming the target and every way to reach it;
- each Pokémon whose learnset has not been rolled; each current or learnable move with no Showdown ID, which is left out (none in the probed save), and a side file of TM compatibility that cannot be read;
- a save whose `@randomlocke_version` differs from the version the export's data was built for, the guard for the data shipped with the export.

The missed-evolution notice skips evolutions into Gen 6+, which the mod blocks ([Series 1 Rules → Gen 1–5 Limits](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#gen-15-limits)). An evolution whose parameter is a level counts once the Pokémon's level meets it; any other evolution (stones, trades, held items, happiness) counts always, since the player can meet it at any time and the save does not say whether they will. On top of that it checks whatever of the evolution's own condition the save holds: the gender for `LevelMale`, `LevelFemale`, `ItemMale` and `ItemFemale`, attack against defense for the stat comparisons, and the personal ID for `Silcoon` and `Cascoon`. Time of day, weather, place and happiness are not checked.

## Data Shipped With the Export

The export reads the save and nothing else: no game folder. It ships the little game data it needs as `data.json` beside its code: the game ID → Showdown ID table, the mod's types per species-form, each item's pocket, each TM's default move, each move's type, Shedinja's fixed abilities, the species' evolutions, and the Randomlocke version the data was built for, which the version notice compares the save's `@randomlocke_version` against. One set of data serves every player because everyone plays the same package ([Series 1 Rules → The Package](../../series/1/reference/rules.md#the-package)), and the probe confirmed that evolutions and types are not randomized in a real save (`@enable_random_evolutions` and `@enable_random_types` false).

A generator, `pkmn/games/anil/generate_export_data.py`, run by the owner only ([How to Run and Deploy the Site → Refresh the export's data](../how-to/run-and-deploy-the-site.md#refresh-the-exports-data)), builds that data from this repository alone, with no Wonder checkout: the mod's `pkmn/games/anil/src/anil/mod/data/showdown.txt` (built from PokéAPI's English names, covering species-forms, abilities, items, moves and natures), `types.txt`, and `species.txt` (the species the mod allows, which bounds the evolutions), the game's `Data/items.dat`, `moves.dat` and `species.dat` in `pkmn/games/anil/game/base/es-v4.14/`, the randomizer's `000_Config.rb` from `game/extracted/es-v4.14/`, and the `VERSION` constant of the mod's `sources.py`, read as text, as the Randomlocke version. The game files are the ones [`anil install`](../../games/anil/how-to/install-the-game.md) fetches into the gitignored `game/`. It also downloads Showdown's `data/items.ts` from the `master` branch of `smogon/pokemon-showdown` on GitHub and reads only its item keys. It writes the data as JSON into `pkmn/site/api/_lib/export/data.json`, only when it changed. It imports none of the `anil` project's code, so it stays a standalone script. It reads the three `.dat` files with `rubymarshal`, which handles them though not the save. Its build rules:

- Types come from `types.txt`, falling back to `species.dat` for species-forms outside it, for every species-form with a Showdown ID.
- A TM's default move is the TM's own `@move`; HMs are left out.
- A move's type is its `@type` in `moves.dat`, lowercased, for every move, since the save can map a TM to any move.
- An item whose Showdown ID is not a key of `items.ts` is dropped from the names table, unless it is a TM or Tiny Mushroom, so the export leaves it out with its usual notice.
- Shedinja's real abilities ship as `fixed_abilities`: the species-forms of `SPECIES_WITHOUT_RANDOM_ABS` that have a Showdown ID, with their `species.dat` abilities.
- Every evolution ships, whatever its method, except `None` (no evolution) and `Shedinja`, which adds a Pokémon rather than evolving one; it is kept only when its target is in the mod's `species.txt`, the list the mod's `check_evolution_internal` checks, so evolutions the mod blocks never reach the export.

## Reading the Save

The save is Ruby Marshal, read by a reader written for the export (`marshal.ts`). `rubymarshal` fails on the owner's real save ("invalid link destination: Object id 5837 is not yet unmarshaled"): the save links back to objects still being read, which `rubymarshal` does not support. A minimal reader that registers each object before reading its contents reads the whole save.

What the three keys read sits under the save's top-level hash: `player` (`@party`), `storage_system` (`@boxes`, each with `@pokemon`), `bag` (`@pockets`, index 0 empty), `randomized_data` (`abilities`, 1,519 entries in the probed save), and `global_metadata` (`@tm_move_map`, each value a `GameData::Move` with `@id`; `@random_abs_pokes`, nil in the probed save; `@randomlocke_version`, 1). Field layouts are in [State](../../games/anil/reference/state.md).

The export is safe to repeat: it reads the zip and never writes the save, and beside the player's zip it writes only the rolled zip. The rolled zip is the player's zip entry for entry, other slots, backups and the game's photos copied back with their contents as stored, with only the slot's side file replaced by the rolled one, or added when the zip had none ([The pkmn Site → Snapshots](site.md#snapshots)). Anything played between zipping the save folder and extracting the rolled zip is lost; that is accepted, since by tournament time a player's run is usually done. A save caught while the game writes it fails to parse, and the export fails with it.

## Decisions

- **The export's purpose:** what a player shares for an open-sheet fight, read by the site; every name from an outside source, never the game's text. **Why:** the game's descriptions do not hold on Showdown, where the fights are played. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Party and every box go out; eggs and permafainted Pokémon do not.** **Why:** neither can be fielded. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_ Permafainted Pokémon go out apart, in `fainted` (below).
- **The export keeps as much of the save as it can:** the fainted Pokémon with every field a living one has, and a `details` object on each Pokémon and on the snapshot with what the save holds beyond a fight. **Why:** the owner wants every value retrieved now, shown or not, so later features can use it. Which fields, their names, the save's own IDs in `details`, no notices about fainted Pokémon, and their TMs rolled too are the implementer's choices. _(owner, 2026-10-27)_
- **Each Pokémon carries species-form, nickname, gender, shiny, level, nature, current ability, possible abilities, types, IVs, EVs and stats, its moves, and nothing else of its strength.** **Why:** the owner's choice: an open sheet now shares everything that sets a Pokémon's strength, on every run. _(owner, 2026-10-21; replaces 2026-10-15's "no stats, IVs or EVs", whose why was that final stats would let the rival work back the IVs and EVs)_
- **The IVs are the effective ones** (31 where `@ivMaxed` marks a stat), **and the stats the stored ones, however stale.** **Why:** the effective IVs are what the stats and a Showdown paste use; the stored stats are what the player sees in game. _(settled 2026-10-21)_
- **Possible abilities are the randomized ones,** with a capsule roll's own list and `@forced_ability` honoured as the game does, and Shedinja's real abilities with no notice. **Why:** the owner asked for the randomized ones; the per-Pokémon list and the forced ability are forced by the game's code. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **A current ability the save has not filled in is worked out** from the table entry, `@ability_index` and `@personalID & 1`, as the game does. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
- **Items are pockets 1 and 5 whole plus pocket 4's TMs, with held items added under the same rule.** **Why:** no hand-kept list of competitive items is needed, and Showdown has no bag use. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Held items of permafainted Pokémon count too,** added like any other held item although the Pokémon itself is left out. **Why:** the player can take the item off or release the Pokémon to get it back, and the group's rules allow both. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **A TM carries its item and its move, never its compatibility.** **Why:** compatibility was dropped on purpose; it goes out per Pokémon instead (below). _(owner, 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-15; per Pokémon 2026-10-16)_
- **Each Pokémon carries its learnable moves,** its relearner moves and its compatible bag TMs, always. **Why:** the owner wanted the moves the relearner and the TMs can teach, and a fully evolved save with all TMs as a test bench for them; the owner made them the default when the site took the export over, having taken them for it already. _(owner, 2026-10-16, then opt-in; always since 2026-10-23)_
- **The learnable moves follow the game's relearner,** with TM compatibility from the save slot's side file before the save, and moves already known kept in the lists. **Why:** forced by the game's code: the relearner is what the player can actually use, and the game loads the side file over the save. Keeping known moves is the implementer's choice, so the lists do not depend on the current four. _(settled 2026-10-16; the unchecked list dropped 2026-10-21, below)_
- **Each Pokémon also carries its current four moves.** **Why:** a move kept from a previous evolution is in neither the relearner nor the TM list, so without them the export missed moves the Pokémon can use. _(owner, 2026-10-17)_
- **A relearner move carries the level its learnset teaches it at,** in `moves.levels`, kept beside the move lists rather than in them, so a snapshot exported before it still reads, with no levels; the summary lists the relearner moves by level and warns over "Can learn" when a snapshot has none. **Why:** the owner missed the levels in the Moves tab; the export read them from the learnset and dropped them. _(owner, 2026-10-25)_
- **Every export rolls the bag TMs the game has not checked against each exported Pokémon's species,** at the game's odds, with no option; learnsets, abilities and evolutions are not rolled and no Pokémon is evolved, a missed evolution keeping its notice. **Why:** the owner wants the sheet complete without asking players to check every TM in game, and players must evolve their Pokémon themselves; the game has already rolled the learnsets and the ability table of the Pokémon a save holds. _(owner, 2026-10-20)_
- **The rolls go into the slot's TM side file, never the save.** **Why:** forced by the game's code: a roll counts only once the game keeps it, and the game loads the side file over the save; writing the save would risk corrupting it. _(settled 2026-10-20)_
- **The export takes a zip of a player's save folder and hands the zip back whole,** with the slot's side file updated and every other entry copied back. **Why:** most players are not technical, so a zip of the folder is what they can send; what they play in between is lost, accepted because tournament runs are usually done. _(owner, 2026-10-21)_
- **The `unchecked` list and the web app's "Not checked yet" TMs are dropped.** **Why:** once the export rolls every missing pair, the list is always empty. _(settled 2026-10-21)_
- **Showdown IDs only,** for species-forms, abilities, items, moves, natures and types. **Why:** Showdown's data is what runs in the fights; PokéAPI and carrying both were rejected. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **An item with no Showdown ID is left out with a notice.** **Why:** it cannot exist in a Showdown fight, and nothing should vanish silently. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **An item counts as having a Showdown ID only when that ID is in Showdown's own item data** (`data/items.ts`); items it does not model, such as Repel, Amulet Coin or Star Piece, are left out with a notice like any other item without one. TMs are the exception and keep their `tm` item ID as a label, since Showdown has no TM items and what the rival reads is the TM's move, itself a Showdown ID. **Why:** Showdown models only items with a battle effect, so the rest cannot matter in a fight. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **Tiny Mushroom goes out although Showdown does not model it,** as `tinymushroom` with its quantity and no notice, the one other exception to the item check. **Why:** an NPC changes a Pokémon's ability for one Tiny Mushroom each, and the team builder compares a build's need against the player's stock. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **A Pokémon with no Showdown species (only `ARCEUS_9`) is left out with a notice,** the item rule applied to Pokémon. **Why:** the agent's inference from the owner's item rule, for the owner to overturn. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
- **Notices** for what is left out, abilities not found, missed evolutions and a Randomlocke version mismatch. **Why:** the owner asked for the first three; the version notice guards the data shipped with the export. _(owner, 2026-10-15; kept in the snapshot since the site's port, where the script printed them)_
- **The missed-evolution notice covers every evolution method,** not only level-up ones: stones, trades, held items and happiness count always, level-style ones once the level is met. **Why:** the owner wants a warning whenever a Pokémon is missing an evolution, whatever the method. _(owner, 2026-10-16)_
- **The export reads the save only; the game data ships with it.** **Why:** the owner wanted no game folder, and every player plays the same package. _(owner, 2026-10-15)_
- **The generator reads the mod's data and version from `pkmn/games/anil/src/anil/mod/` and the game from `pkmn/games/anil/game/`,** with no Wonder checkout and no argument. **Why:** the mod and the game files now live in this repository, which is the single source of truth for the export's data. _(settled 2026-10-21)_
- **The generator's build rules** (rubymarshal for the `.dat` files, the version from `sources.py`, types with a `species.dat` fallback, TM moves from `@move` without HMs, every move's type from `moves.dat`, `fixed_abilities`, evolutions kept only into the mod's `species.txt`): the implementer's build choices. _(settled 2026-10-15)_
