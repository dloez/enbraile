# Progression

The order of Añil's eight gyms, as the game data and the running game give it: where each gym is, which switch its badge turns on, who leads it and the level caps on either side of it. The cap values are owned by the Cap per badge row of [Level Caps](level-caps.md); they are repeated here only to sit next to the gym they belong to. `harness.progression` loads this table and names the next gym from the badges. The [gates](#gates) on the way to every gym follow the gym table. Its facts were verified with Wonder's harness and are kept as recorded there; [Runtime](runtime.md) maps Wonder's commands, modules and paths to `anil`'s.

## Gyms

Order is the badge's index in `$player.badges` plus one. Each gym map is named "Gimnasio Pokémon" in `MapInfos.rxdata`, and its parent map is the town. The leader event is the gym map's event that starts the leader battle; its first page, after the battle is won, sets `$player.badges[order - 1]` and turns the badge switch on (Control Switches). The challenge cap is the cap while the gym is next (the cap after the badges before it); the cap after is the cap once its badge switch is on.

## Gates

A gate is a story step that stops the player on the way to a gym until it is done. A **wall** is a blocker that stays until its switch, variable or self switch is set: a touch strip whose page shows a message and moves the player back a tile, an NPC or rock on the same switch, or both; or door events that show a tile, or only say the door is closed, until their switch is on (gym 7). A **scene** is a touch strip that plays a story scene, usually a forced trainer battle, when the player walks over it, and sets its switch or variable as it plays; it cannot be walked past without playing it. A few scenes the route needs are started by talking to an NPC instead (Mr. Fuji on the Torre Pokémon's top floor and in his house); their events are the NPC. A few are autoruns that play on arrival once their condition holds (the assistant in Koga's gym, Sachiko in Ciudad Fucsia); their event is the autorun. A blocker the player opens on the spot with a key item that then sets its switch (the Snorlax on Ruta 12, woken with the Poké Flute) is kept as a wall on that switch, with the key item in `Gym.key_items`. A door the player opens on the spot by answering its question, which turns on the door's own self switch A (Silph S.A.'s magnetic door), is kept as a wall on that self switch. Each gym lists the gates between the gym before it (or the new game, from the bedroom with variable 55 at 0) and its leader, in route order; `harness.progression` holds them as `Gym.gates`.

No key item opens a route to gym 1, 2 or 3: no Cut tree, Rock Smash rock, water or Rock Climb wall lies on the only route, so the `*ITEM` key items given on the way (`POKERIDER` in Prof. Oak's Pokédex scene, map 29 event 19; `FLASHITEM` in Ruta 2's gate, map 33 event 15; `ROCKSMASHITEM` from Brock, map 42 event 16; `CUTITEM` from the S.S. Anne's captain, map 59 event 88) gate nothing here.
