# Shinies

How often Añil makes a Pokémon shiny under the Randomlocke, what raises the chance, and which Pokémon can be shiny. How players read these facts is in [Rules → Shinies](../../../series/1/reference/rules.md#shinies). The group's limits on shinies are in [Rules → Rules in Every Battle](../../../series/1/reference/rules.md#rules-in-every-battle); this doc only gives the facts. Its facts were verified with Wonder's harness and are kept as recorded there; [Runtime](runtime.md) maps Wonder's commands, modules and paths to `anil`'s.

Verified 2026-10-02 on `es-v4.14` with the Randomlocke mod and the bridge, by `wonder check shinies` (`packages/harness/src/harness/check.py`). It passed in session `20261002-135959` (save `shiny-2`), booted into the snapshot `shiny-new` (the bedroom right after the intro of a new game driven in session `20261002-135643`), first with smaller samples and then with the sample sizes below. It passed again in session `20261002-140502` (save `shiny-new-1`), a new game started by `wonder run --hidden` on an empty save and driven from the title through the intro to the bedroom, whose figures are the ones given here; that session then ran it inside `wonder check all`, where all 41 checks passed (the wild sample there: 201 shiny in 100,000) and `replay` replayed the session with no digest mismatch ([Runtime → Checks](runtime.md#checks)). The check reads settings and game data by eval, saves a snapshot, moves the player to map 3 (Ruta 1), makes Pokémon through the game's own code in batches of 2,000 per eval, and restores the snapshot. Each Pokémon made is counted twice: shiny by its personal ID (the roll below, recomputed by the check) and shiny as the game finally says (`shiny?`), so a shiny the personal ID does not explain came from the Visible Overworld Wild Encounters add-on's extra roll. Rows marked as read from code or data were not exercised in game; their citations are to `S/` (the extracted scripts), `P/` (the plugins) and the map files.

## The Shiny Roll

| Item | Value | How verified |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Base chance | `Settings::SHINY_POKEMON_CHANCE` is 65 (`S/002_Settings.rb:70`): a Pokémon is shiny when the two 16-bit halves of its personal ID XOR its owner's ID, XORed together, are below 65, so 65 in 65,536, about 1 in 1,008 (`S/277_Pokemon.rb:445-452`). The roll is fixed at creation: it follows the personal ID, which is drawn once | The check's read; for gift Pokémon the measured rate below |
| Super shiny | `Settings::SUPER_SHINY` is true. A Pokémon shiny by its personal ID is super shiny (square sparkles) when that value is exactly 0, 1 in 65 of them (`S/277_Pokemon.rb:461-470`) | The check's read; counts below |
| Forcing switches | Switches 31 ("Forzar shiny", every wild Pokémon shiny) and 38 (force super shiny) are off, and no map or common event turns them on | The check's read; a scan of every map's and common event's commands for those switches |

## Wild Pokémon

Every wild Pokémon is made by `pbGenerateWildPokemon` (`S/262_Overworld_WildEncounters.rb:383`), which ends by running the `on_wild_pokemon_created` handlers. Three of them can make it shiny, in this order: `make_shiny_switch` (switch 31), `shinyzador` (the Shiny Potion, below) and `hacer_shiny_a_salvajes`, the add-on's catch-chain roll.

| Item | Value | How verified |
|------|-------|--------------|
| The add-on's extra roll | `hacer_shiny_a_salvajes` (`P/Visible Overworld Wild Encounters/004_Cadenas_shinys.rb`) runs only when `salvajes_visibles_en_ow` is 1, which means visible wild Pokémon are **off**, the value the Randomlocke locks. It gives every wild Pokémon a second, independent shiny chance of 1 in `SHINYPROBABILITY` (1,000), on top of the personal ID roll; one in ten of its shinies is super shiny. So turning visible Pokémon off does not remove the chains: under the Randomlocke they are always on. With the option set to 0 (forced past the mod's locked setter) the extra roll stops | The check: `CHAINLENGTH` 10 and `SHINYPROBABILITY` 1,000 read; samples below |
| Wild rate | 100,000 wild Pokémon drawn from map 3's land table (`choose_wild_pokemon` then `pbGenerateWildPokemon`, nine random species), with no chain and no charm: 198 shiny, 1 in 505. 104 were shiny by the personal ID (99.2 expected, 1 in 1,008) and 94 by the add-on's roll; 12 were super shiny. 199.1 were expected at the combined rate, 1 in 502 | The check, session `20261002-140502` |
| Visible Pokémon on | 20,000 the same way with `@salvajes_visibles_en_ow` set to 0: 19 shiny (1 in 1,053; 19.8 expected at 1 in 1,008), all by the personal ID, none by the add-on | The check |
| Rate per battle | A wild battle meets one wild Pokémon, so at the measured rate a player meets 0.20 shinies per 100 wild battles: 0.59 in 300 (45% chance of at least one) and 1.98 in 1,000 (86%). How many wild battles a run fights is the player's; the figure scales with it | Arithmetic on the measured wild rate |

## Raising the Chance

| Item | Value | How verified |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Catch chains | The add-on keeps one chain, `$PokemonGlobal.catchcombo` (count and species). At the end of each wild battle (`P/Visible Overworld Wild Encounters/001_VOE script.rb:1488-1506`) a win or a catch adds one if the species is the chain's, or restarts the chain at 1 on the new species; fleeing or losing leaves it alone. When the wild Pokémon is the chain's species, the extra roll's odds improve every 10 in a row: 1 in 800 at 10–19, 600 at 20–29, 400 at 30–39 and 200 at 40 or more. The chain's messages show only when the "mensaje_shinys" option is 0; it starts at 1 | The check called the handler for win, win, catch, flee, loss on Rattata, then a win on Pidgey: the chain read 1, 2, 3, 3, 3, then 1 on Pidgey. 20,000 Rattata made with a chain of 40 Rattata: 117 shiny (1 in 171), 18 by the personal ID and 99 by the add-on's roll; 119.7 expected, 1 in 167 |
| Number battled | `HIGHER_SHINY_CHANCES_WITH_NUMBER_BATTLED` is true: once the Pokédex counts 50 or more battles against a species, its wild Pokémon have a 1.5–3% chance of 1 to 5 extra personal ID rolls (`S/262_Overworld_WildEncounters.rb:409-420`) | The check's read of the setting; the rest read from code |
| Shiny Charm | "Amuleto Iris", a key item. Held in the bag, it gives every wild Pokémon two extra personal ID rolls and halves the add-on's odds, to 1 in 500 with no chain (`S/262_Overworld_WildEncounters.rb:405-427`, `P/Visible Overworld Wild Encounters/004_Cadenas_shinys.rb`) | 20,000 wild Pokémon from map 3 with the charm added to the bag: 106 shiny (1 in 189), 62 by the personal ID (59.5 expected, 1 in 336) and 44 by the add-on; 99.3 expected, 1 in 201 |
| Getting the Shiny Charm | Only Professor Oak gives it (map 29, Laboratorio, event 20, page 4, on switch 242), when `contar_primeros_pokemon(1027)` counts exactly 1,027 species owned, the whole National Pokédex. The Randomlocke's random Pokémon come from 649 Gen 1–5 species, so in practice no player can get it. The item randomizer keeps it, since key items are never randomized | Read from the map data; the check read the 649 species in the allow-list and that `unrandomizable_item?(:SHINYCHARM)` is true |
| Poké Radar | Its shiny handler is registered, but no map or common event gives a Poké Radar (`POKERADAR`). The "PokéRadar" the player receives on map 28 (event 22, `RADAR`) is Añil's encounter-list screen (`S/412_Encounter_List_UI.rb:295-300`), not the shiny radar | A scan of every event's script commands |

## The Shiny Potion

| Item | Value | How verified |
|------|-------|--------------|
| What it does | "Poción Brillante" (`SHINYZADOR`): using it turns on switch 122, and the next wild Pokémon made is shiny and not super shiny, unless the add-on's roll, which runs after it, also hits (1 in 10,000); the switch then goes off (`P/Misc Scripts Añil/005_Shinyzador.rb`). The legendary battles' script uses it the same way (`P/Deluxe Battle Kit/[002] Midbattle Scripting/[005] Combates Legendarios.rb:501-505`) | With switch 122 turned on by eval, the next wild Pokémon from map 3 was shiny and not super shiny although its personal ID was not, and the switch read off afterwards |
| Where it comes from | The Battle Tower's BP shop (map 215, Torre Batalla, event 7) sells it for 80 BP. It is also in the item randomizer's pool of 576 items, so any randomized item found or received can be one, about 1 in 576. The alpha nests that name it as a reward (maps 14, 23, 110, 116, 155, 158, 189, 194, 199) give it through `pbReceiveItem`, which the randomizer replaces with a random item | The check: `bp_price` 80, pool size, in the pool, not kept by `unrandomizable_item?`; the shops and nests read from the map data. When the Battle Tower can be reached is not checked |

## Which Pokémon Can Be Shiny

| Item | Value | How verified |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Wild and static | Every wild Pokémon, including scripted battles started with a species and level (`S/260_Overworld_BattleStarting.rb:451-458`), roamers, and the alpha nests' raids, goes through `pbGenerateWildPokemon`, so it gets the personal ID roll, the add-on's roll and the Shiny Potion. Scripted battles that set their own wild Pokémon may override this | Wild measured above; the rest read from code |
| Gift Pokémon | Made with `Pokemon.new`: the personal ID roll only, about 1 in 1,008, with no add-on roll. The randomizer changes the species before the Pokémon is built (`P/Random Pokemon & Moves + Randomizer EX (Abilities)/001_Main.rb:381-415`), so the roll is made once and kept | 40,000 `Pokemon.new(:RATTATA, 5)`: 40 shiny (1 in 1,000), all by the personal ID; 39.7 expected |
| Guaranteed shiny gifts | Four gifts are made shiny on purpose (`make_shiny`), after the randomizer picks their species: Bill's Eevee (map 202, event 14), Cintia's Gible (map 205, event 6), Mirto's Larvesta (map 206, event 8) and Máximo's Beldum (map 207, event 7). Bill, Cintia and Mirto give theirs after a battle, Máximo his when spoken to; their maps are on the islands (Villa Ámbar, Ruta 29, Cabo Magenta, Villa Magenta). When they can be reached is not checked | Read from the map data |
| Starters | The three random starters are `Pokemon` objects made at the new game, so the personal ID roll only | The check: in session `20261002-140502` Cleffa, Charmander and Larvitar, none shiny, each matching its personal ID |
| Trainer Pokémon | Never shiny: the trainer loader sets every Pokémon's shininess from the PBS (`S/141_Trainer.rb:153-154`), none of the 1,841 PBS trainer Pokémon is marked shiny, and the randomizer keeps those objects. The one exception, read from code: a trainer's Mega Stone holder whose random species has no Mega Stone is rebuilt with `Pokemon.new` (`P/Random Pokemon & Moves + Randomizer EX (Abilities)/006_Trainers.rb:85-102`), which rolls again | The check: 28 trainers loaded through the randomizer, 69 Pokémon, none shiny |
| Shinies and the Nuzlocke | A shiny does not use up a map's encounter (the shiny clause), so a wild shiny can always be caught ([Nuzlocke](nuzlocke.md)) | The `clauses` check |
