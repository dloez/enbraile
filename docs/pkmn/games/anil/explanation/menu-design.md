# Menu Design

How the harness drives the screens the dialog layer hands off: the prompt chains (naming, move learning, evolution and the level-up stat boxes) wherever they open, the bag, party, Pokévial, save, fly and lens verbs through the pause menu, the Poké Mart's buy and sell verbs, the PC's `pc` verb and the move relearner's `relearn` verb. Scoped with the owner on 2026-10-02 and built in five slices, each verified in game before the next (screens readable, prompt chains, the pause-menu verbs, the Poké Mart, the PC), with flying and the Lente de la Verdad added on 2026-10-04 and the move relearner on 2026-10-09; this doc owns the layer's design as built. The code is `anil.harness.menu`, with the PC verb in `anil.harness.storage`, the steps it shares with dialog in `anil.harness.steps` and the state comparison in `anil.harness.changes`. It is copied from Wonder's menu design (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/menu-design.md`), where it was built and checked against the game, without Wonder's agent and control panel. Sessions named as evidence ran under Wonder's harness, whose commands, files and bridge module read `wonder` where `anil`'s read `anil` ([Runtime Reference](../reference/runtime.md)).

It builds on [Dialog Design](dialog-design.md) (the driver loop, answers and `decision`, transcript, changes, handoff) and [Runtime Design](runtime-design.md) (context, payloads, lockstep, `note`). Game facts cited here are owned by Menus (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md`) (each screen's layout and texts) and [Runtime → Context](../reference/runtime.md#context) (payloads and the context table), where each names how it was verified. The layer needs only Python in WSL and the existing bridge: no Windows-side pieces, and no secrets or config.

## What the Layer Does

Fifteen entry points:

- `continue_menu(driver, answers)` drives any open prompt chain to its end, wherever it opened: after a gift or a catch, after an egg hatches on a walk, after a level-up out of battle, mid-battle, or inside a bag verb that ended `decision`. In that last case it then closes the bag and the pause menu, so the game is back on the idle overworld and the verb can be issued again. It is refused when no chain is open.
- `use_item(driver, item, pokemon, count=1, answers=())` uses an item on the party Pokémon in slot `pokemon` (0–5): the pause menu, "Bolsa", the item's pocket and row, "Usar", then the party panel. A TM's "Has seleccionado" message and its "¿Quieres enseñarle…?" confirm (answered Sí) come before the panel. When the game opens its quantity picker (a Rare Candy), `count` is entered there; otherwise the panel is pressed `count` times, one use each. The prompt chains a use raises (stat boxes, move learning, evolution) are driven with `answers`, as `continue_menu` drives them.
- `give_item(driver, item, pokemon)` gives an item to hold: the bag, the item, "Dar", the panel. When the Pokémon already holds an item, the swap confirm is answered Sí, and the old item goes back to the bag.
- `take_item(driver, pokemon)` takes the held item back into the bag: the pause menu, "Pokémon", the slot, "Objeto", then "Guardar".
- `swap_party(driver, a, b)` swaps two party slots: the party, slot `a`, "Cambiar", then slot `b`.
- `set_exp_share(driver, pokemon, on)` turns Añil's per-Pokémon exp share on or off: the party, the slot, "Repartir Exp." and its confirm, answered Sí.
- `use_vial(driver)` heals the party with the Pokévial: the pause menu, its JUMPUP hotkey, the charges message and "¿Quieres curar a tu equipo?", answered Sí.
- `fly(driver, connection, town)` flies with the PokéRider to a landing point of the region map, named as the map names it ("Ciudad Verde", "ciudad carmin"): the pause menu, its JUMPDOWN hotkey ("[S] Volar"), the region map's cursor to the point, then C. It needs the world cache for the landing points (World → Flying (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/world.md` → Flying)).
- `use_lens(driver)` uses the Lente de la Verdad on the map: the pause menu, "Bolsa", the lens's row in the Key Items pocket and "Usar"; the bag closes and the plugin's message and animation play. It finishes once the lens is in use (`$scene@eye_of_truth_time` above 0), naming the frames left; it is refused while the lens is already in use.
- `save(driver)` saves into the game's current slot: the pause menu, "Guardar", then "Guardar en <slot>".
- `buy(driver, [(item, quantity), ...])` buys each item in turn on the mart's open buy list: the item's row, the quantity picker (none for a key item), and "¿Así que quieres…?", answered Sí. Then B leaves the list; with no items it only leaves the list.
- `sell(driver, [(item, quantity), ...])` sells each item in turn on the mart's open sell list, the bag: the item's pocket and row, the quantity picker when more than one is held, and "Puedo pagarte…", answered Sí. Then B leaves the list; with no items it only leaves the list.
- `pc(driver, [ops])` works the PC's storage box an event opened, one operation after another: `Withdraw(box, slot)` takes a box Pokémon into the party ("Sacar"), `Deposit(pokemon, box)` stores a party Pokémon in the box's first free slot (the party panel, "Guardar", then the box in "¿Dejar en qué caja?"), and `Move((box, slot), (box, slot))` picks a box Pokémon up ("Mover") and puts it down in an empty slot of any box ("Dejar"). Then B on the box and No to "¿Desea hacer más operaciones?" close it; with no operations it only closes the box.
- `relearn(driver, moves, answers=())` teaches each move in turn on the move relearner's open list, then leaves it: the move's row, C, "¿Enseñar …?" answered Sí, and the game's move learning, whose prompts are answered from `answers` as `continue_menu` answers them; then B and "¿Prefieres que … no aprenda un movimiento nuevo?" answered Sí. With no moves it only leaves the list. The list opens from a Pokémon Center assistant's "Recordar movimiento", after `choose_pokemon` picks the member and `continue_dialog` hands off (Menus → Move Relearner (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Move Relearner)).
- `choose_pokemon(driver, pokemon)` picks the party Pokémon in slot `pokemon` on a party screen an event opened (`pbChoosePokemon`, "Elige un Pokémon"), or leaves it with none picked when `pokemon` is left out: the slot, then C, or B. It finishes once the screen has closed, back in the event, which the caller carries on with `continue_dialog`. The store question a Pokémon received with a full party asks ("¿Qué quieres hacer con…?") opens this screen after "Añadir al equipo", to choose who goes to a box (Menus → Receiving a Pokémon with a full party (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Receiving a Pokémon With a Full Party)).

Every verb but the mart's, the PC's, `choose_pokemon` and `relearn` starts only on the idle overworld and closes everything it opened. The mart's start on the list an event opened, after dialog's `handoff` from the clerk's "Quiero comprar" or "Quiero vender", and leave the game back at the clerk's "¿Puedo ayudarte en algo más?", which the caller answers with `continue_dialog`. `pc` starts on the storage box, after dialog's `handoff` from "PC de alguien", and leaves the game back at "¿A qué PC quieres acceder?", which the caller answers with `continue_dialog`. `relearn` starts on the relearner's list, after dialog's `handoff`, and ends once the list has closed, back in the assistant's event, which on this build ends with no line. `choose_pokemon` starts on the party screen an event opened, after dialog's `handoff`, and is refused anywhere else, the pause menu's party screen included. The item is the game's ID (`RARECANDY`), a Pokémon its party slot (0–5), and a stored Pokémon its box and slot as `$PokemonStorage` indexes them (box 1 is "Caja 2").

A chain is the `special` contexts `naming`, `learn_move`, `forget_move`, `evolution` and `stats`. Dialog hands each of them off on its first tick, as it does every `special` context, so the usual sequence is a `handoff` from `interact` or `continue_dialog`, then `continue_menu`, then `continue_dialog` again if the event goes on. A battle hands them over the same way: the battle layer's calls end `handoff` at a chain, and `continue_battle` goes on after `continue_menu` ([Battle Layer Design → Results](battle-layer-design.md#results)). `anil.harness.menu` owns these contexts in battle too; every other battle context is the battle layer's.

Judgment stays out of the harness. The caller passes typed, ordered answers. Each prompt takes the first remaining answer of its kind, and a prompt with no fitting answer ends the call `decision` with the screen left open. Calls hold no state: each reads the live game, and calling again continues from wherever the game is.

## Answers

| Answer | Kind | Consumed by |
|--------|------|-------------|
| `Nickname(name)` | nickname | The naming screen: the entry is cleared, the name typed and read back, then RETURN |
| `Forget(slot)` | move | The forget-move screen: the cursor moved to `slot` (0–3) by reading `cursor` back, then C. The "forget a move?" question before it is answered Sí without consuming it |
| `Skip()` | move | "forget a move?" answered No, or "stop learning?" answered Sí. On the forget-move screen it presses B, which leads to "stop learning?" |
| `Evolve(allow)` | evolution | An evolution that can be cancelled, at its opening "¡Anda!": `True` lets the animation run, `False` presses B through it |

An evolution that cannot be cancelled (a stone's) runs through and consumes nothing. The stat boxes and the chain's messages need no answer: each is closed with C, as dialog's message step does.

## Results

`MenuResult` is `DialogResult`'s shape ([Dialog Design → Results](dialog-design.md#results)): `outcome`, `reason`, `started` and `tick`, `context`, `transcript`, `decision`, `unused` answers, `frames`, `changes` and `events`. Outcomes:

- `finished`: the chain closed and the game is back where it was opened from, with no prompt-chain context left. The reason names the context: the idle overworld, an `event`, a dialog `message`, or a battle's. A bag verb finishes back on the idle overworld once every use or the give is done, and so does each party, Pokévial and save verb once its one action is done. `set_exp_share` finishes at once, touching nothing, when the flag already matches. `buy` and `sell` finish back at the clerk's question once every item is bought or sold, and `pc` back at the PC's question once every operation is done. `relearn` finishes once every move is taught and the list has closed.
- `decision`: a prompt is open with no answer of its kind, or a `Forget` slot that does not exist. `MenuDecision` holds `kind` (`nickname`, `move` or `evolution`), the `prompt`, and as fits: `species` and `new_species`, the `move` to learn and the known `moves`, the shown `options` with `cursor`, and `can_cancel`. On the forget-move screen it also holds the member's `species`, the `move_id` to learn and the member's `personal_id`, so a caller can find the member and score the moves (since 2026-10-09).
- `failed`: the game refused, or would refuse, and the call backed out. A nickname the forced-nickname rule refuses (empty, or the species name ignoring case) or one outside the entry's length limits is not typed, and the screen is left open; the answer stays in `unused`. A bag verb fails when a use or give changed neither the Pokémon nor the item's count and consumed none of the caller's answers: "No tendría ningún efecto.", a Rare Candy at the level cap ("No tendrá ningún efecto."), "Chispa no puede aprender Ventisca." or "Chispa ya conoce Garra Dragón.". It also fails, before anything is used, when `count` is above the quantity picker's maximum. Without a picker, `count` is repeated presses, and the call fails on the first press that changes nothing (a Rare Candy with `count` 2 and one level left to the cap); the uses before it stay done and show in `changes`, as with `buy`, `sell` and `pc`. The reason is the game's last message, or the picker's maximum, and the bag and pause menu are closed first. The party verbs and `use_vial` fail the same way, judged by the state rather than by text: `take_item` when the Pokémon still holds its item (a full bag: "La Bolsa está llena. No se ha quitado el objeto del Pokémon."), `swap_party` when the two slots did not trade places, `set_exp_share` when the flag did not change, and `use_vial` when the charges did not drop (from the maximum when the game set the Pokévial up during the use; "El Pokévial está vacío. Recárgalo en el Centro Pokémon.", or "El Pokévial está bloqueado."). `fly` fails when the player is not on the landing tile once the menus have closed, with the game's last message as the reason, and when the region map shows another region than the landing's. `save` fails when the pause menu is still open after the save question, which is where a failed save leaves it ("El guardado ha fallado."). `buy` fails on the first item whose count in the bag did not rise by the quantity: the challenge's bans ("¡La regla "Compra de objetos curativos" te impide comprar objetos de curación en las tiendas!" for a healing item), "No tienes suficiente dinero.", or a quantity above the picker's maximum, which is what the money buys (at most 999); the picker is then left with B, which buys nothing. `sell` fails on the first item whose count did not fall by the quantity, such as "Oh, no. No puedo comprar MT02s." for an item the mart will not take. Either leaves the list before ending, so the game is back at the clerk's question; the items handled before the failing one stay bought or sold and show in `changes`. `relearn` fails when the member does not know the move once the game's move learning is done, as when the move was given up ("Shroud no ha aprendido Fachada.", the reason), or when a move is no longer on the list; it leaves the list first, and the moves taught before stay taught. `pc` fails on the first operation the boxes and party do not show done: a withdrawal when the party did not grow by one or the slot is not empty ("¡Tu equipo está lleno!"), a deposit when the party did not shrink by one or the box did not gain one ("La Caja está llena.", "¡Es tu último Pokémon!"), a move when the source is not empty or the target still is. It closes the box first, so the game is back at the PC's question, and the operations before the failing one stay done.
- `handoff`: an `unknown` context opened, logged to `unknown_contexts.jsonl`.
- `stuck`: no progress for `STUCK_STEPS` (3) steps, a cursor or value that never lands (a bag pocket, the box cursor, the quantity), or a press that does nothing it should (opens, closes, asks or swaps nothing), as in dialog; a question a verb or the learn-move chain answers itself that it does not know, or whose reply is not among the shown options; or a screen, row or command the verb expects that is not there: a pause-menu row, a bag or buy-list row holding another item, an item command, the party panel, the party reopened to switch, the buy or sell list back after an item, the box list's row, the storage box shown or back after an operation, the PC's leave question, a naming screen that closes or never reads the typed name, or menus that never close.
- `refused`: no prompt chain is open, or a name entry is open on the cursor grid (`$PokemonSystem.textinput` not 0), which the layer does not drive. A bag verb is refused off the idle overworld, for an empty party slot, an item not in the bag, a `count` outside 1 to the number held, an item not used on a Pokémon (neither `field_use` 1 nor a machine), a TM with `count` above 1, or, for `give_item`, an item that cannot be held (`can_hold?` false or `is_important?` true, as TMs are). `take_item` is refused for a Pokémon holding nothing or holding mail (the game hides "Objeto" then, `302_UI_Party.rb:1393`), `swap_party` for the same slot twice, `set_exp_share` when `expshare_enabled?` is false (it is never false on this build, Menus → Party (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Party)), `use_vial` with neither `VIAL` nor `EMPTYVIAL` in the bag, and `save` when the game has no save slot yet (`$player.save_slot` nil). `fly` is refused, before any input, for a town that names no landing point (the reason lists them), with no `POKERIDER` in the bag, with the game's own lines when a partner follows ("No se puede usar cuando hay alguien contigo.") or the map is not outdoors ("No se puede usar aquí."), for a landing whose map is not visited, and for one hidden behind its switch. `buy` is refused when the buy list is not open (a `transition` is waited through first), for a quantity below 1, an item not in the shown stock, or a key item (`is_important?`) with a quantity other than 1; `sell` when the sell list is not open, for a quantity below 1, or more of an item than the bag holds, summed over the call. `relearn` is refused when the relearner's list is not open (a `transition` is waited through first), for a move not on its list, which the reason names, or a move named twice. `pc` is refused when the storage box is not open (the party panel or a prompt over it counts as not open), for a box or slot out of range, or an operation that does not fit the boxes as the operations before it leave them: an empty slot to withdraw or move, a party slot with no Pokémon to deposit, a move onto a taken slot or onto its own slot.

`changes` is dialog's `StateChanges`, read at both ends of the call. Members now compare their nickname, held item and exp share as well, and the Pokévial's charges are compared too, so a naming, a give, an exp share toggle and a heal all show in `changes`. A swap shows as two changed slots, each listing every field that differs; a Pokévial run dry shows as `VIAL` 1 → 0 and `EMPTYVIAL` 0 → 1, both named "Pokévial". A save changes nothing that `changes` compares. `pc` alone compares the boxes too, as `boxes`: each box slot whose Pokémon was added, removed or changed, with the member fields above, so a move shows as one slot removed and another added.

## Steps

The message, choice, number, wait and cursor steps are dialog's, moved into `anil.harness.steps` so both layers call one copy. Each step checks the payload it acts on stays under the context it started in, so the same choice step answers a top-level `choice` for dialog and the learn-move confirm inside `special` for the menu layer.

| Newest payload in a chain context | Step |
|-----------------------------------|------|
| `naming` | Naming: refuse as `failed` before typing (see Results); clear the entry with BACKSPACE until `text` reads empty; type the name in one tick and wait for `text` to read it; press RETURN until the payload closes |
| `choice` (`learn_move`) | The confirm, recognised by its question (Menus → Prompt Chains (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Prompt Chains)), answered Sí or No through dialog's choice step |
| `menu` (`forget_move`) | Move the cursor to the slot and press C, or press B for `Skip` |
| `message` starting the evolution (`¡Anda!`) | The evolution step: close the message, then nothing, or B on alternate ticks until the next message opens |
| any other `message` (stat boxes included) | Dialog's message step |
| none, or anything else | Dialog's wait step |

Outside a chain context, a `transition` is waited through, since the chain's own fades (into the forget-move screen and the evolution scene) pass through one; in a battle, only while the battle's move learning (`152:Battle_ExpAndMoveLearning` `pbLearnMove`) is in the stack, and any other transition there ends the call `finished`, back to the battle layer. Outside a battle, the Pokédex page an evolution into a species not yet owned opens after the scene (`pokedex_entry`) is closed with C as a `pokedex` step, by the chain and by the verbs' own settling; `continue_menu` takes it as an open chain. In a battle the page is the battle layer's, as after a catch. Anything else ends the call: `unknown` as `handoff`, everything else as `finished`. _(build choice, 2026-10-07: in session `20261007-060153` a Rare Candy evolved Starly into Staravia, and `continue_menu`, answering the evolution, ended `stuck` ("3 steps in pokedex_entry made no progress"))_

The bag verbs add their own steps around these. Each screen is opened by one press and a wait for its context, pressed again up to three times in all, since the pause menu's first B after an eval-driven setup is lost (Runtime → Randomlocke Mod (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/runtime.md` → Randomlocke Mod), the Showdown export row). Every cursor is moved by reading it back: the pause row and the item row with Down and Up, the pocket with Right and Left until `pocket` reads the item's pocket, and the party panel with Right and Left, which step one slot. The item row is checked against the payload's `item` before C. The command ("Usar", "Dar") is answered through dialog's choice step. After each press on the panel the verb drives what the game shows until the panel or the item list waits again: chain contexts through the chain's steps, messages (`bag_message` and top-level) through dialog's message step, the TM and swap confirms answered Sí, and the quantity picker entered with Up and Down by one and Right and Left by ten, read back from `value`. Then B closes the panel, the item list and the pause menu in turn.

The party verbs open the party from the pause menu's "Pokémon" row and move its cursor with Right and Left, read back, then press C on the slot and answer the commands ("Objeto", then "Guardar"; "Cambiar"; "Repartir Exp.") through dialog's choice step, as the bag's commands are. A swap then moves the cursor again in the reopened list (`switching` true) and presses C; the panels' slide is a `transition`. `use_vial` presses JUMPUP in the pause menu, and `fly` presses JUMPDOWN there, waits for the region map, and moves its cursor one square per press with Right, Left, Down and Up (columns first), reading `x` and `y` back after each press once the cursor has `settled`; on the point it presses C, waits through the flight's fades and checks the player stands on the landing tile. `save` moves the pause cursor to "Guardar" and presses C. After that, each verb drives what the game shows until the party list or the pause menu waits again, or until the idle overworld, which is where a save leaves the game: messages through dialog's message step, and the one confirm the verb expects (the exp share's, the Pokévial's, the save question) answered from that verb's own replies. Any other confirm ends the call `stuck`.

The mart verbs move the buy list's cursor with Down and Up to the item's row in the payload's `stock`, read back and checked against `item`, and press C; the sell list is the bag, reached as the bag verbs reach an item (Right and Left skip empty pockets there). Then each drives what the game shows until the list waits again, as above, with the two mart confirms answered Sí and the quantity entered through the bag's picker step. The quantity question "¿Cuántos Poké Balls quieres?" is a brief message that closes itself before the picker opens, so it is recorded and waited through, never pressed: a C on the tick the picker opens would take its first value. Leaving presses B on the list and waits through the scroll back until the clerk's message or choice shows.

The relearner verb moves the list's cursor with Down and Up to the move's row in the payload's live `moves`, read back and checked against the move, and presses C. Then it settles as the mart verbs do, with the teach and leave confirms answered Sí and the move-learning prompts answered by the chain's steps, until the list waits again or has closed: a list left empty closes itself after "No hay más movimientos…". It leaves with B and the leave confirm, waiting through the fade until the relearner is no longer in the stack.

The PC verb moves the box cursor by reading `box` and `cursor` back after each press (Menus → PC Storage (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → PC Storage)): to another box by Up to the box name and Right or Left there, the shorter way round the 50 boxes; then down from the name, to the target row with Down and Up, and to the column with Right and Left the shorter way round the row; to the party button by Left into columns 0–2, then Down. The box sliding, the party panel sliding and a Pokémon picked up or put down are `transition`s, waited through. C on the slot opens the commands, answered through dialog's choice step as the bag's are. A deposit opens the party panel from the party button, moves its cursor with Right and Left and answers "¿Dejar en qué caja?" by moving to the box's row and pressing C; if the list comes back (a full box), B leaves it. After each operation the verb settles until the box or party panel waits again, closing messages with dialog's message step, and B hides the party panel before the next operation.

## Commands

`anil menu continue [ANSWER]...` runs `continue_menu`, `anil menu choose [SLOT]` runs `choose_pokemon` (no slot picks none), `anil menu use ITEM SLOT [--count N] [ANSWER]...` runs `use_item` and `anil menu give ITEM SLOT` runs `give_item`; the item ID is upper-cased. `anil menu take SLOT`, `anil menu swap A B`, `anil menu exp-share SLOT on|off`, `anil menu vial`, `anil menu lens` and `anil menu save` run the other six, and `anil menu fly TOWN [--build BUILD]` runs `fly`. `anil menu buy [ITEM[=QUANTITY]]...` and `anil menu sell [ITEM[=QUANTITY]]...` run `buy` and `sell`; the quantity defaults to 1, and a call with no item only leaves the list. `anil menu relearn [MOVE]... [--answer ANSWER]...` runs `relearn` with the move IDs upper-cased; with no move it only leaves the list. `anil menu pc [OP]...` runs `pc`, with each operation as `withdraw=BOX,SLOT`, `deposit=PARTY_SLOT,BOX` or `move=BOX,SLOT:BOX,SLOT`, in order; a call with no operation only closes the box. Answers are `nickname=NAME`, `forget=SLOT`, `skip`, `evolve=yes` and `evolve=no`, in order. Each attaches as the driver, prints each line as it happens, then the outcome, reason, decision, unused answers and changes (and the stack for `stuck`); it exits 0 on `finished`, else 1.

## Observability

Each call writes `menu` entries to `record.jsonl` through `note`, with a `stage` field: `start` (`entry` `continue`, `use_item` with `item`, `pokemon` and `count`, `give_item` with `item` and `pokemon`, `take_item` with `pokemon`, `swap_party` with `pokemon` and `other`, `set_exp_share` with `pokemon` and `on`, `buy` and `sell` with `items` as `[item, quantity]` pairs, `pc` with `ops` as the command line takes them, `relearn` with `moves`, `choose_pokemon` with `pokemon`, `fly` with `town`, or `use_vial`, `use_lens` and `save` alone, and the `answers` as typed on the command line), a `step` for each prompt it answers or decides on (`prompt`: `naming`, `learn_confirm`, `forget_move` or `evolution`; for the verbs also `pause`, `bag`, `item`, `bag_party`, `party`, `slot`, `confirm` and `quantity`, for `pc` `box_slot`, `storage_party` and `box`, for `relearn` `move`, and for `fly` `region_map` and `landing`; and the `screen`), one `line` per transcript line, and `result`. The fields are dialog's ([Runtime → Session Record](../reference/runtime.md#session-record)). Replay skips the kind; the presses are ordinary advances. Steps log to the `harness.menu` logger.

## Decisions

### Scope and Interfaces

- **Built before the game database:** nothing can act in the game without these screens, and every verb takes explicit arguments (an item ID, a party slot, a box slot), so none of them needs the database first. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **The full set of screens, scoped together and built in slices:** the prompt chains, the bag, party, Pokévial and save verbs, the Poké Mart and the PC share one driver. Scoping only the prompted screens, or adding field moves, were rejected. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Out of scope:** battles themselves (the battle subsystem is deferred; the battle layer has since been built to drive them, [Battle Layer Design](battle-layer-design.md)); field moves and items used from a menu (Cut, Rock Smash and Strength items, the Pokerider, Headbutt, Flash and Rock Climb), which [Navigation Design → Scope Edges](navigation-design.md#scope-edges) keeps for later (flying with the PokéRider was added on 2026-10-04, [Flying](#flying)); renaming an owned Pokémon; the summary and Pokédex views, which state reads cover; the trainer card, options, infinite repel, radar, bag sorting and search, registering and favouriting items, item storage, the move relearner (added on 2026-10-09, [The Move Relearner](#the-move-relearner)) and the Battle Point shop. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **No irreversible verbs:** releasing a Pokémon (one or a whole box) and tossing items are left out, while selling stays in. Neither is ever needed, since a dead Pokémon can stay in the PC, and a wrong slot would lose a living Pokémon forever. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **The verbs do not enforce the group's honour rules:** they do exactly what they are told. A second competitive item, vitamins and EV or IV items, a legendary in the party and the Day Care are the rules engine's guard (`anil.harness.rules`, Rules Engine Design (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/rules-engine-design.md`)), in one place. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **One verb per player action, never a generic menu walker,** returning a `MenuResult` shaped like `DialogResult`, with dialog's outcomes plus `failed` for a game refusal. A walker could release or toss something silently on a wrong row. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **The pause-menu verbs start from the idle overworld only,** as `interact` does, and close everything they open; starting from an already open bag or party screen was rejected. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **The mart and PC verbs are separate calls after dialog's `handoff`:** dialog hands off, the verb works on the open screen and leaves it, and the caller runs `continue_dialog`, so each call stays easy to follow in the record. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **One call per mart or PC visit, taking a list:** one operation per call would leave a screen open between calls and need a close verb. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **`save` overwrites the current slot and is refused when there is none:** a run is one save, which fits [Series 1 Rules → Fair Play](../../../series/1/reference/rules.md#fair-play). _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **One prompt driver for every chain, wherever it opens,** with typed, ordered answers and `decision` when none fits; `anil.harness.menu` owns the chain contexts in battle too, and the battle subsystem will hand them over once it exists (the battle layer has since 2026-10-03). Fixed policies, which would put judgment in the harness, and an out-of-battle-only driver were rejected. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Calls hold no state:** `continue_menu` finishes the chain and the caller re-issues whatever verb raised it. A resumable verb would need state the game does not hold. _(owner, settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Real input only,** through `Driver.advance`: no verb changes state by eval, since the players' rules forbid shortcuts ([Series 1 Rules → Fair Play](../../../series/1/reference/rules.md#fair-play)). Setting up a test state by eval stays allowed in development. _(forced by existing decisions, 2026-10-02)_
- **Nicknames are typed in the keyboard entry** (forced by the game): keyboard mode is the default (`$PokemonSystem.textinput` 0, `322_UI_TextEntry.rb:180`), needs no grid layout and types any name in one tick through the bridge's text input. _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-02)_

### Build Choices

- **Shared steps in `anil.harness.steps`, changes in `anil.harness.changes`:** dialog's `Loop` became a subclass of `Steps`, the step class both layers use, and the choice and number steps take the answer and a callback that consumes it, so the menu layer can answer Sí or No for a typed answer without consuming it. `same_text` stays navigation's. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **The first remaining answer of a prompt's kind** is consumed, rather than strictly the next answer in the list, so `[Evolve(True), Forget(1)]` works whichever prompt comes first. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **`Forget` and `Skip` are one kind,** since both answer the same question; the confirm before the forget-move screen is answered Sí for `Forget` without consuming it, so the screen consumes it. An out-of-range `Forget` therefore reaches the screen before ending `decision` there. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **The evolution decision is taken at "¡Anda!",** the scene's opening message, which waits for input (`\1`) while nothing else in the scene does: the animation runs without input and only B during it cancels, so a decision left anywhere later would not hold once the driver detaches and the game runs free. An evolution with `can_cancel` false is not a decision. _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-02: Menus → Prompt Chains (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Prompt Chains))_
- **The naming payload carries `species` and `forced`,** read from the entry screen's Pokémon icon and `ChallengeModes.on?(:FORCE_NICKNAME)`, so a refused name ends `failed` before it is typed rather than after the game's refusal loop. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **The learn-move questions are told apart by their text,** since both are Sí/No choices under the same frames: the out-of-battle and in-battle wordings are both matched (Menus → Prompt Chains (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Prompt Chains)). An unrecognised choice in the chain ends `stuck`. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **The cursor-grid name entry is refused, not classified:** nicknames are typed in the keyboard entry ([Scope and Interfaces](#scope-and-interfaces)), and the grid is `refused` by recognising an `unknown` stack holding a `322:UI_TextEntry` frame while `$PokemonSystem.textinput` is not 0, at the start of `continue_menu` or mid-chain, so no context line is added for a screen the layer never drives. Verified in session `20261002-233002`: dialog handed the grid entry off as `unknown`, and `anil menu continue nickname=Pika` ended `refused` ([Runtime → Context Table](../reference/runtime.md#context-table)). _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **`finished` is any context outside the chain but a `transition`:** the call cannot know what opened the chain, and the battle's, an event's or the overworld's context all mean the chain is done. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_ In a battle, a transition ends it too unless the battle's `pbLearnMove` is in the stack: waiting through the EXP bar after the last foe's stat boxes ran a wild battle to its end inside `continue_menu`, so the battle layer never saw its outcome ([Battle Layer Design → Build Choices](battle-layer-design.md#build-choices)). _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-03)_
- **The in-battle chain needed its own context lines (forced by evidence):** battle move learning runs through `Battle#pbLearnMove`, whose messages and confirm are the battle scene's, so lines under `152:Battle_ExpAndMoveLearning` `pbLearnMove` classify them as `special` (`learn_move`); the stat boxes, forget-move screen and evolution scene are the same as out of battle. The EXP bar's fill (`pbEXPBar`) became a `transition` line, met on the way ([Runtime → Context Table](../reference/runtime.md#context-table)). _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-02)_
- **Record kind `menu` with a `step` stage** beside dialog's `start`, `line` and `result`. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **`use_item` takes the chain answers and a required Pokémon:** a Rare Candy or a TM raises a chain inside the call, so the verb takes `answers` as `continue_menu` does, rather than always ending `decision` there. `pokemon` is required, since only items used on a Pokémon are built; a `pokemon=None` form would only serve field items, which are out of scope ([Scope and Interfaces](#scope-and-interfaces)). _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **`count` goes to the picker when the game opens one, else to repeated uses:** a Rare Candy opens a picker capped at the levels left to the cap (Menus → Bag (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Bag)), and a Potion opens none, so three Potions are three presses on the panel, each checked to have done something. A `count` above the picker's maximum ends `failed` with B on the picker (which uses nothing) rather than using the maximum, so the call never does less than it was asked and reports success. Without a picker (a maximum of 1 or less), the presses that worked before the failing one stay done rather than the call failing before any use: repeated presses are partial for every item anyway (a second Potion on a healed Pokémon), and knowing the maximum before the first press would mean asking the game for it for this one case. _(build choice, 2026-10-02; partial no-picker uses, 2026-10-03)_
- **A use failed when nothing changed and no answer was taken:** the game's refusals (no effect, cannot learn, already knows, the cap) all come back to the panel with the Pokémon and the bag unchanged, while a use whose move the caller chose to skip took one of the caller's answers. Comparing the state rather than matching refusal texts covers refusals not yet seen. `give_item` fails when nothing about the Pokémon changed, held item included. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **A give always swaps:** the verb answers the swap confirm itself, since each verb answers its own confirm (below); Sí is the only answer that does what the call names, and the old item goes back to the bag. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **`continue_menu` closes the bag and pause menu after a chain** when a pause menu payload is still open beneath it, so a verb that ended `decision` still closes everything it opened once its chain is done, as the pause-menu verbs must ([Scope and Interfaces](#scope-and-interfaces)); a chain opened by an event or a battle is left as before. _(build choice, 2026-10-02)_
- **Party verbs judged by the state, not the game's texts,** as the bag verbs are: a take by the member's compared fields (its held item among them), a swap by the two slots' fields trading places, an exp share by the member's flag, a heal by the Pokévial's charges falling. Only `save` has nothing in the state to compare, so it is judged by where the game ends: the pause menu closes itself after a save and stays open after a failure (`[Multi Save] Multi Save.rb:556-602`). _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The typed state gained what the new verbs change:** `Trainer.save_slot` (the refusal check), `Pokemon.exp_share` and `GameState.vial` (charges, maximum, locked), so `anil state` shows them and `changes` compares the flag and the charges (State (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/state.md`)). Without them the exp share and the heal's charge would have been invisible to both. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **Each verb answers only its own confirm,** from a per-verb list of question patterns and replies (the bag's TM and swap confirms, the exp share's, the Pokévial's, the save question with "Guardar en <slot>"), so an unexpected question ends `stuck` rather than being answered Sí. `settle` also stops on the idle overworld, since a save closes the pause menu itself. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **An egg hatching needs no verb of its own:** the hatch is a step handler, so a walk ends `interrupted` at its "¿Y esto?"; `continue_dialog` drives that message, the hatch scene and "¡X salió del Huevo!", and hands off at the naming screen, which `continue_menu` answers with a `Nickname`, ending on the idle overworld ([Runtime → Context Table](../reference/runtime.md#context-table)). The scene's animation is a `transition` context rather than a chain kind, since unlike an evolution nothing in it can be pressed or cancelled; under the forced-nickname rule there is no confirm first. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **A known question whose reply is not among the shown options ends `stuck` too,** naming the prompt and its options, rather than `decision`: the same holds for the learn-move confirms and the PC's "stop" question, since `decision` is only for the chain kinds. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **`set_exp_share` that already matches finishes without input,** as [Results](#results) states, with no `failed` or `refused`: the caller asked for a state the game is already in. Its refusal when the exp share is not enabled reads `expshare_enabled?` before anything opens; on this build it never refuses, since the method always answers true. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **An empty Pokévial is driven, not refused:** the bag holding `EMPTYVIAL` is let through so the game's own message ("El Pokévial está vacío…") comes back as the `failed` reason; only a bag with no Pokévial at all is refused. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The mart verbs judge by the bag, not the game's texts,** as the bag verbs do: a buy by the item's count rising by the quantity, a sale by it falling, so every refusal (the challenge's three bans, money, a full bag, an item the mart will not take) ends `failed` with the game's own message as the reason, including refusals not yet seen. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The mart verbs stop at the first failed item** and leave the list, rather than going on to the next: the caller asked for the whole list, and stopping keeps what was done visible in `changes` without the call doing part of a list it could not finish. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **A quantity above the picker's maximum fails rather than buying the maximum,** as `use_item`'s `count` does, so a call never does less than it was asked and reports success. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The mart verbs end at the clerk's question** rather than answering it: the mart and PC verbs are separate calls after dialog's `handoff` ([Scope and Interfaces](#scope-and-interfaces)), so the caller runs `continue_dialog`, and the question could as well lead to the other list. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **A brief screen message is waited through, not pressed:** the mart's quantity question is shown with `brief` and returns on its own, straight into the picker (Menus → Poké Mart (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/menus.md` → Poké Mart)), where a C pressed by the message step could land on the picker's first tick. The step sits in the shared settle loop, but only the mart shows a brief message on this build: the bag's `pbDisplay` takes the argument and ignores it, and nothing passes it. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The sell list rests on the bag's payload:** its context is `mart_sell` while its payload is the bag's `menu` with `screen` `bag`, so the resting check maps the one to the other. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The party swap's slide is a `transition`** (forced by evidence): `pbSwitchBegin` and `pbSwitchEnd` animate the panels with their own loops, which read `unknown` and ended the first `swap_party` as `handoff` in session `20261003-000510` ([Runtime → Context Table](../reference/runtime.md#context-table)). _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-03)_
- **The PC verb is its own module,** `anil.harness.storage`, a `Verb` subclass whose resting screens are the storage box and party panel and whose confirm step also answers the box list, so `anil.harness.menu` stays the shared driver. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The operations are checked before any input** by playing them over the boxes and party as the call starts, so a caller's mistake (an empty slot, a taken move target, an out-of-range box) is `refused` and nothing is half done; a full party or box is left to the game and ends `failed` with its message. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **A move needs an empty target:** "Cambiar" onto a taken slot leaves the other Pokémon held, which would need a third placement the caller did not name, so a move onto a taken slot is refused rather than becoming a swap. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The PC verb judges by the boxes and party, not the game's texts,** stops at the first failed operation, closes the box and ends at the PC's question, as the mart verbs do with their list. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **An empty `buy` or `sell` only leaves the list, and an empty `pc` only closes the box.** Why: a list or box a caller opened and then may not use (a guard refusal leaves it open, since the guard runs before any input) had no other way out; verified in session `20261004-131047`, where an empty `buy` left Ciudad Añil's buy list for the clerk's question. _(build choice, 2026-10-04)_
- **Box changes are `StoredChange`s in `StateChanges.boxes`,** keyed by box and slot with the member fields, and compared only when `conclude` is asked to (`pc`), as [Results](#results) states; dialog and the other verbs leave them empty. _(build choice, 2026-10-03)_
- **The storage screen's animations are `transition`s** (forced by evidence): the box slide, the party panel's slide in and out, `pbHold` and `pbPlace` read `unknown` in session `20261003-003515` before their lines were added ([Runtime → Context Table](../reference/runtime.md#context-table)). _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-03)_
- **An event's party screen is picked on by its own verb, `choose_pokemon`:** session `20261008-184101` bought the Magikarp seller's Pokémon in the Centro Pokémon by Monte Moon (map 70) with a full party. The store question was answered "Añadir al equipo", the event opened the party screen to choose who goes to a box, and no verb worked there. The verb works any party screen an event opens, which the game's trades and tutors open too, and is told from the pause menu's by the stack (no `pbPokemonScreen` frame). It judges by the screen closing rather than by a text: the event's own lines that follow belong to dialog, and an ineligible pick (`pbChooseAblePokemon`'s message) leaves the screen open and ends `failed`. It compares the boxes as `pc` does. _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-08)_
- **The transcript strips `\me[]`** as it does `\se[]`: the save message carries `\me[GUI save game]` ([Dialog Design → Transcript](dialog-design.md#transcript)). _(forced by evidence, 2026-10-03)_

### Flying

- **Flying is a pause-menu verb, `fly`, reached by the "[S] Volar" hotkey:** the owner put flying with the PokéRider to the gym's city in as the route a pending freeze takes, as harness work beside the rules engine (Rules Engine Design → The Freeze Route (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/rules-engine-design.md` → The Freeze Route)). The hotkey is the one way in the game offers besides using the item from the bag, and it opens the same region map. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_

- **The game's refusals are checked before any input, with the game's own lines:** the game opens the region map first and says "No se puede usar aquí." or "No se puede usar cuando hay alguien contigo." only after a point is chosen (`pokerider.rb:14-29`), so driving it would cost a menu round trip for a known answer; both lines were also shown driven through the game (World → Flying (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/world.md` → Flying)). An unvisited landing is refused too, since C on it does nothing. _(build choice, 2026-10-04)_

- **The region map got its own payload and context line** (forced by evidence): its loop is the v21.1 hotfix's `pbMapScene` (`P/v21.1 Hotfixes/Misc bug fixes.rb:131`), which was `unknown`; the bridge wraps `PokemonRegionMap_Scene#pbMapScene` as `menu` `region_map` with `fly`, `region` and the live cursor `x`, `y` and `settled`, and `region_map` is a resting screen B closes. _(build choice, 2026-10-04)_

### The Move Relearner

- **The move relearner is a verb, `relearn`, on the list the assistant opens:** the owner wants movesets improved with the relearner (2026-10-09). This widens the out-of-scope screens ([Scope and Interfaces](#scope-and-interfaces)) by this one. _(owner, 2026-10-09)_
- **It follows the mart and PC verbs:** a separate call after dialog's `handoff`, one call per visit taking a list of moves, an empty list only leaving, and judged by the state rather than the game's texts: a move is taught when the member, found by its personal ID, knows it. Why: the list is one more screen an event opens, and those verbs already settled how such a screen is worked. _(build choice, 2026-10-09)_
- **The verb leaves the list itself,** where the mart verbs end at the clerk's question: the list's B asks a confirm and closes, and the event after it has no question to answer. _(build choice, 2026-10-09)_
- **The forget-move screen's payload names the member** (`species`, `personal_id`), carried into `MenuDecision` with the move's ID, so a caller can weigh the moves of the member it names. _(build choice, 2026-10-09)_
- **Verified in session `20261009-145404`** (booted into `post-gym2-search`, save `relearn-probe2`), at the Ciudad Celeste assistant (map 43 event 9) after `anil interact 43 9 --answer "Recordar movimiento"`, `anil menu choose 0` and `anil dialog`, with Mothim knowing four moves: `anil menu relearn TACKLE` was refused naming the list; `anil menu relearn ROUND` answered "¿Enseñar Canon?"
