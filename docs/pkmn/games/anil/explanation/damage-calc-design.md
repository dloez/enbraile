# Damage Calc Design

The harness's damage and threat calculation: damage ranges, hits to KO, accuracy, the critical hit's chance, the speed order and each foe's threats, computed deterministically from what the [knowledge view](#the-knowledge-view) shows and the game database. `anil battle` prints it with its results ([Battle Annotations](#battle-annotations)). It was scoped with the owner on 2026-10-04 and built that day in parts, the core formula and the stat ranges first, then abilities, items and the field and side effects, the move effects by mechanism, the foe's assumed moves, accuracy, the critical hit's chance, the speed order and the targets, the end of the round and hits to KO, and last the annotation of Wonder's battle tools. Each calc part was checked against the game with `wonder check calc`, and the annotation through a scripted model against the game. This doc owns the calc's design as built. The code is `anil.harness.calc`, reading `anil.harness.knowledge`; the game facts it ports are owned by Battle Mechanics (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md`). It is copied from Wonder's damage calc design (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/damage-calc-design.md`), where it was built and checked against the game, without Wonder's agent, tool API and control panel, which carried the calc to a model; here it reaches the owner through `anil battle`. Sessions named as evidence ran under Wonder's harness, whose commands, files and bridge module read `wonder` where `anil`'s read `anil` ([Runtime Reference](../reference/runtime.md)). The battle verbs it annotates are [Battle Layer Design](battle-layer-design.md)'s.

The calc only computes: it decides nothing about what to do with the numbers. It reads no secrets and no config, stores nothing, and calls no model.

## What the Calc Does

`anil.harness.calc` is a set of pure functions over the knowledge view's `KnownBattle` and the game database:

- **`own`, `foe` and `conditions`** build the inputs. `own` makes our battler a `Combatant` with its exact stats, ability, item, battler effects, PP, happiness and party. `foe` makes a foe one from what the view shows, its stats a range over the IV, EV and nature rules and its HP the range its databox per cent allows, or `None` when its species or level is hidden. `conditions` takes the weather, terrain, shown side and field effects and side sizes from the view.
- **`damage`** gives one move's damage into one target, at each of the 16 rolls with and without a critical hit, hit by hit, as HP and per cent ranges, with recoil, crash, a substitute's HP, an immunity or failure, the named risks of the foe's unrevealed ability and item, and what it does not model.
- **`threats`** gives a foe's threats against one of ours: its revealed damaging moves, tagged `revealed`, and the worst plausible moves for its unseen slots, tagged `assumed`.
- **`accuracy`** and **`critical`** give the chance a move gets past its accuracy check and the critical hit's chance per hit, each as a range.
- **`speed_order`** gives who moves first, `user`, `target` or `depends`, with the reasons, the priorities, the effective-speed ranges and Trick Room.
- **`move_targets`** gives a move's targets in a double battle.
- **`end_of_turn`** and **`hits_to_ko`** give a battler's HP change at the end of each round and the fewest and most hits to knock a target out, plain and with every hit critical.
- **`annotate`** puts these together for a battle ([Battle Annotations](#battle-annotations)).

What is hidden is taken over every value it can have, so a range never understates the risk, and anything not modelled is named on the result, never silently wrong. The decisions below hold what each part covers.

## Battle Annotations

`calc.annotate(connection, battle, badges, rolled)` gives a `BattleCalc` for the battle the view shows:

- **`ours`**, one entry per standing battler of ours, with a `MoveCalc` for each of its moves against each target `move_targets` gives it: the target battler, the set the move hits and whether it is a spread move, the damage range (`DamageRange`: HP and per cent with and without critical hits, the hits, type and effectiveness, recoil, crash, a substitute, an immunity or failure, the named risks and what is not modelled), the hits to KO, the accuracy, the critical hit's chance and the speed order against that target. A spread move gives one `MoveCalc` per battler it hits, computed with the set's size as the target count. A status move carries only its accuracy and the order. A move that hits only its user or a side has no entry.
- **`foes`**, one entry per standing foe, with a `ThreatCalc` for each threat against each standing battler of ours: the move, `revealed` or `assumed`, the damage range, the hits to KO and the accuracy, and the speed order when the threat's priority can differ from 0. A foe whose species or level is hidden has no threats and names it.

In every order the `user` is our battler, so `user` first means we move first. Our move's order takes the foe with no move, at priority 0; a threat's takes our battler with no move.

`anil battle` prints it ([Battle Layer Design → Commands](battle-layer-design.md#commands)): a battle verb whose result is a `command` decision prints, after the result, the calc for the battle the call ended in, built from that battle through the knowledge view, the reveal memory, the badge count and the view's rolled data; any other result prints none. `anil battle calc` prints the same for the battle running now, without acting. Under `calc:`, each of our battlers lists one line per `MoveCalc`, the move, its target and then the damage (type, category and effectiveness, HP and per cent with and without critical hits, the hits, recoil, crash, a substitute and each named risk), the hits to KO, the accuracy, the critical hit's chance and the speed order with its reasons; each foe lists one line per `ThreatCalc`, tagged `revealed` or `assumed`; anything not modelled closes its line in brackets. A calc that raises prints `calc: the damage calc failed: …` after the verb's result, which still stands.

## The Knowledge View

`anil.harness.knowledge` is Wonder's knowledge view (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/knowledge-view-design.md`), copied whole: a `KnowledgeView(policy, connection)` that tags every fact and filters the game's true state down to what a player sees. The calc reads it under its story policy, `STORY` (the static data, our own side, the opponents' species and what the screen and battle text have revealed): `KnowledgeView.battle(battle, reveals)` gives the `KnownBattle` the calc takes, a foe's moves, ability and item `hidden` until revealed, and `KnowledgeView.random(session)` the rolled data, an unowned species' tables `hidden`. The reveals are what the bridge's reveal hooks record in the save (`$PokemonGlobal@anil_reveals`, [Runtime → Events](../reference/runtime.md#events)), read by `anil.harness.state.read_reveals`. Its views of the other verbs' results and its `know` commands served Wonder's agent and the owner's checks of it, and have no command here.

## Interfaces

`anil battle`'s verbs and `anil battle calc` print the calc's results with their own. A trainer's rolled team is unknown until its first battle loads it, so a threat before a fight exists only for remembered reveals.

## Checks

`wonder check calc` holds the Python formula against the game's `pbCalcDamage` at every roll, with and without a critical hit, for every case it builds, and the accuracy, critical-hit chance, turn order, targets and end of round against the game's draws and classes; `wonder check calc-moves` holds the assumed moves against the randomizer's draws (Wonder's `harness.check`). Each prints the Python and the game's values per case. Neither is in `anil.harness.check` yet.

## Observability

The calc writes nothing. `anil battle` prints it, and the session's record holds the battle the calc was computed from.

## Re-running and Rollback

Pure functions; rollback is a revert.

## Decisions

### Scope and Interfaces

- **Done meant:** For every battle decision the battle tools return, each of our moves against each target and each foe's threat against each of ours carries a damage range in per cent and HP, hits to KO with and without crits, the speed order with its reason, and the tags saying what is revealed, what is assumed and what is not modelled; and `wonder check calc` shows the Python formula matching the game's `pbCalcDamage` at every roll for the cases it covers. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Out of scope:** the foe AI's move choice (the Advanced AI System plugin); PvP and Showdown's Level 50 calc (deferred); any decision about what to do with the numbers. The calc only computes. _(owner, 2026-10-04; Jev's option labels, once out of scope here, went with Jev when it was dropped, 2026-10-07)_
- **A Python port, not the game's own methods:** A Python port, checked in development against the game's `pbCalcDamage` with a fixed roll and the crit override, as `check.py` already does for the battle mechanics (Battle Mechanics (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md`)). Why: forced. The game's own methods in a real run would use the foe's true values (a leak), draw `rand` (breaking replay and changing outcomes) and need eval, which a real run refuses. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Inputs: the knowledge view's battle, our side exact, and the game database:** Only the knowledge view's battle output, our own side exact, and the game database. Why: forced by Knowledge Policy → Decisions (Wonder: `docs/general/explanation/knowledge-policy.md` → Decisions): every model-facing number goes through the view, or it leaks hidden foe facts. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **A foe's hidden stats are a min–max range:** A min–max range over every value the game's rules allow, with the worst case reported as the risk. The IV and EV rules for trainer, wild and Mega Stone holders' Pokémon are world facts owned by IVs and EVs → Where IVs Come From (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/ivs-evs.md` → Where IVs Come From); beyond them, any nature, and the HP bounded by the per cent the databox prints. Why: the owner's answer; it never understates risk, which survival first needs (Competition Format → Decisions (Wonder: `docs/domains/competition/explanation/competition-format.md` → Decisions)). _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Unseen moves are the worst plausible:** The worst plausible: revealed moves exactly, and each unseen slot as the strongest hit the game allows that foe, of its own types and of any coverage type, within the randomizer's limits by badge window (Randomizer → Moves (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Moves)), narrowed to its rolled learnset when the species is owned. Each threat is tagged revealed or assumed. Why: the owner's answer. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **An unrevealed ability is neutral plus the named risks:** Neutral, plus the few abilities that would change the matchup most, named, both ways ("if Levitate: Earthquake 0%", "if Huge Power: up to 78%"). A revealed ability is computed exactly; an owned species' rolled set narrows the named ones. Why: the owner's answer; the worst case over every randomized ability (Randomizer → Abilities (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Abilities)) would make every foe look like Huge Power. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **An unrevealed held item is neutral plus the named risks:** As abilities: neutral, plus the named items that change the matchup most ("if Focus Sash: survives at 1 HP", "if Choice Scarf: outspeeds", "if Air Balloon: Ground 0%"). _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Coverage: the whole formula and every multiplier stage:** The whole formula and every multiplier stage the game applies (the Deluxe Battle Kit's split: global, abilities, items, field, badges, weather, random roll, type, status). Abilities, items and move function codes come in badge-window order, the windows reached first first. Anything not modelled is named in the output, never silently wrong, and each piece modelled is checked against the game. Why: the owner's answer. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Field and side effects from battle text:** Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil, Tailwind, Trick Room, hazards and the like are announced in battle text, which already passes as revealed, so the knowledge view gains them, with turns counted from their start messages, and the calc uses them. This narrows the Knowledge Policy's "effects stay hidden" to what the text does not announce (Knowledge Policy → Decisions (Wonder: `docs/general/explanation/knowledge-policy.md` → Decisions), 2026-10-04); it is not a new policy, since what the screen shows is revealed. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Speed as an order with its reason:** An order computed from the ranges, with priority brackets, Trick Room, paralysis, stages and Tailwind: "we move first", "they move first" or "depends", with the reason (a speed tie, random in Gen 9, or an unseen Scarf). _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Crits, accuracy, hits to KO:** KO chances with and without crits; accuracy separately, never folded into one number; hits to KO counting end-of-turn damage (burn, poison, weather) and revealed recovery (Leftovers). _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Doubles:** A calc per target, with the 0.75 spread modifier. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **Interfaces:** in Wonder, the battle tools and the battle read returned the calc to the agent; here `anil battle` prints it, as [Interfaces](#interfaces) describes. _(owner, 2026-10-04; Jev's labels dropped with Jev, 2026-10-07; the agent left behind in Wonder, 2026-10-21)_

### Build Choices

- **The stat ranges:** A wild foe: IVs 0–31, EVs 0. A trainer's: IVs 0–31 and EVs 0–85, since the view hides the PBS entry's IVs and level, the level cap only lowers the level (so the PBS level's EVs can reach the 85 cap whatever the shown level), and a Mega Stone holder rebuilt by the randomizer has random IVs and no EVs. Any nature, so 0.9–1.1 on each stat but HP. Each stat's range is its lowest and highest value over these; a damage range takes the attacker's lowest stat with the defender's highest and the reverse, per roll. The current HP is the range the databox per cent allows over the total-HP range. Two wild cases fall outside these rules: the scripted armoured Mewtwo (IVs 31, EVs 100), which cannot be caught, and the boosted HP of the Deluxe Battle Kit's wild bosses (`hp_level`, Battle Mechanics → Damage Formula (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Damage Formula)); the view cannot tell them from an ordinary wild foe, so every wild foe's result names them. Built 2026-10-04 (`anil.harness.calc`); the ranges held the game's damage at both ends in `wonder check calc` (Battle Mechanics → Damage Formula (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Damage Formula)). Why: forced by IVs and EVs → Where IVs Come From (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/ivs-evs.md` → Where IVs Come From) and what the view shows.
- **The assumed moves:** `calc.threats` lists a foe's threat against one of ours: each revealed damaging move exactly, tagged `revealed`, then the assumed moves for its unseen slots, tagged `assumed`, each with its full damage result. A status move is no hit and is left out. The pool the randomizer draws from is every allowed, damaging move outside `MOVEBLACKLIST` and the OHKO moves, with a maximum power (the plugin's `display_max_power(true)`, ported as `calc.max_power`) of at most 70 while the window is under 3 badges; `LATE_GAME_BANNED_MOVES` never narrows it, since a team or learnset drawn at 3 or 4 badges can hold them (Randomizer → Moves (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Moves)). The window is our badge count for a trainer's Pokémon, and for a wild one the higher of it and its level's window. Without a rolled learnset, the assumed moves are the strongest pool move of each type, the types ordered by that move's top damage into our Pokémon, as many as there are unseen slots: so the foe's own types and any coverage type compete on damage, STAB counted. A wild foe of an owned species takes its moves as the game gives a new Pokémon: the last four distinct moves of its rolled learnset at or below its level, plus the strongest pool move at our badges when none of those four has power above 10; the assumed moves are the strongest of those not revealed. Moves are ranked by their top damage with the foe's own move counters at zero, since a move it has not shown has not been used; the damage reported keeps the foe's battler effects hidden. The counter moves and Bide (`COUNTER_FUNCTIONS`) are never assumed from the pool: their damage is what the foe takes, so with the round unknown they would top every ranking at the foe's whole HP. Built 2026-10-04; `wonder check calc-moves` held every rule against the game's draws (Randomizer → Moves (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Moves)). Why: the owner's answer above. Three build choices go past its wording, decided 2026-10-04: `LATE_GAME_BANNED_MOVES` never narrows the pool and a wild foe's window is the higher of ours and its level's, both forced by the randomizer drawing a learnset by each move's level and the extra move by our badges (Randomizer → Moves (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Moves)); the counter moves and Bide are never assumed, since their damage is the foe's, not a hit of its own.
- **An owned species narrows only a wild foe's assumed moves:** No: only a wild foe's. A trainer's Pokémon gets four moves drawn from the whole pool at our badge count (`RERANDOM_TRAINER_MOVESET`), not from its learnset, and 176 of 183 damaging moves of freshly loaded trainer Pokémon were outside their species' rolled learnset (Randomizer → Moves (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Moves)). Narrowing them to it would understate the threat. Decided 2026-10-04, forced by evidence; it narrows the owner's "narrowed to its rolled learnset when the species is owned" to the foes whose moves come from it.
- **The core:** The base formula, stat stages with the critical hit's ignoring of them, the spread modifier, weather, the critical hit, the 16 rolls, STAB, type, burn, screens and terrain, in the game's order and with Ruby's rounding. Screens and the target count are inputs to `Conditions`; the view shows screens since 2026-10-04 (Knowledge View Design → Shown Effects (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/knowledge-view-design.md` → Shown Effects)), and the calc takes them from it with the other field and side effects (next question). The badge stage is a no-op, verified. The core named on every result each known ability and held item, an unrevealed ability or item of the foe, a move whose function code is not `None` (over-naming secondary-effect moves until move effects are modelled), the foe's types when the databox shows none, a scripted wild battle's set IVs and EVs or boss HP on a wild foe, and battler and field effects; the next question narrows that list. Built 2026-10-04.
- **Abilities, items and effects:** Every ability and held item whose damage handler or immunity reads only what the calc holds (the move's type, category, flags and effect chance, weather, terrain, a threshold of HP, status, type effectiveness, the critical hit, the species), in the game's stages, as listed in Battle Mechanics → Abilities, Items and Field Effects (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Abilities, Items and Field Effects), with Mold Breaker (off against an Ability Shield), Unaware, Neutralizing Gas, Cloud Nine and Air Lock, Klutz, the Ruin abilities and the auras over the two battlers of the hit. A hit with no effect has damage 0; one stopped by an ability or item names it as its immunity. The shown effects the calc uses: Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil on the defender's side, with that side's count of unfainted battlers in the view, and Mud Sport, Water Sport, Gravity and Magic Room on the field; Wonder Room is named. A foe's HP is a range, so a threshold it straddles (a third, a half, full) is computed both ways and the damage range covers both; the databox's 100% is full HP for certain only when the total is at most 100. Still named: the damage-relevant abilities not modelled (Supreme Overlord, Analytic, the Flash Fire boost, Protosynthesis, Quark Drive, Slow Start, Añil's Acometida, Stakeout, Minus, Plus, Battery, Power Spot, Friend Guard, Rivalry, Protean, Libero, Disguise, Ice Face, Tera Shell, Multitype, RKS System, Forecast), the Metronome item and the Ogerpon masks, the foe's unrevealed ability (unless its rolled set holds none of those) and item beyond the named risks, the foe's battler effects (Helping Hand, Charge, Gastro Acid, Embargo, Magnet Rise and the like, which the view hides; ours are modelled since the battler effects were built, below), a shown field effect that changes damage and is not used (Wonder Room), other battlers' abilities in doubles, and the function codes not modelled (see the move effects question below). Any other ability or item is taken as not changing a hit's damage; those that change the critical hit's chance (Super Luck, Scope Lens) belong to the KO chances. Battle Armor and Shell Armor take the critical hit away, which the critical rolls show since the hits and recoil were built (below). Built 2026-10-04 (`anil.harness.calc`), every case matching the game in `wonder check calc`.
- **No badge window orders abilities and items:** None: all of them were taken in one pass. Forced by evidence: the randomizer's ability table gives each species abilities from the same 303 with no badge limit (Randomizer → Abilities (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Abilities)), and no badge limit is recorded on the random held items of wild and trainer Pokémon, only excluded kinds of item (Randomizer → Trainers (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Trainers)), unlike moves, whose power and bans follow the badge window (Randomizer → Moves (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/randomizer.md` → Moves)); so no ability or item is known to reach the run before another, and the badge-window order applies to move function codes. Decided 2026-10-04.
- **The named risks of an unrevealed ability or item:** Each modelled ability, narrowed to the species' rolled abilities when the caller passes the view's rolled data and it shows the species' table entry (`KnownRandom.abilities`, Knowledge View Design (Wonder: `docs/subsystems/harness/explanation/knowledge-view-design.md`)), and each modelled item is put on the foe in turn, attacking or hit; the three of each that change the hit's damage most, by the change of its top end with an immunity first and ties by name, are named with their damage range, an immunity as `immune`. Sturdy (not through Mold Breaker, and only when the rolled set allows it) and the Focus Sash are added as `survives at 1 HP` when the foe could be at full HP and the hit could take all of it. The Choice Scarf's speed goes with the speed order. Built 2026-10-04.
- **Move effects:** A move's function code is damage-neutral when its class overrides none of the methods through which a class changes a hit's damage (Battle Mechanics → Move Effects (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Move Effects)); `anil.harness.calc` holds those 127 codes, and `wonder check calc` checks the set against the game's classes. A neutral code is never named, so Bite no longer is. Of the other 128, the calc models the fixed-damage codes (Sonic Boom, Dragon Rage, Seismic Toss, Night Shade, Psywave, Super Fang, Endeavor, Final Gambit and the OHKO moves) and the codes whose power reads only what the calc holds: HP, stat stages, weight with Heavy Metal, Light Metal and the Float Stone, happiness, status, a held item's presence, terrain, Gravity, weather, type effectiveness, or a random draw. It also models Earthquake, Surf, Gust and the like, whose only other input is the target's semi-invulnerable state, a battler effect, read from our side since the battler effects were built (below) and named for the foe. A failure the calc can tell (Endeavor at or above the target's HP, an OHKO move into a higher level, Sturdy or, for Sheer Cold, an Ice type) gives damage 0 with `fails` set, and a possible failure puts 0 at the bottom of the range. A random power gives the range over every outcome, with 0 at the bottom for Present, whose last outcome heals the target. A foe's happiness is 0–255, since the view does not show it; ours is read from the party. HP-dependent power is taken over the HP range on its own, apart from the HP thresholds of abilities, so the range can only widen. Every other code was still named; the remaining parts of the move-effects work model them, and none is named since the counters and speed were built (below). Built 2026-10-04 (`anil.harness.calc`), every case matching the game in `wonder check calc`.
- **Type changes:** The move's type is computed as the game's `pbCalcType` gives it, and every stage reads that type (Battle Mechanics → Type Changes (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Type Changes)). That covers the -ate abilities, Añil's Piel Tétrica and Piel Herbácea, Liquid Voice, Tintineo and Normalize with their 1.2× power. It covers the codes that set their own type: Hidden Power, Judgment, Techno Blast, Multi-Attack, Aura Wheel, Revelation Dance, Ivy Cudgel, Raging Bull (which also ignores screens), Weather Ball, Terrain Pulse and Natural Gift, the last three with their power. It also covers the codes that change effectiveness: Flying Press, Freeze-Dry, Atrapabicho, Smack Down and Thousand Arrows (which also hit through Levitate and an Air Balloon) and Sky Drop (failing into 200 kg). When the type hangs on something the view hides, the calc takes every type it can be. For a foe's Hidden Power, that is every Hidden Power type, since its IVs are hidden. For a foe's Judgment, Multi-Attack or Techno Blast with its item hidden, it is no item and each plate, memory or drive. For a foe's Natural Gift with its item hidden, it is no item (a failure, so 0 at the bottom) and one berry for each type and power pair. The damage range covers them all. The result's `types` lists every type the move can be, and `type` and `effectiveness` are those of the one doing the most damage. A foe's Natural Gift names only berries among its item risks, since every other item makes it fail, which the range already shows. The pledges' combined types are modelled since the battle history was built (below); Tera Starstorm and Nihil Light are modelled since the stats, defences and items were built (below). `PIELELECTRICA` left the not-modelled names: it is not an ability in the build, and Piel Eléctrica is Galvanize. Build choices and a fact forced by evidence, 2026-10-04 (`anil.harness.calc`), every case matching the game in `wonder check calc`.
- **Hits and recoil:** A result now carries the number of hits as a range (`hits`), every hit's damage at each roll (`hit_rolls`, `hit_crit_rolls`), and a total over the hits. The total's bottom is the fewest hits at their lowest rolls, its top the most hits at their highest. `rolls` and `crit_rolls` stay the first hit's. `damage`, `crit_damage` (every hit a critical hit) and their per cents are the totals. The hits follow Battle Mechanics → Hits and Recoil (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Hits and Recoil). That covers the two-, three-, two-to-five- and ten-hit codes, Skill Link and the Loaded Dice, Water Shuriken on Ash-Greninja, the per-hit power of Triple Kick, Triple Axel and Triple Hit, and Beat Up from the party's able, status-free Pokémon. For a foe, whose party the view hides, Beat Up takes 1–6 hits, each at any power the build's base Attack values give. Dragon Darts hits twice when the target's side has one battler, and 1–2 times otherwise. It also covers Surging Strikes' critical hits and Parental Bond with its quartered second hit. A move whose accuracy is checked per hit takes 1 to its full hits unless Skill Link or the Loaded Dice skips that check, or the move never misses (accuracy 0). Each hit after the first is computed into the target's HP less what the earlier hits took, not at full HP, and without a resist berry that weakened the first (the berry and the held item gone, as Knock Off sees it since the stats, defences and items were built). Battle Armor and Shell Armor (not through Mold Breaker) and a shown Lucky Chant on the target's side (read from code; no `wonder check calc` case sets it) take the critical hit off the critical rolls and off Surging Strikes. For Surging Strikes' rolls, an unrevealed foe ability that could be Battle Armor or Shell Armor is taken both ways; the critical rolls stay critical, the worst case. The Loaded Dice joins the modelled items, and Skill Link, Parental Bond and Reckless join the modelled abilities, so each can be a named risk. A result also carries `recoil`, the HP the user loses after all hits, from the non-critical total capped at the target's HP, and at most the user's HP. It is 0 with Rock Head, Añil's `ICEHEAD` or Magic Guard, and 0 at the bottom when an unrevealed foe ability could be one of them. It also carries `crash`, the HP lost when a crash move misses, at most the user's HP, which only Magic Guard stops. Reckless's 1.2× applies to both kinds. Still named when a move can hit more than once: a target ability or item that changes later hits (Stamina, Weak Armor, Water Compaction, Seed Sower, Sand Spit, Mummy, Lingering Aroma, Wandering Spirit, the Kee and Maranga Berries), or an unrevealed foe ability that could be one. Sturdy and the Focus Sash are no longer named as `survives at 1 HP` when every outcome hits more than once. The not-modelled ability the check names is now Friend Guard. Build choices, 2026-10-04 (`anil.harness.calc`), every case matching the game in `wonder check calc`.
- **Stats, defences and items:** The codes of the fourth part, following Battle Mechanics → Stats, Defences and Items (Wonder: `docs/domains/game/reference/battle-mechanics.md` → Stats, Defences and Items). Foul Play takes the target's Attack and its stage, Body Press the user's Defense and its stage, and Psyshock, Psystrike and Secret Sword the target's Defense and its stage, with sandstorm's Rock boost off and snow's Ice boost on for them.
