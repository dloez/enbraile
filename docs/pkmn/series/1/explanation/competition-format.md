# Competition Format

Series 1 is the group's Randomlocke of Pokémon Añil: 6 players each play the story under the same rules, meet in a league round after every gym, and those who finish the story meet in a final tournament. The rules themselves are in [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules); this doc says why the series is shaped that way and what the rules mean for a run.

## Story Run

Players each play the same packaged game (Pokémon Añil with the Randomlocke mod) with the same randomizer settings and rules, but every new game is randomized on its own, so every player faces a different game. Progress is held by a **per-gym level cap**; there is no gym cap, so a player may race ahead in the story.

Randomlocke is a Nuzlocke variant: a Pokémon that faints is dead and can't be used again, and there are no lives. That makes survival a first-class concern for every routine decision.

## The League and Pokepastes

Each gym opens a league round: a 1v1 best-of-three on Pokémon Showdown between two of the 6 players, worth 1 point to the winner. The team for a round is the **pokepaste the player froze right after beating that gym**, before catching anything new, so a player far ahead in the story still plays each round with the party they had at that gym. What matters for the run is therefore the party at the moment each gym falls: whatever a player wants in a round must be alive, in the party and within the rules by then. Dead Pokémon never enter a pokepaste.

## Eggs

Each league win opens one of the 16 eggs the mod puts in every new game; with 8 league rounds, at most 8 are ever opened. An egg hatches into a random Pokémon drawn at the hatch from the window of the badge count at that moment, so an egg opened later matches that later point of the run, and it never costs a route. Fights in the story never earn eggs.

## Final Tournament

Players who finished the story and never lost the challenge meet in a bracket seeded by league points. Each brings a pokepaste made right after beating the League, up to 6 living Pokémon, with no bans and team preview showing species. Legendaries and mythicals may be caught but never used, so a player's roster that counts is the one alive at the end of the story with neither in it.

## Decisions

- **No fights against the AI.** The rulebook, dictionary and this format were copied from the AI agent's competition docs with its fights, its own run and its role removed: there is no optional fight against the AI each round, so an egg opens on a league win only and no loss costs a route. **Why:** the owner. _(owner, 2026-10-21)_
- **Rule clarifications of 2026-10-04:** the one-of-each-shop-item rule covers held battle berries and every shop, Battle Point shops included; nature mints and Pokérus are allowed under the EV and IV rule; nothing is levelled between beating a gym and exporting its pokepaste; guides are allowed for everyone. Written into [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules). **Why:** the owner's answers while scoping the rules engine. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **6 players.** The league and the final tournament are among the 6 players, so the first 5 rounds' new-opponent rule covers every opponent. **Why:** the owner's answer. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Format as in [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules):** same package, each new game randomized on its own; per-gym level cap and no gym cap; one league round per gym, Bo3, 1 point a win; teams from the pokepaste frozen at each gym; eggs opened by league wins; a final tournament seeded by league points with a pokepaste made after the League and no bans; dead Pokémon unusable; legendaries and mythicals never used; no lives. **Why:** the group's rules text and the owner's additions, scoped with the owner 2026-10-02. _(settled 2026-10-02; replaces the 2026-09-29 weekly gym cap and the tournament's submit 8, ban 1, bring 6; eggs from league wins only 2026-10-21)_
- **The group's rules document** is the group's text with the owner's additions, written into Rules. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Eggs:** every new game gets 16 eggs, given by the mod in a PC box; each hatches after 100 steps in the party, into a species drawn at the hatch for the badge count at that moment, and never uses a route. A league win opens one. **Why:** the owner's choice; 100 steps feels fast but still needs a short walk, and an egg opened later in the run should match that point of the run. _(settled 2026-10-02; drawing at the hatch replaces drawing every species at new game; league wins only 2026-10-21)_
- **Points:** a league Bo3 win gives 1 point, a loss 0; points rank players and seed the tournament bracket. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Mythicals count as legendaries.** Legendaries and mythicals may be caught but never used: not in the party, not in any pokepaste; an egg that hatches a legendary or a mythical can't be used. **Why:** the owner's answer; the game's legendary clause already treats Mythical-flagged species as legendary, and eggs can hatch mythicals (Meloetta hatched in a check). _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **A shiny legendary or mythical stays in the PC** and does not oblige the shiny-in-party rule: the never-used rule wins over the shiny one. It still counts toward the 6 shinies, which come from any source. **Why:** the owner's answer. _(owner, 2026-10-03)_
- **No Item Clause:** the same item may be held by several Pokémon, as long as the one-of-each-shop-item rule holds. **Why:** the owner's player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Posting a pokepaste to pokepast.es is optional;** the pokepaste is the export, frozen at its gym. **Why:** the owner's player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **At most 6 shinies, from any source; a seventh is not caught.** The rule no longer says "at a time" or offers releasing one to make room. **Why:** the owner's player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02; replaces "at most 6 at a time, a seventh released or not caught")_
- **No exploiting:** no reloading a save or restarting the game to change an outcome, and no bugs or glitches that give an advantage. **Why:** the owner's addition to the player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **PvP medium:** Pokémon Showdown, "[Gen 9] Custom Game" with Species, Sleep, OHKO and Evasion Moves clauses and team preview, for league matches and the tournament. **Why:** the owner's choice; Custom Game accepts the export's Level 50 Gen 1–5 teams. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
