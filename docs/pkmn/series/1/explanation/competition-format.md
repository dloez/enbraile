# Competition Format

Series 1 is the group's Randomlocke of Pokémon Añil: 6 players each play the story under the same rules, meet in a league round after gyms 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7, and those who finish the story meet in an open-sheet final tournament. The rules themselves are in [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules); this doc says why the series is shaped that way and what the rules mean for a run.

## Story Run

Players each play the same packaged game (Pokémon Añil with the Randomlocke mod) with the same randomizer settings and rules, but every new game is randomized on its own, so every player faces a different game. Progress is held by a **per-gym level cap**; there is no gym cap, so a player may race ahead in the story.

Randomlocke is a Nuzlocke variant: a Pokémon that faints is dead and can't be used again, and there are no lives. That makes survival a first-class concern for every routine decision.

## The League and Pokepastes

Gyms 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 each open a league round: a 1v1 best-of-three on Pokémon Showdown between two of the 6 players, worth 1 point to the winner, so over the 5 rounds every player meets every other once. The two players may agree to play their match under other rules. The team for a round is the **pokepaste the player froze right after beating that gym**, before catching anything new, so a player far ahead in the story still plays each round with the party they had at that gym. What matters for the run is therefore the party at the moment each gym falls: whatever a player wants in a round must be alive, in the party and within the rules by then. Dead Pokémon never enter a pokepaste.

## Eggs

Each league win opens one of the 16 eggs the mod puts in every new game, and the Pokémon League's best try opens one more per combat won (the four Elite Four members and the rival), so at most 10 are ever opened. An egg hatches into a random Pokémon drawn at the hatch from the window of the badge count at that moment, so an egg opened later matches that later point of the run, and it never costs a route. Other fights in the story never earn eggs.

## Final Tournament

Players who finished the story and never lost the challenge, whether or not they beat the League, meet in a bracket seeded by league points, with a losers' triangular that sends one first-round loser back into the semifinals. The tournament is open sheet: each player exports their whole PC when they decide they are done playing, the postgame included, and builds each team from that sheet while knowing the opponent's. Pokémon lost in the League come back for it, and saving a Pokémon by quitting is allowed in the postgame, so the League and the postgame are a last chance to grow the sheet without shrinking it. Legendaries and mythicals may be caught but never used, so a player's roster that counts is the one alive when they stop, with neither in it.

## Decisions

- **Tournament teams may use any move of a Pokémon's learnset,** above its level too. Written into [Rules → Final Tournament](../reference/rules.md#final-tournament). **Why:** the owner: players are able to choose all of a Pokémon's moves, at least in this tournament. _(owner, 2026-10-28)_
- **Cup semifinals keep the rulebook's pairing:** the 2–5 winner meets the triangular's winner, and the 1–6 winner meets the 3–4 winner. The group's league app pairs 1–6 with the triangular and 2–5 with 3–4; the site follows the rulebook. **Why:** the owner, asked which holds. _(owner, 2026-10-22)_
- **League ties go to the winner of the match between the tied players:** with three or more tied, the most wins in their matches; a loop that leaves them level goes to a random draw. Fainted Pokémon and games won are not tie-breaks. Written into [Rules → The League](../reference/rules.md#the-league). **Why:** the owner: with five rounds every pair of players meets exactly once, so two tied players always have a direct result. Counting faints was meant for the eight-round league, where two players could meet twice and split 1–1, and no longer applies. _(owner, 2026-10-27; replaces head-to-head, then game difference, Pokémon difference and a random draw, taken from the league app on 2026-10-22, and fewer Pokémon fainted first)_
- **The Pokémon League counts its best try:** each combat won in a player's best try (the four Elite Four members, then the rival) gives 1 league point and opens 1 egg. Written into [Rules → The League](../reference/rules.md#the-league) and [Rules → The Pokémon League](../reference/rules.md#the-pokémon-league-1). **Why:** the owner: the group's league app counts it this way, and the rulebook follows it. _(owner, 2026-10-22; replaces Bo3 wins as the only points, and one egg per Elite Four member and two for the rival)_
- **Rule changes of 2026-10-22:** league rounds after gyms 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 only, each opponent once, and a pair may change their match's rules when both agree; the Pokémon League may be retried at will, its dead stay dead in it but come back for the tournament, and it opens one egg per Elite Four member and two for the rival; the postgame islands are closed, items may still be searched for with the Añil map, the legendary sidequest may be played until it turns to Sinnoh's legendaries, and quitting to save a Pokémon is allowed in the postgame; the final tournament is open sheet, from a whole-PC export made when the player is done, seeded 1–6, 2–5, 3–4 with first and second in opposite halves, and a losers' triangular whose winner meets the lower half's winner; the shiny-in-party rule is held strictly, and players should try to keep their shinies alive. Written into [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules). **Why:** the owner, from how series 1 is actually played. _(owner, 2026-10-22; replaces one round per gym, eggs from league wins only, and a tournament pokepaste made after the League)_
- **The losers' triangular:** each pair once, a Bo3 in the league's format; a 1–1–1 loop is broken by its own tie-breaks, not the league's: head-to-head, then game difference, then Pokémon difference, then the higher league seed. Written into [Rules → Final Tournament](../reference/rules.md#final-tournament). **Why:** the owner and the group, who kept Bo3 over Bo1; a round robin of three, as in football's triangulars, where a loop leaves head-to-head level and games and fainted Pokémon play goal difference's part. The owner kept it apart when the league's tie-breaks changed. _(owner, 2026-10-22; kept apart from the league 2026-10-27)_
- **Postgame items:** any item left on the mainland may be collected, with the Añil Item Atlas or without it, under the usual rules and with no extra limit; the islands stay closed for items too. Written into [Rules → The Postgame](../reference/rules.md#the-postgame). **Why:** the owner. _(owner, 2026-10-22)_
- **No fights against the AI.** The rulebook, dictionary and this format were copied from the AI agent's competition docs with its fights, its own run and its role removed: there is no optional fight against the AI each round, so an egg opens on a league win only and no loss costs a route. **Why:** the owner. _(owner, 2026-10-21)_
- **Rule clarifications of 2026-10-04:** the one-of-each-shop-item rule covers held battle berries and every shop, Battle Point shops included; nature mints and Pokérus are allowed under the EV and IV rule; nothing is levelled between beating a gym and exporting its pokepaste; guides are allowed for everyone. Written into [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules). **Why:** the owner's answers while scoping the rules engine. _(owner, 2026-10-04)_
- **6 players.** The league and the final tournament are among the 6 players, so the 5 league rounds cover every opponent once. **Why:** the owner's answer. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Format as in [Rules → The Group's Rules](../reference/rules.md#the-groups-rules):** same package, each new game randomized on its own; per-gym level cap and no gym cap; one league round per gym, Bo3, 1 point a win; teams from the pokepaste frozen at each gym; eggs opened by league wins; a final tournament seeded by league points with a pokepaste made after the League and no bans; dead Pokémon unusable; legendaries and mythicals never used; no lives. **Why:** the group's rules text and the owner's additions, scoped with the owner 2026-10-02. _(settled 2026-10-02; replaces the 2026-09-29 weekly gym cap and the tournament's submit 8, ban 1, bring 6; eggs from league wins only 2026-10-21)_
- **The group's rules document** is the group's text with the owner's additions, written into Rules. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Eggs:** every new game gets 16 eggs, given by the mod in a PC box; each hatches after 100 steps in the party, into a species drawn at the hatch for the badge count at that moment, and never uses a route. A league win opens one. **Why:** the owner's choice; 100 steps feels fast but still needs a short walk, and an egg opened later in the run should match that point of the run. _(settled 2026-10-02; drawing at the hatch replaces drawing every species at new game; league wins only 2026-10-21)_
- **Points:** a league Bo3 win gives 1 point, a loss 0; points rank players and seed the tournament bracket. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Mythicals count as legendaries.** Legendaries and mythicals may be caught but never used: not in the party, not in any pokepaste; an egg that hatches a legendary or a mythical can't be used. **Why:** the owner's answer; the game's legendary clause already treats Mythical-flagged species as legendary, and eggs can hatch mythicals (Meloetta hatched in a check). _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **A shiny legendary or mythical stays in the PC** and does not oblige the shiny-in-party rule: the never-used rule wins over the shiny one. It still counts toward the 6 shinies, which come from any source. **Why:** the owner's answer. _(owner, 2026-10-03)_
- **No Item Clause:** the same item may be held by several Pokémon, as long as the one-of-each-shop-item rule holds. **Why:** the owner's player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **Posting a pokepaste to pokepast.es is optional;** the pokepaste is the export, frozen at its gym. **Why:** the owner's player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **At most 6 shinies, from any source; a seventh is not caught.** The rule no longer says "at a time" or offers releasing one to make room. **Why:** the owner's player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02; replaces "at most 6 at a time, a seventh released or not caught")_
- **No exploiting:** no reloading a save or restarting the game to change an outcome, and no bugs or glitches that give an advantage. **Why:** the owner's addition to the player guide. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
- **PvP medium:** Pokémon Showdown, "[Gen 9] Custom Game" with Species, Sleep, OHKO and Evasion Moves clauses and team preview, for league matches and the tournament. **Why:** the owner's choice; Custom Game accepts the export's Level 50 Gen 1–5 teams. _(settled 2026-10-02)_
