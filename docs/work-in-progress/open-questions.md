# Open Questions

Everything not yet decided. Never resolve one silently in code; close it as described in [Recording Decisions](../general/how-to/write-documentation.md#recording-decisions).

## League tie edges

On equal league points, the winner of the match between the tied players ranks higher. Two players who met twice and split 1–1, or three players tied in a loop, have no rule yet.
